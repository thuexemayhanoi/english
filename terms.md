<h2>No legal advice</h2>
<p>Vietnam's traffic laws, licence requirements, fines and vehicle classifications change over time. Our legal guides are reviewed against primary sources — Vietnamese legislation, government and ministry publications, and official treaty information — and carry review dates, but they are explanatory writing, not legal advice.</p>
<p>Legal guides on this site do not replace the authorities, the official legislation itself, or professional legal advice where your situation needs it. Before relying on any legal detail — especially licence categories, IDP acceptance or fine amounts — verify the current rules with official sources or the relevant authorities.</p>
<p>Two examples of why this matters. Licence categories in Vietnam are defined by engine capacity and power output, and the boundaries changed with recent legislation; an article summarising them can be out of date the moment a new decree takes effect. Fine amounts are set by government decree and are periodically revised. Our guides carry review information so you can judge how current they are, but verification with official sources remains your responsibility for anything consequential.</p>
