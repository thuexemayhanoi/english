<h1>Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>Short answers to the questions riders ask most. Each answer links to a full guide where you can read more.</p>

<h2>Do I need a licence to ride a motorbike in Vietnam?</h2>
<p>For most bikes above 50cc, yes. The exact rules depend on engine size, your home country, and whether Vietnam recognizes your International Driving Permit. Read <a href="{{ '/articles/can-tourists-ride-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}">Can tourists ride a motorbike in Vietnam?</a> and <a href="{{ '/articles/vietnam-motorbike-licence-classes-explained/' | relative_url }}">Vietnam motorbike licence classes explained</a> for the details.</p>

<h2>Can I ride with my home licence or IDP?</h2>
<p>It depends on which convention your IDP was issued under and how Vietnam recognizes it. See <a href="{{ '/articles/1968-vienna-convention-idp-vietnam/' | relative_url }}">the 1968 Vienna Convention and riding in Vietnam</a> and <a href="{{ '/articles/idp-vietnam-motorbike-rules/' | relative_url }}">IDP rules for motorbikes in Vietnam</a>.</p>

<h2>Do I need a helmet?</h2>
<p>Yes, helmets are required by law for riders and passengers on motorbikes. See <a href="{{ '/articles/helmet-law-vietnam/' | relative_url }}">the Vietnam helmet law explained</a>.</p>

<h2>What happens if I break a traffic rule?</h2>
