The Weaver 200 is the electric that behaves like a motorcycle — because legally it is one. All figures below are Dat Bike's official published data.

## Official specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Max power | 6,000 W |
| Max speed | 90 km/h |
| Battery | lithium-ion 72 V 68 Ah |
| Range | 200 km at 35 km/h / 130 km at 70 km/h |
| Charging | 1 h for first 100 km, 3 h for 200 km |
| Wheels | 17-inch (2.75 front / 3.0 rear) |
| Weight | 120 kg |
| Max load | 165 kg |
| Waterproofing | IP65 |
| Brakes | hydraulic discs front and rear, throttle-controlled regen |
| Licence | A1 required |

## What stands out

The range honesty is refreshing: Dat Bike publishes both test speeds, so the 200 km claim (35 km/h) comes with its own 130 km reality check (70 km/h). Either way, a full charge covers a week of Hanoi commuting — or the brand's own example trips, Hanoi to Ha Long included.

Fast charging reframes ownership: one hour of charging buys 100 km. The regen-braking throttle lets you ride entire avenues on the right hand, and the 6,000 W motor keeps up with petrol flow everywhere licence-free bikes cannot go.

## Trade-offs

You need an A1 licence — this is a motorcycle in law, not a moped. The 120 kg kerb weight and 17-inch wheels make it an underbone-style ride, less step-through friendly than a scooter. Charging still takes hours without a Dat Charge station; there is no battery swapping.

## Who it suits

Licensed riders who want motorcycle pace with moped running costs, long-distance commuters, and anyone whose week includes trips the licence-free class cannot reach. The Weaver++ adds power and water protection — see the comparison.
