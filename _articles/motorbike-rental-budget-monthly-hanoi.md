Budgeting a month of motorbike rental in Hanoi means counting four real lines and one held-in-reserve line. Here is the framework with the honest numbers.

## Line 1: the rental rate

The fixed monthly cost. Where published (motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi): Honda Click and Yamaha Mio 1,000,000–1,200,000 VND/month; Honda Air Blade 1,400,000 VND/month; Honda Vision 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND/month. For models without a published monthly rate (Wave, Sirius, E-Bike, 50cc), the price is a direct conversation — never an extrapolated figure.

## Line 2: fuel

The variable cost, and at month scale the one that tracks your kilometres. Small automatics in mixed city riding use roughly 1.5–2.5 litres per 100 km — real figures vary by model, load and traffic, so treat any consumption number as a planning aid, not a specification. Petrol is inexpensive in Vietnam; a month of daily commuting costs notably less than a single day's rental rate.

## Line 3: parking

Small but daily where you use attended lots: typically a few thousand dong per session around the city. A commuter parking at work and at home lots daily multiplies that into a real monthly line — the routine is described in parking-monthly-renter-hanoi.

## Line 4: the maybe costs

- Consumables: tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation — budget a contingency and agree responsibility in writing (consumables-monthly-rental-hanoi)
- Rain gear in the wet months, a helmet upgrade if you want your own (do-hanoi-rentals-include-helmets)
- Security extras if you choose them: a disc lock is cheap peace of mind (keeping-rental-motorbike-secure-hanoi)

## The reserve line: deposit

Typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the bike — held, not spent, returned at term end per the agreement (how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi). It is not a cost, but it must be cash you can park for the whole term.

## What is not in the budget

Rider insurance is not included in any rental (is-insurance-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi); if you arrange your own cover (insurance-for-long-term-renter-hanoi), add it as its own line. And there are no hidden fees to budget for: no promotions, no surprise charges — just the agreed rate and the real lines above.

## The one-line version

Rate + fuel + parking + a consumables contingency, with the deposit parked as reserve — that is the whole honest monthly budget.
