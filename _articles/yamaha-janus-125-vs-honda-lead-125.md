The Janus and the Lead sit at opposite ends of the practical 125 spectrum: Yamaha's entry model is about light weight and low cost; Honda's Lead is about the biggest bay in the class. Verified figures from the official pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and economy

The Janus's Blue Core 124.9 cc engine makes 7.0 kW at 8,000 rpm and 9.6 Nm at 5,500 rpm, with published consumption of 1.98 litres/100 km from a 4.2-litre tank and Stop & Start. Honda's Lead page did not publish output figures in our retrieval; what is verified is the 125 cc eSP+ four-valve liquid-cooled engine with PGM-FI. On published economy the Janus is the documented sipper - see the fuel economy comparison.

## Weight and storage

Janus: 97 kg kerb, 15.3-litre underseat bay, USB port, hydraulic front disc. Lead: 37-litre underseat bay holding two full-face helmets, with an ABS version available. This is the whole decision in two numbers - the Janus wins every parking-speed contest, the Lead wins every cargo contest.

## Price

The Janus lists from 29,151,000 VND - the value anchor of the 125 class. The Lead costs more and gives you the bay; check Honda's current Lead pricing when buying.

## Verdict

Minimum weight and price, maximum familiarity: Janus. Family-and-cargo duties: Lead, no contest.
