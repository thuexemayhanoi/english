Most Hanoi motorbike rentals are concluded in a few minutes of conversation, and many smaller shops run on a handshake plus a deposit. But the terms still exist even when the paper does not, and agreeing them out loud before money changes hands is what protects both sides.

## The terms that matter

- Period and rate: the daily, weekly, or monthly price, and exactly when the rental day starts and ends
- Deposit: the amount, what it covers, and when it comes back
- Late return: hourly charge and when a full day kicks in
- Damage and consumables: which repairs are the shop's responsibility and which are yours
- Insurance: whether any is included — confirm rather than assume
- Fuel: the level the bike should come back with
- Permitted use: who may ride it, and whether trips outside Hanoi are acceptable
- Contact: the shop's phone number and what to do in a breakdown

For Nguyen Tu's published position on each of these — deposit typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND, late return 20,000 VND per hour with a full day possibly charged after six hours, no rider insurance provided, cash/bank/QR payment — see motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi, late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi, and is-insurance-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi. Confirm current terms directly with the shop before renting; terms for your specific rental are what you agree at handover.

## What to check before agreeing

Test the questions on the shop, not just the bike: "What happens if the bike is stolen?", "What if I get a puncture outside the city?", "Can my friend ride it?", "What happens if I come back two days early?" Early return, for example, carries no automatic refund entitlement — the answer you receive at handover is the one that counts, and it is easier to negotiate before paying than after.

## Documentation

Ask for a receipt for the deposit, or at minimum photograph the bike from all four sides plus the odometer at handover — timestamped photos settle damage disputes faster than memory. Note existing scratches in front of the shop. The full handover routine is in rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist.

## If the agreement is broken

Disputes are rare when terms were spoken aloud at the start. When they are not, the deposit return is the pinch point: how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi describes what a fair return process looks like, and theft or loss situations follow the responsibility rules in theft-loss-responsibility-rental-motorbike-hanoi.

One habit worth keeping even with the friendliest shop: put the agreed key terms in a message to the shop's phone at handover. A one-line written record protects both of you.
