Over a daily rental, security is the shop's problem after you return the bike. Over a monthly rental, the bike lives your life — your apartment, your workplace, your parking habits. Security becomes a routine, not a one-off.

## Understand your responsibility

Theft and loss responsibility on rental bikes is covered in theft-loss-responsibility-rental-motorbike-hanoi: in short, if the bike is stolen through your own carelessness, the cost conversation is much harder than if you took reasonable care. Read that article before you pick up a long-term bike, not after something goes wrong.

## Overnight storage

The best overnight option is a locked or guarded space — a building basement, a paid motorbike garage, a courtyard behind a gate. Street parking every night for a month multiplies risk. If your accommodation has no storage, ask about nearby parking garages when you set up the rental, and see parking-monthly-renter-hanoi for how daily parking works.

## Locks and habits

Use whatever lock came with the bike, plus a disc lock or chain if you carry one. Never leave the key in the bike "just for a minute". Do not leave the registration papers on the bike — carry copies and keep originals somewhere safe, as documents-needed-rent-motorbike-hanoi explains.

## Keys over months

Losing the only key to a rental mid-term is a real problem; lost-key-rental-motorbike-hanoi covers why replacement can be expensive and slow. Keep the spare key at home, not in the bike's under-seat storage.

## The one-line version

Guarded parking overnight, locks used every time, keys and papers off the bike — a month of small habits is what keeps the deposit intact.
