The CT125 and the Sirius RC come from opposite corners of the Vietnamese market: Honda's trail-styled Cub is a premium weekend machine, while Yamaha's Sirius is the budget workhorse that competes on price and simplicity. Honda Vietnam and Yamaha Vietnam publish full specs for both (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## The core difference

The Sirius RC 110 is a 110.3 cc air-cooled semi-automatic: 5.9 kW at 8,000 rpm, four-speed rotary gearbox, 100 kg wet weight, 21,993,000 VND list for the alloy-wheel RC version with a front disc. The CT125 is a 123.9 cc air-cooled adventure-styled Cub: 6.76 kW at 6,250 rpm, four-speed gearbox, 800 mm seat, 165 mm ground clearance, 117 kg, 85,997,455 VND.

## The verified numbers

| Spec | Sirius RC 110 | CT125 |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 110.3 cc, SOHC, air-cooled | 123.9 cc, air-cooled |

| Kerb weight | 100 kg wet | 117 kg |
| Ground clearance | not published | 165 mm |
| List price | 21,993,000 VND (RC version) | 85,997,455 VND |

Real-world fuel economy varies with speed, load and conditions. Yamaha does not publish the Sirius seat height; Honda does not publish CT125 storage capacity in litres.

## What it means on the road

The Sirius is the value answer: it costs about a quarter of the CT125, carries 0.7 litres more fuel than Honda's small underbones, and its RC version adds a front hydraulic disc. It is transport, pure and simple.

The CT125 is expedition equipment: 165 mm of clearance, torque peaked at 4,750 rpm, 80/90-17 tyres at both ends and a claimed 1.61 L/100 km that undercuts the Sirius despite the bigger engine. It is also 17 kg heavier with a tall 800 mm seat that shorter riders should try before committing.

## Which one for you?

If the budget is the question, the Sirius RC wins before the test ride starts. If rough roads, tall-seat comfort and weekend range matter more than four times the price, the CT125 has no rival in the verified underbone set.
