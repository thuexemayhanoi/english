For a month-long rental, delivery of the bike to your door saves a trip to Long Bien — but delivery in Hanoi is always conditional, never a guaranteed standard service.

A monthly term can hold up to three handovers rather than one: the start, a visit in the middle if the bike needs servicing or swapping, and the end. This guide takes them in that order. Choosing between pickup and delivery in the first place is a separate question, compared in [the guide to picking a handover route]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), so it is not repeated here.

## What delivery actually means here

Delivery or pickup in Hanoi may be available depending on location and prior arrangement, within opening hours (09:00–21:00 daily). There is no fixed delivery fee, no guaranteed delivery time and no delivery outside opening hours. Contact Nguyen Tu to arrange delivery for your situation and confirm the details directly.

That wording is the whole of what can be promised. It does not say who brings the bike, how long a handover takes or whether a particular street can be reached, so each of those is a question for you to put to the shop and keep in writing. The contact route is WhatsApp, Zalo or phone on +84 942 467 674, and [how booking by message, phone or walk-in works]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers what to put in a first message. The other route is to collect the bike yourself at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De, Long Bien, which is open 09:00–21:00 daily.

## Why monthly is different from daily

For a daily rental, delivery is a convenience. For a monthly rental, it is usually only needed twice: once at the start and once at the end. That makes it worth arranging properly — the start-of-rental delivery is also when you do the pickup inspection, sign the agreement and hand over the deposit, so allow real time for it rather than treating it like a parcel drop.

Treat "twice" as the usual case rather than a rule. A month can also bring a third occasion in the middle, such as a maintenance visit or a change of bike, and a change of address can mean the last handover happens somewhere other than the first. The table sets the three moments side by side.

### The three handovers at a glance

| Moment | What happens | Settle beforehand |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Inspection, photos, agreement and deposit | The handover spot, who is present, how the deposit is paid, how the papers are shown |
| Middle, if needed | A maintenance visit or a change of bike | Whether you ride in or the shop can come to you, which bike you ride meanwhile, how the rate and deposit follow a change |
| End | Walk-around, keys, settlement of the deposit | Which moment ends the term, the place and time window, the fuel level, how and when the deposit is settled |

## Questions to settle when arranging

Where exactly can the bike be handed over near your address? Who inspects the bike with you at delivery — the same photos and checks as in [Rental Motorbike Inspection Checklist: What to Check Before You Ride]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}) apply, wherever the handover happens? How will the deposit be paid — cash, bank transfer or QR payment are accepted. [Shop Pickup vs Delivery When Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the general delivery process.

### Your address and the handover spot

A delivery address has to work for someone who has never been to it. Give the street, the house number, the lane or building name and the ward, and add a map pin from whichever map app you use. Hanoi lanes branch into smaller lanes, and a number on its own can leave a person standing at the wrong branch; a pin and a landmark close that gap.

Then settle four practical points before the day:

- **A number you will answer.** Give your own number on Zalo or phone, and keep the phone at hand during the handover window.
- **A spot you can name.** Suggest the building entrance, the end of the lane or a gate, somewhere level with room to walk all the way round the bike in good light, and confirm it in the chat.
- **Who will be there.** Plan to attend yourself. Whether someone else, such as a receptionist or a colleague, may receive the bike for you is not published, so ask before the day rather than assuming it is allowed.
- **Where the bike will sleep.** A month-long rental needs a night-time place for the bike before it needs a delivery slot. Ask your building whether a rental motorbike may stay overnight and where; [daily parking as a monthly renter]({{ '/articles/parking-monthly-renter-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the routine once the bike is yours.

### The papers and the money at the door

Ask at handover how the registration certificate and the compulsory insurance certificate will be shown at a stop, and carry your own licence. [The guide to documents a rider carries]({{ '/articles/documents-to-carry-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) sets out the legal side, so the handover only needs the practical answer for this particular bike.

Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance, so a monthly renter arranges their own cover before the first ride. [Insurance for a long-term renter]({{ '/articles/insurance-for-long-term-renter-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the long-term side of that.

The deposit is typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions, so ask for the figure for your bike when you book. Then ask where and in which form it is paid at the door: cash, bank transfer and QR payment are the accepted forms, and a card is not on the list. Whatever the amount, ask for a receipt: over a monthly term it is the record of what you paid at the start, and you will want it when the deposit is discussed at the end. [Deposits for monthly motorbike rental]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-monthly-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) explains what to expect.

## The handover at the start

The walk-round is the same whether it happens at the shop or at your door, so the full list stays in the inspection checklist. A month adds one thing: the photographs become the baseline you compare against at the end. The table shows what to record and why it matters across a term.

| Check | What to record | Why it matters over a month |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Both sides, front and back, every existing mark | The return walk-around is measured against these photos |
| Lights, horn, brakes, mirrors | A short test of each, with anything weak noted in the chat | All four matter on every ride, and a fault found on day one is easier to tie to the handover than one first noticed in week three |
| Tyres | Tread and sidewalls, and the pressure if you can check it | Inner tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation, so a record of their state helps later |
| Fuel and odometer | A photo of the dashboard | The fuel level to return at is agreed against it, and the odometer shows how far the bike has gone |

Keep the start photos where you will find them in a month: the message thread with the shop is a better home than a camera roll holding hundreds of pictures. Before you sign, check that the chat shows the rate, the deposit and how it is paid, the date and hour the term ends, the way the bike will come back and the fuel level at both ends. The rest of the term, from the first week to renewals, is mapped in [the monthly rental guides]({{ '/topics/monthly-rental/' | relative_url }}).

## During the term: visits, swaps and moves

### A maintenance visit

Maintenance may include an oil change, chain adjustment and tension, tyre-pressure checks and normal inspection, while consumables such as inner tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation. Support is primarily for Hanoi and local use. None of that says where a visit takes place, so these are questions to ask rather than rules to assume:

- Do you ride the bike to Long Bien, or can the shop collect it from your address? This is the delivery question again, and the same wording applies: it may be available depending on location and arrangement.
- How long will the bike be away, and will another bike be available meanwhile? Availability of any bike is for the shop to confirm.
- If a tube or tyre is needed, which part falls to you?

The shop owner's guidance is a periodic check at around 1,500 km or more of use. That is practical guidance, not a manufacturer interval, but it gives a daily commuter a reason to book a visit instead of waiting for a symptom. [Maintenance during a monthly rental]({{ '/articles/maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out the split of responsibilities. No roadside rescue service and no rescue time is promised, so ask in advance what to do if the bike stops away from home.

### A change of bike

Swapping to another bike partway through is a fresh handover: the same checks, a fresh set of photos, and the first bike's condition closed out before the second one begins. What the shop will agree on the day count, the rate and the deposit when the bike changes is a question to settle in the chat. Do not assume that the rate of one model carries over to another. [Switching bikes mid-rental]({{ '/articles/switching-bikes-mid-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the points to agree.

### A change of address

If you move house during the term, tell the shop before any collection is arranged, because the place where the bike is handed back may no longer be the place where it was handed over. [Moving house during a motorbike rental]({{ '/articles/moving-address-during-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the parking side of a move, which is usually the harder part.

## At the end of the rental

Pickup of the bike at the end of the term is a separate arrangement with the same conditions — location-dependent, arranged in advance, within opening hours. Do not leave the return to the final hour of the final day; agree the pickup a few days ahead so the return inspection is unhurried.

Four things are worth writing down when you agree it:

- **Which moment ends the term.** Ask whether the rental ends at the collection time you agreed or when the bike reaches the shop, and have the end date and hour in the chat.
- **Who will be there.** The person who rode the bike should be at the walk-around, which is run against your start photos. [How returning a rental motorbike works]({{ '/articles/returning-a-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the steps in order.
- **Fuel and keys.** The level agreed at the start, and every key you were given.
- **The deposit.** How and when it is settled, and what you will receive as confirmation.

A collection window that slips past the agreed end falls under the published late-return terms: 20,000 VND per hour, and more than six hours late a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. Message the shop as soon as you know and agree any change before the deadline, not after it. If you might want the bike longer, raise that while the term is still running; [returning a long-term rental late]({{ '/articles/late-return-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) shows why the order matters.

If a flight sets your deadline, work backwards from it and leave a margin. Delivery and pickup stay inside 09:00–21:00, so a very early or very late departure may leave no room for a collection, and a ride to the shop inside opening hours then becomes the plan.

## Which route fits your situation

The table sorts common monthly situations by what to ask at each moment. It describes questions, not how the shop runs its handovers.

| Your situation | At the start | During the term | At the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living or working near Long Bien | Collect at the shop, so no address needs finding | Ride in for a visit | Return it to the shop |
| Based far from Long Bien | Ask whether delivery reaches your address | Ask whether a visit can be arranged there | Ask about collection and agree a window |
| Hotel for the first weeks, a flat later | Give the hotel address and ask whether the bike may stay | Tell the shop before you move | Give the new address, not the old one |
| Leaving Hanoi on a fixed flight | Plan the end first, then the start | Raise the departure date early | Return inside opening hours with a margin |

## Mistakes that cost time

- **Arranging only the start.** The middle and the end are separate arrangements, so a delivery at the start says nothing about where the bike will be collected.
- **Sending a pin with no contact number.** A pin gets a person to the lane; only a number answered on the day gets them to you.
- **Assuming the building will take the bike.** If the bike has nowhere to stay overnight, a handover only moves the problem to your door.
- **Letting an address change go unreported.** The end-of-term collection is planned from the address the shop has on record in the chat.
- **Skipping the dashboard photo.** Without the odometer and fuel gauge at the start, distance and fuel become a matter of memory at the end.

## Common questions

### Can a monthly rental be delivered to my apartment?

It may be, depending on location and arrangement, and the shop confirms it for your address and dates. Collecting the bike at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street is the other route.

### Is there a delivery fee for a monthly rental?

No fixed delivery fee is published. If an arrangement involves a cost, ask for the amount in the booking chat before the day.

### Can the bike be serviced or swapped at my address?

Maintenance may include an oil change, chain adjustment and tension, tyre-pressure checks and normal inspection, but where that happens is not published. Ask whether the shop can collect the bike or whether you should ride in, and ask what is available meanwhile.

### Can I have the bike delivered in the evening or before opening?

No. Delivery and pickup stay inside the opening hours, 09:00–21:00 daily, so a slot outside them is not available.

## The one-line version

Delivery and pickup for a monthly rental are arrangeable but conditional — plan both handovers in advance, allow inspection time, and confirm details directly with the shop.
