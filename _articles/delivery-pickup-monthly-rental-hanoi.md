For a month-long rental, delivery of the bike to your door saves a trip to Long Bien — but delivery in Hanoi is always conditional, never a guaranteed standard service.

## What delivery actually means here

Delivery or pickup in Hanoi may be available depending on location and prior arrangement, within opening hours (09:00–21:00 daily). There is no fixed delivery fee, no guaranteed delivery time and no delivery outside opening hours. Contact Nguyen Tu to arrange delivery for your situation and confirm the details directly.

## Why monthly is different from daily

For a daily rental, delivery is a convenience. For a monthly rental, it is usually only needed twice: once at the start and once at the end. That makes it worth arranging properly — the start-of-rental delivery is also when you do the pickup inspection, sign the agreement and hand over the deposit, so allow real time for it rather than treating it like a parcel drop.

## Questions to settle when arranging

Where exactly can the bike be handed over near your address? Who inspects the bike with you at delivery — the same photos and checks as in rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist apply, wherever the handover happens? How will the deposit be paid — cash, bank transfer or QR payment are accepted. Motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi covers the general delivery process.

## At the end of the rental

Pickup of the bike at the end of the term is a separate arrangement with the same conditions — location-dependent, arranged in advance, within opening hours. Do not leave the return to the final hour of the final day; agree the pickup a few days ahead so the return inspection is unhurried.

## The one-line version

Delivery and pickup for a monthly rental are arrangeable but conditional — plan both handovers in advance, allow inspection time, and confirm details directly with the shop.
