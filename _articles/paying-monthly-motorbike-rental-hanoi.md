Paying for a month-long rental is simpler than paying for a day in one respect — one payment instead of many — and more important in another, because larger amounts and longer terms deserve clear records. Pay by cash, bank transfer or QR payment, and agree the due date and proof with the shop.

The published payment options are cash, bank transfer and QR payment, and every published rate and deposit figure is in Vietnamese dong (VND). What is not published is the timetable: when each payment is due, what a renewal payment looks like, what proof you receive, and how the deposit is taken and returned. Those are the points to settle with the shop before the term starts.

## Accepted payment methods

Nguyen Tu accepts cash, bank transfer and QR payment. Card payment is not part of the published arrangements — if you need one, ask directly rather than assuming. The same forms apply whether you are paying a daily, weekly or monthly rate.

Each method leaves a different trail, so the method decides what you need to keep; the table sets them side by side.

| Method | Ask the shop | You keep |
|---|---|---|
| Cash | How the amount is counted and how receipt of it is acknowledged | A written or messaged acknowledgement |
| Bank transfer | The account name, bank and number, and what to write in the transfer note | The confirmation from your bank, with its reference |
| QR payment | Which code you will be shown, and the name that should appear before you confirm | The confirmation screen from the app you pay with |

### Paying in cash

Nothing is recorded automatically when notes change hands, so the paper side needs more care than with the other two methods. Count the money in front of the person receiving it, and ask for a message or a written line stating what was received, on what date and for which rental period.

### Paying by bank transfer

A transfer sent from a banking app produces its own confirmation, which makes it a useful record. Take the receiving account details from the shop itself, by message to +84 942 467 674 or in person, and not from an unsolicited message or an unrelated page; no bank details are published on this site. Before you confirm, read the account name your app displays. If it does not match the name you were given, stop and ask.

Ask what to write in the transfer note, so that the shop can match the payment to your rental. Once the money has been sent, message the shop with the confirmation and ask for an acknowledgement in the same thread.

### Paying by QR code

QR payment means scanning a code, and the published facts do not say which app the shop expects, so ask which code you will be shown and which app to use. The National Payment Corporation of Vietnam (NAPAS) calls the form in which the payee shows the code and the customer scans it "merchant-presented"; the shop publishes "QR payment" without naming a scheme.

If it is a VietQR code, NAPAS sets out the steps on its English-language site: log in to your bank's app, choose the scan function, scan the code or open a saved image of it, check the beneficiary's details, and confirm. NAPAS adds that VietQR complies with EMVCo's QR Payment standard and the State Bank of Vietnam's basic standard for QR codes, and it describes the service as letting customers transfer money from their own accounts to an account at any other NAPAS member bank.

The step to take seriously is the beneficiary check. A code stands for a receiving account, and the name that appears on your screen before you confirm is a part you can check.

## Which method suits which situation

The right method depends mostly on where your money is and what record you want. The table matches a situation to a question worth asking, and it ranks nothing.

| Your situation | Worth asking about | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You hold an account at a Vietnamese bank whose app can scan codes | QR payment or a transfer | The confirmation comes from your own bank app |
| Your only bank account is abroad | A transfer, after checking with your bank, or cash | Whether your bank can pay an account in Vietnam comes first |
| You want a record that your own bank produces | A transfer or QR payment | Cash needs a written acknowledgement from the shop |
| You will pay on the day of handover | Whichever method the shop confirmed beforehand | The method, the amount and the due date are already in the thread |

## When payment falls due

The published facts set no due date for a monthly rental, so the practical pattern to ask about is payment at the start of the rental period for the agreed month, by the method agreed at booking. If you renew for a further month, the renewal follows the same pattern — arrange it before the current term ends rather than after, which keeps the timeline clean (see [Extending or Renewing a Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/extending-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})). Whether the shop agrees to a renewal is for you to ask.

### Questions to settle before you pay

Send these in the booking thread, so that the answers sit next to the dates and the rate; [how to book by WhatsApp, Zalo, phone or walk-in]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers that thread.

- What is the total for the whole term, written out in VND, and which dates does it cover?
- Is payment due at handover, before it or on another date, and is the answer the same for the rent and the deposit?
- Can the rent be paid in more than one part, and if so on which dates?
- Which method will each payment use, and will a renewal use the same one?
- What proof will you receive for each payment, and when?
- In what form is the deposit taken, and in what form and when does it come back?
- What happens if a payment arrives after its due date?
- If the bike is delivered, are the payment and the deposit taken at the door, and by which method?

On late payment: the published late-return charge is 20,000 VND per hour and applies to returning the bike late; beyond six hours a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. The published facts say nothing about a payment that arrives late, and nothing about whether a renewal agreed after the end date counts as a late return, so ask. [The late-return guide for long rentals]({{ '/articles/late-return-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the charge itself.

Delivery in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement, and [the delivery guide for monthly rentals]({{ '/articles/delivery-pickup-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers that side of the handover.

### Renewal payments

A renewal is a second payment, and it is easy to leave looser than the first. Before paying for the next period, ask for three things in the thread: the amount in VND, the dates it covers, and whether the deposit stays as it is or is handled again.

### Paying for time you may not use

The published position on early return is that there is no automatic refund entitlement for unused time, so a payment covering a whole month pays for the period you agreed, not for the days you end up riding. Choose the term with that in mind, and if you would want a refund or a credit for unused days, ask for the answer in the thread before you pay; [the guide to ending a monthly rental early]({{ '/articles/early-return-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) has the detail.

## Receipts and records

For month-scale amounts, keep the same records you would keep for any significant payment: a receipt or transfer confirmation, and a message thread that shows the agreed rate and period. This is not distrust — it is what a written agreement looks like in practice, and it protects both sides. The terms worth having in writing are listed in [Long-Term Motorbike Rental Agreements in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-agreement-long-term-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

If the shop hands over a paper receipt, photograph it straight away, while every line is legible. Check each confirmation against the thread before you put it away: the amount in VND, the receiving name, the date and the period it pays for.

### Keeping the record usable

Keep the proofs and the thread in one place you can open without a mobile connection, such as a single album and one saved conversation, and back them up before you travel or change phone. Keep, too, the message in which the shop gave its payment details and the amount, so that each payment can be traced to a request. When you renew, add the new amount, dates and proof to the same thread, and keep it all until the bike is back and the deposit is settled.

## Budgeting the payment

The monthly rates on published-rate models run 1,000,000–2,000,000 VND depending on the bike ([Motorbike Rental Monthly Prices in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }})); the deposit (typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND) is held, not spent, and comes back at term end. The published facts do not fix that timing, so treat "term end" as a point to confirm before you pay, not as a promise. The full month-scale budget — fuel, parking, consumables — is laid out in [Monthly Motorbike Rental Budget in Hanoi: The Full Cost]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-budget-monthly-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

### The deposit is a payment of its own

On the first day two sums may fall due: the rent for the term and the deposit. Give the deposit its own questions: in what form it is taken, what is written down when it is received, when and in what form it comes back, and which charges, if any, could be taken from it instead of being paid separately. [The monthly deposit guide]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-monthly-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers what a long hold means, and [how a deposit return works]({{ '/articles/how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the return walk-around, so this guide stays with the payment itself.

Add the two figures together before you go, so that you know what you need on the day, and check the daily limit your own bank applies to transfers and cash withdrawals: a month's rent plus a deposit is a bigger sum than a day's rental. The wider picture of how a longer rental runs is in [Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi: How It Works]({{ '/articles/monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

### Reading amounts in VND

Vietnamese dong amounts have many digits, and one missing zero changes a sum tenfold. The published daily rates (150,000 VND and 200,000 VND, depending on the model) show the scale. Write every amount in full, with separators, and read each reply digit by digit before you pay. If a message quotes another currency, ask for the VND figure and the exchange rate behind it, so that the figure you pay is the figure you were quoted.

## Foreign renters: questions for your own bank

Everything above assumes you can reach a Vietnamese account. If your money is in a bank outside Vietnam, the shop's details are only half of a transfer, and the other half depends on your bank and your account, not on the shop. Put these questions to your bank before you rely on a transfer or a QR payment:

- Can it send a payment to an account in Vietnam, and in which currency would your account be debited?
- What fees and exchange rate will it apply, and could an intermediary bank deduct a charge on the way?
- What per-payment and daily limits apply, and how long does a payment take to arrive?
- Will the one-time code or approval prompt reach you while you are in Vietnam?
- Can its app scan a Vietnamese QR code at all, given that NAPAS describes VietQR transfers between accounts at its member banks?
- If you will pay in cash, what does it charge for withdrawals in Vietnam, and what limit applies to each one?

Then ask the shop the matching question: does it accept a transfer from an account abroad, and who bears any fees, so that the full amount due is the amount received? Keep the answer in the thread. If no route works, cash is a published method.

## Splitting payment within a household

If two people share the rental cost, decide the split before the term starts, and let one person make each payment, so that receipts and transfer references sit in one place; ask the shop whether it prefers a single payment for each agreement.

For households renting two bikes, each bike carries its own agreement and deposit: see [Renting Two Motorbikes for a Household in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/two-motorbikes-household-hanoi/' | relative_url }}). Ask the shop how it records two bikes, and keep a separate set of payment proofs for each one. Settle your own split with the other renter by a separate transfer, and keep that message with the rental records.

## Common questions

### Can I pay for a monthly rental by card?

No card option is published. The accepted methods are cash, a bank transfer or a QR code, so plan around one of them unless the shop tells you otherwise in writing.

### Do I have to pay a month in advance?

The published facts do not say. Ask whether payment is due at handover, before it or at another point, and get the answer in writing next to the amount.

### Will I get a receipt?

A receipt format is not published. For cash, ask for a message or a written line; for a transfer or a QR payment, keep your bank's confirmation and the thread that shows what it paid for.

### Is the deposit paid the same way as the rent?

The published deposit range is typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the vehicle and conditions. The published facts do not say in what form it is taken or returned, so ask, and ask for its acknowledgement to be separate from the rent's.

## The one-line version

Cash, transfer or QR; agree when each payment is due; keep the receipt and the message record; renew before the term ends. More on long rentals is gathered in [the monthly rental guides]({{ '/topics/monthly-rental/' | relative_url }}).
