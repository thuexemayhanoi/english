Between the entry Vision and the stronger Air Blade 160 sits the Air Blade 125 - same family, same styling language, lighter and cheaper. Honda Vietnam publishes the 160's full technical table but not a complete separate table for the 125, so this review sticks strictly to what the manufacturer's pages state (retrieved 23 September 2026): a 125 cc liquid-cooled engine, 110 kg kerb weight, and a 23.2-litre underseat compartment that takes two half-helmets, plus a USB-C charge port. Power and torque figures are left out rather than estimated.

## Where the 125 sits

Engine capacity in Vietnam affects both licence requirements and insurance categories, which our licence [guides]({{ '/articles/vietnam-motorbike-licence-classes-explained/' | relative_url }}) explain. For most riders the 125 class is the sweet spot: enough torque for two-up city riding without the weight or price of a 160. Our 110cc vs 125cc comparison covers the practical differences.

## Everyday usability

At 110 kg (Honda's figure for the 125, versus 113 kg for the 160) it is manageable for smaller riders while still feeling planted. The 23.2-litre underseat bay is the practical headline: two half-helmets or one full helmet plus groceries. See the scooter underseat storage comparison for how that ranks against rivals.

## Who it suits

Riders who find the Vision's 110 cc too small but don't need the 160's extra performance; commuters who carry a helmet and a day-pack daily. Honda lists the Air Blade family retail price from 42,404,727 VND.
