The under-seat tub is the scooter's superpower: a lockable, weatherproof, vibration-damped boot that arrives with the bike. Most riders use a quarter of it well and the rest badly. This is the guide to the rest.

## What the space actually is

On classic automatic scooters it is a moulded tub that fits a full-face helmet (on many models) plus a flat section for documents and small items. On newer scooters, some of that volume is consumed — by the fuel tank or battery — and the tub shrinks: know your model's reality before planning storage around it. The tub is not waterproof-forever: seat seals age, and monsoon rain eventually finds old gaskets. Keep a sealed pouch inside for anything that matters — the everyday-carry list is in what-to-carry-riding-motorbike-vietnam.

## What belongs under the seat

The honest priority list: documents (vehicle papers if you own; a copy of rental contact details if you rent), rain poncho (the single most-used item in Vietnamese scooter life), a microfiber cloth for the visor and mirror, the spare-parts kit (the under-seat spares list is in spare-parts-kit-underseat-motorbike), and the helmet when parked — the most security-conscious habit in daily city use, and better than any helmet lock (top-boxes-racks-motorbike-vietnam covers the same logic for box riders). Valuables go under the seat only while you ride: under-seat storage on a parked scooter is a locked drawer, not a safe — the parking realities are in where-to-park-motorbike-hanoi.

## What does not belong there

Petrol in containers (fumes plus sparks plus a hot, sealed tub — never), food that invites the local ants and gecko population into your paperwork, wet riding gear sealed while damp (everything moulds — dry things before storing), and anything heavy that turns the tub floor into a rattle-factory against the frame.

## The two habits that make it work

Empty it occasionally — the tub becomes an archaeological site otherwise — and check the seat's hinge, cable and latch now and then: a seat that stops latching is a lock that stopped existing. The under-seat space plus a rack or box is the full Vietnamese scooter luggage system; used deliberately, it swallows most of daily life without a single bungee cord.

The tub is the most reliable storage on the scooter because the bike is already carrying it — fill it with the right things, and it rides like a pillion who never complains.
