Phone navigation drains fast in Hanoi traffic, so a built-in charging port is a genuinely useful commuter feature. This list is built only from what manufacturer pages state (retrieved 23 September 2026) - port types are often unspecified, and we say so rather than guess.

## Verified USB-C ports

- Honda Vario 125 - USB-C port in the front box
- Honda SH Mode 125 - USB-C port in the front box
- Honda Air Blade 125 - USB-C charge port (verified alongside the 23.2-litre bay)
- Honda Air Blade 160 - USB-C charge port

## Verified USB-A or unspecified ports

- Honda Vario 160 - USB-A port rated 10.5 W (5V, 2.1A) in the left front box
- Yamaha Janus 125 - USB charging port on current versions
- Yamaha FreeGo 125 - phone charging port
- Yamaha Grande - phone charging port

For several models (Yamaha NMAX, NVX, Lexi, Gear, Latte, Honda Lead, SH160i, Vision), the retrieved manufacturer pages did not state a charging port - those are omitted from both lists rather than assumed either way.

## Adding charging to any scooter

Most scooters accept an aftermarket USB charger wired to the battery - our USB chargers guide covers what to buy and the electrical basics, and our phone mounts guide covers where to put the phone legally (phone use while riding is a fineable distraction - our article covers the rules).
