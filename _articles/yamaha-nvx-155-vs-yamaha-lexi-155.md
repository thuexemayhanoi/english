Yamaha sells three scooters on the same 155.1 cc Blue Core VVA engine - the NMAX, the NVX and the Lexi - and the sharpest internal choice is between the NVX's aggressive value package and the Lexi's comfort-first layout. All figures are from Yamaha Vietnam's official pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Same engine, same numbers

Both publish 11.3 kW at 8,000 rpm and 14.2 Nm at 6,500 rpm from the liquid-cooled VVA engine. There is no performance case between them - the choice is chassis and mission. How VVA varies valve timing and lift is covered in our dedicated explainer.

## Chassis and comfort

The Lexi is the comfort commuter: 118 kg wet, a low 768 mm seat, 14-inch wheels at both ends and a long 1,353 mm wheelbase for stability - our wheelbase guide explains why that matters on rough streets. The NVX is the sport bike of the pair: 125 kg, 790 mm seat, a fat 140/70-14 rear tyre and sharper steering.

## Storage and tank

The NVX's 25-litre underseat bay beats the Lexi's unlisted bay on the retrieved page (the Lexi page did not publish a storage figure, so we do not quote one). The Lexi's small 4.2-litre tank is its one real compromise versus the NVX's 5.5 litres - fuel-range planning matters more on the Lexi; see our longest-range scooters guide for how to think about tank times economy.

## Price

Yamaha lists the Lexi 155 VVA-ABS from 48,500,000 VND (48,900,000 VND for the version shown) and the NVX 155 VVA ABS at 55,300,000 VND. The Lexi is the comfort-and-value pick; the NVX charges more for attitude and storage.

## Verdict

Ride far, two-up, on mixed roads: Lexi. Ride hard, carry more, want the sporty look: NVX. Cross-check both against the NMAX in our NMAX vs NVX and Lexi vs NMAX comparisons. Neither is a price-published Nguyen Tu rental model - contact us to confirm current availability.
