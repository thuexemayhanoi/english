The Wave Alpha and the Future 125 FI are two ends of Honda Vietnam's semi-automatic range: the country's cheapest new motorbike and its long-legged 125-class underbone. Honda publishes the full specification for both (retrieved 23 September 2026), so the trade-offs are easy to see.

## Engines

Wave Alpha: 109.2 cc, air-cooled, 6.12 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.44 Nm at 5,500 rpm. Future 125 FI: 124.9 cc, air-cooled, 6.83 kW at 7,500 rpm and a much stronger 10.2 Nm at 5,500 rpm. Both use a four-speed rotary gearbox with no clutch lever, both offer kick and electric start. The Future's extra 1.76 Nm of torque is what you feel pulling away with a passenger or climbing a ramp.

## Economy and range

The Future is Honda's thriftiest listed underbone at 1.47 L/100 km against the Alpha's 1.72 L/100 km, and its 4.6-litre tank is the largest of any 110/125-class Wave or Blade. Manufacturer figures apply to standard test conditions; real-world economy varies with traffic, load and riding style.

## Size and comfort

Future 125: 1,931 mm long, 756 mm seat, 104 kg. Wave Alpha: 1,913 mm, 770 mm seat, 96 kg. The Future's lower seat and longer wheelbase (1,258 vs 1,224 mm) suit taller riders and two-up travel; the Alpha's 8 kg weight advantage shows up when pushing the bike into tight parking.

## Price and rental

List prices run from 18,841,091 VND (Alpha) to from 30,622,909 VND (Future, three versions).
