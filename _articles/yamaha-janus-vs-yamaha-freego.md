Both use Yamaha's 124.9 cc Blue Core engine with CVT and Stop & Start - so choosing between Janus and FreeGo is not about the engine. It is about what sits around it. All figures from Yamaha Vietnam's product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## The numbers side by side

| | Janus 125 | FreeGo 125 |
|---|---|---|
| Max power | 7.0 kW @ 8,000 rpm | 7.0 kW @ 8,000 rpm |
| Max torque | 9.6 Nm @ 5,500 rpm | 9.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm |
| Kerb weight | 97 kg | 100 kg (wet) |
| Seat height | 770 mm | 780 mm |
| Claimed economy | 1.98 L/100 km | 2.03 L/100 km |
| Fuel tank | 4.2 L | 3.9 L |
| Underseat storage | 15.3 L | 25 L |
| Tyres | 80/80-14, 100/70-14 | 100/90-12, 110/90-12 |
| Equipment | USB port, front disc | SmartKey, LED headlight, USB, front disc; ABS version (FreeGo S) |
| Listed retail price | from 29,151,000 VND | from 30,340,000 VND |

## What the numbers mean

- **Storage:** the FreeGo's 25-litre bay takes a full-face helmet; the Janus's 15.3-litre bay takes a half-helmet and not much more.
- **Equipment:** the FreeGo adds smart key and an LED front light; the Janus keeps it simple and light.
- **Wheels:** the Janus's 14-inch wheels vs the FreeGo's 12-inch set - our tyre [sizes explainer]({{ '/articles/scooter-tyre-sizes-explained/' | relative_url }}) covers the ride trade-offs.
- **Weight and economy:** near-identical; the Janus is marginally lighter and marginally thriftier on paper.
- **Brakes:** both use a front disc with rear drum; the FreeGo S offers ABS.

## Which should you choose?

Janus for the lowest price and weight - the pure city tool. FreeGo for storage, features and the ABS option. Neither is among Nguyen Tu's price-published rental models - contact us to confirm current availability, and see our Honda vs Yamaha comparison for the rival models.
