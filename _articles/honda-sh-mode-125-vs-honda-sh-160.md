Both of these are premium Honda scooters, and both carry SH DNA - but they are aimed at different riders and different budgets. All figures come from Honda Vietnam's official model pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and performance

The SH Mode 125 uses a 124.8 cc liquid-cooled eSP+ engine with 8.2 kW at 8,500 rpm and 11.7 Nm at 5,000 rpm. The SH160i's 156.9 cc liquid-cooled eSP+ unit produces 12.4 kW at 8,500 rpm and 14.8 Nm at 6,500 rpm - roughly 50 percent more power and substantially more torque, delivered slightly higher in the rev range. The SH Mode is a comfortable city scooter; the SH160i has genuine open-road pace for longer Hanoi-to-province runs.

## Fuel economy

Honda's published figures: SH Mode 125 at 2.16 litres/100 km from a 5.6-litre tank; SH160i at 2.34 litres/100 km from a 7-litre tank. The SH Mode is more economical per kilometre, but the SH's larger tank means fewer stops overall - see our fuel economy comparison for the full class picture.

## Size, weight and seat height

The SH Mode weighs 116 kg with a 765 mm seat - accessible to nearly everyone. The SH160i weighs 134 kg with a 799 mm seat - the tallest and heaviest combination in the mainstream premium class. Shorter riders should try both seats before deciding; our seat height comparison puts the numbers side by side.

## Equipment and safety

SH Mode: 4.2-inch TFT display, smart key, USB-C, 18.5 L underseat storage, front ABS on the Sport, Special and Premium versions, 16-inch front wheel. SH160i: 4.2-inch TFT with Bluetooth phone connectivity (SH160i), smart key, USB-C, 28 L underseat storage, ABS on both wheels plus Honda's HSTC traction control on ABS versions, 16-inch wheels front and rear. The SH160i is the only one of the two with two-wheel ABS and traction control.

## Price

Honda's published list prices: 66,361,091 VND for the SH Mode versions shown, versus 104,490,000 VND for the SH160i Sport version. That gap of roughly 38 million VND is the real decision - see our premium scooter overview for where each sits in the market.

## Verdict

Choose the SH Mode 125 if you ride mostly in the city, want a lower seat and lighter chassis, and like the badge at a lower price. Choose the SH160i if you ride longer distances, carry a pillion, want two-wheel ABS with traction control, and accept a taller, heavier machine.
