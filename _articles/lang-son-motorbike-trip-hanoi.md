Lang Son is the closest thing Hanoi has to a border-town weekend: about 150-170 km northeast, a provincial capital under limestone cliffs with the Huu Nghi (Friendship) crossing to China on its doorstep. For motorbike riders it is a two-to-three-day trip with a twist the guidebooks gloss over — the cars all take the expressway, and you cannot.

## The legal road for bikes

The Hanoi-Lang Son Expressway (CT01) does the run by car in two and a half to three hours, but expressways are closed to motorbikes in Vietnam, with real fines for violators. Riders take the old QL1A corridor northeast instead — the classic colonial road, busier than the mountain routes but rolling and scenic through the delta fringe and into the hills. Budget four to five hours rather than three, and plan the trip around that honestly. Our expressway rules guide covers exactly which roads are off-limits and why.

## What to do there

- **Huu Nghi border gate:** the Friendship crossing, open roughly 7:00 to 19:00 (published guidance suggests arriving before late afternoon to clear queues). You can visit the gate area as a sight; actually crossing into China requires full Chinese visa/entry arrangements and is a separate mission.
- **Lang Son city:** Tam Thanh cave and pagoda in the cliff behind town, the Ky Lua/To Thi market streets, and the limestone walls that ring the whole bowl.
- **Chi Lang and the Mac Dynasty Citadel:** the historic defile south of the city where invading armies bled for six centuries — a worthwhile stop on the QL1A approach.
- **Bac Son valley (en route):** the photogenic rice-bowl valley with its famous viewpoint, about 160 km out on the QL1A with the last stretch on the DT241/243 through Huu Lien. Rider guides call the Huu Lien section one of the most beautiful rides in the province; one night suffices, two is better if you want the viewpoints at the right light.

## A workable plan

- **Day 1:** Hanoi via QL1A to Bac Son valley, afternoon at the viewpoint, homestay in Bac Son or Huu Lien.
- **Day 2:** Bac Son to Lang Son city (short leg), Tam Thanh cave, the market streets, overnight in the city.
- **Day 3:** Huu Nghi gate in the morning, then the QL1A/QL4 option home — or continue toward Cao Bang on the QL4A flank of the northeast loop.

## Practical notes

- The QL1A carries trucks: ride it defensively, overtake only with clean sightlines, and expect village-speed sections through every town.
- Carry your passport; this is a border province and identity checks on the QL4A/Ql1A corridor are routine. Our tourist-riding licence guide covers what documents you need on the road.
- Homestays in Bac Son and hotels in Lang Son are simple but reliable — apply the overnight-stays checklist including bike parking.
- Autumn gives the best weather and the Bac Son rice colours; summer afternoons bring storms (see our seasons [guide]({{ '/articles/motorbike-trip-seasons-north-vietnam/' | relative_url }})).
