Yamaha's 125 range spans from the hybrid-assisted Gear to the roomy, style-led Latte, and the two make a revealing pair: one chases the lowest running cost, the other the most practical layout. Figures below are from Yamaha Vietnam's official pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines

Both are Blue Core 124.9 cc singles with CVT, but tuned differently. The Gear 125 Hybrid pairs its air-cooled engine with a smart motor generator that assists for up to three seconds after launch, publishing 6.2 kW at 6,500 rpm, 10.6 Nm at 4,500 rpm and a remarkable 1.7 litres/100 km - the headline figure of [fuel economy ranking]({{ '/articles/most-fuel-efficient-scooters-vietnam-verified/' | relative_url }}). The Latte's simpler forced-air-cooled engine makes 6.05 kW at 6,500 rpm and 9.77 Nm at 5,000 rpm at 1.80 litres/100 km. How the hybrid system works is covered in our dedicated explainer.

## Layout and storage

The Latte is the practical one: 37 litres of underseat storage on a flat floor, 100 kg wet, 790 mm seat and small 10/12-inch wheels for agility. The Gear is the featherweight: 96 kg, 750 mm seat, 17.4-litre bay and 12-inch tubeless tyres.

## Price

The Gear lists from 30,437,000 VND; the Latte at 39,077,000 VND for the version shown. You pay more for the Latte's space and style; you save with the Gear and spend less on fuel.

## Verdict

Carry a full-face helmet and shopping, ride in skirts-friendly step-through comfort: Latte. Minimum weight, minimum fuel, minimum price: Gear 125.
