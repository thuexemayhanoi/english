The Vision is Vietnam's default scooter; the Vario 125 is Honda's newest mainstream 125 and it arrives with hardware the Vision never had. If you are choosing between Honda's proven favourite and its new standard-bearer, the trade-offs are clear once you line up the verified Honda Vietnam figures (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine

The Vision runs a 110 cc air-cooled eSP single - simple, proven and cheap to keep alive, with the whole city's repair ecosystem knowing it. The Vario 125 runs a 125 cc liquid-cooled eSP with PGM-FI, 8.48 kW at 8,500 rpm and 11.9 Nm at 5,000 rpm, plus idling stop for Hanoi's endless red-light queues. The Vario is the stronger, more refined engine; the Vision is the simpler one that never surprises you.

## Weight and wheels

Honda lists the Vision at 97 kg kerb and the Vario 125 at 111 kg. That 14 kg gap is real at parking speed and when paddling a loaded bike - see our verified kerb weight table for how this class lines up. The Vision's 16-inch front wheel is a stability asset on broken streets, explained in our 16-inch wheel guide; the Vario's 14-inch wheels trade a little of that for agility and a lower seat.

## Features and price

The Vario 125 brings a smart key, LCD panel, USB-C port, full LED lighting and CBS brakes - features that used to sit a class above. The Vision keeps things basic and cheap to run. The Vario's list price is 41,913,818 VND for the versions shown; the Vision has long traded on price of ownership rather than specification.

## Rental context

Nguyen Tu publishes rental rates for the Honda Vision (200,000 VND day; 800,000-1,000,000 VND week; 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND month). No rental rate is published for the Vario 125 - contact us to confirm current availability of either model.

## Which one

Buy the Vision for simplicity, light weight, low running cost and parts availability everywhere. Buy the Vario 125 if you want a modern 125 with liquid cooling, smart key and idling stop, and accept the extra weight.
