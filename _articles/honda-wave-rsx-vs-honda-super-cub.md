Both of these are Hondas with the same underbone silhouette, and both shift through four gears without a clutch lever. But the Wave RSX costs 25,664,727 VND and the Super Cub C125 costs 88,353,813 VND (Honda VN list prices, retrieved 23 September 2026). What does three and a half times the money actually buy?

## Engines

Super Cub C125: 123.9 cc, air-cooled, 6.87 kW at 7,500 rpm, 10.15 Nm at 5,000 rpm. Wave RSX: 109.2 cc, air-cooled with PGM-FI, 6.46 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.70 Nm at 6,000 rpm. The Cub is the stronger engine, but not dramatically - you are buying finish and heritage as much as performance.

## Economy

This is close: Honda publishes 1.55 L/100 km for the Cub and 1.56 L/100 km for the RSX. The RSX holds 4.0 litres, the Cub 3.7 litres, so touring range is similar. Manufacturer figures, real world varies.

## Size and storage

Cub: 780 mm seat, 109 kg, 1,910 mm long. RSX: 760 mm seat, 98 kg, 1,922 mm. The RSX's U-box takes one half helmet and uses a 4-in-1 key; Honda lists no storage for the Cub beyond its classic front luggage hook aesthetic. The RSX is 11 kg lighter and easier to wrangle in Old Quarter alleys.

## Which one?

As transport, the RSX is the rational buy - our RSX review covers why. The Cub is a lifestyle statement with global heritage and premium finish, closer to a scooter price than an underbone price.
