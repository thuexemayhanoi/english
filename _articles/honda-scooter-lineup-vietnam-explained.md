Honda dominates Vietnam's scooter market, and its range is now wide enough that choosing between models is genuinely confusing. This guide walks the whole Honda Vietnam scooter lineup from bottom to top, using only specifications published on Honda Vietnam's official product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026), plus the owner-approved rental price list where one exists.

## Honda Vision - the default city scooter

The 110 cc entry point: air-cooled eSP engine, 97 kg kerb weight, 16-inch front wheel, published consumption 1.82 litres/100 km. The most common scooter on Vietnamese roads. Rental price published by Nguyen Tu: 200,000 VND per day, 800,000-1,000,000 VND per week, 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND per month. Full review: Honda Vision 110.

## Honda Air Blade 125 and 160 - the sporty commuters

Two versions of the same idea: a compact, quick automatic for riders who want more pace than a 110. The 160 (156.9 cc, liquid-cooled, 11.2 kW) is the sporty pick; the 125 is the balanced middle ground. Rental price published by Nguyen Tu for the Air Blade category: 200,000 VND per day, 800,000 VND per week, 1,400,000 VND per month. Reviews: Air Blade 160, Air Blade 125.

## Honda Lead 125 - the cargo tool

Built around its 37-litre underseat bay - the biggest in the mainstream class - with a 760 mm seat, the lowest in our comparison, and an ABS version available. The choice for riders who carry things every day. Full review: Honda Lead 125.

## Honda Sh mode 125 - the junior premium

The SH look without the SH price: liquid-cooled eSP+ engine (8.2 kW), TFT display, smart key, front ABS on upper versions, 16-inch front wheel, listed at 66,361,091 VND. Full review: Sh mode 125; compared against the full SH here.

## Honda SH160i/125i - the flagship

The status scooter: 12.4 kW in 160 form, two-wheel ABS with HSTC traction control on upper versions, Bluetooth-connected TFT display, 7-litre tank, 134 kg. Listed at 104,490,000 VND for the SH160i Sport version. Full review: SH160i/125i.

## Honda Vario 125 and Vario 160 - the new sporty pair

The newest models in Honda's range: the Vario 125 (liquid-cooled, smart key, USB-C, CBS, 41,913,818 VND) and the Vario 160 (11.3 kW eSP+, front ABS, flat floor, 56,690,000 VND). Reviews: Vario 125, Vario 160.

## Honda Click - the import

The Click 125i is imported through Honda's network rather than built on a Honda Vietnam product page we could verify, so we make no technical claims here. It is on Nguyen Tu's owner-approved rental price list at 150,000 VND per day, 600,000-700,000 VND per week, 1,000,000-1,200,000 VND per month. Contact us to confirm current availability.
