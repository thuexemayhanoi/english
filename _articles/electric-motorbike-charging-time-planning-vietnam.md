Charging time is the electric rider's real currency: not how far the bike goes, but how long the bike is unusable while it gets there. Vietnamese machines answer that question in three very different ways, and the official numbers make the differences plain.

## Three speeds of refuelling

| Method | Verified time | Range bought |
|---|---|---|
| Dat Bike Weaver 200 home charge | 1 h for the first 100 km; 3 h for 200 km | up to 200 km (claimed, 35 km/h conditions) |
| Dat Bike Weaver++ at Dat Charge | 100 km in 20 minutes | 200 km per charge (claimed) |
| VinFast battery swap | the time to exchange packs | a fresh pair of 1.5 kWh packs |

The swap is the structural answer: a V-Green cabinet exchange takes as long as lifting one pack out and seating another, and VinFast's network had its first 4,500 stations installed by mid-January 2026, with 45,000 cabinets planned across 34 provinces and cities. A swap rider plans routes around cabinet locations, not wall-clock hours.

## Planning around real days

Home charging rewards routine. A Weaver 200 ridden 40 km a day needs under an hour of charging most evenings — overnight is unnecessary, though it does no harm to the schedule. A VinFast scooter rider with one pack on the bike and one on the charger at home effectively never waits: the spare is always full.

The hard case is the unplanned long day. A rider who burns range faster than expected faces hours, not minutes, on a home-charged machine — which is why the Weaver++'s 20-minute fast-charge figure and the swap network exist. If your week contains such days, buy for them.

## The habits that shrink charging anxiety

- Charge on arrival home, not before the next ride — packs topped up in the evening are ready for the morning.
- Know your real range, not the claimed one — hills, load and cold shrink it (see the real-world range guide).
- Keep the charger with the bike; the manufacturer's charger is the only one matched to the pack.
- For shared or apartment situations, read our [home-charging and work-charging guide]({{ '/articles/charging-electric-motorbike-at-home-vietnam/' | relative_url }})s.

Charging time is a solvable planning problem. Treat it like fuel stops and it stays boring — which is the goal.
