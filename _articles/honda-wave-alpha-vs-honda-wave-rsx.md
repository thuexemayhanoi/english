Honda sells two 110-class Waves in Vietnam, and from the outside they look nearly identical. The difference is bigger than the badges suggest: Honda Vietnam publishes different engines, tanks and prices for the Wave Alpha and the Wave RSX (all figures retrieved 23 September 2026).

## The core difference

The Wave Alpha is the entry point: 109.2 cc, air-cooled, 6.12 kW at 7,500 rpm and 8.44 Nm at 5,500 rpm, electric start, a 3.7-litre tank and a manufacturer list price of 18,841,091 VND. The Wave RSX sits above it: the same 109.2 cc capacity but with Honda's PGM-FI electronic fuel injection, more power (6.46 kW at 7,500 rpm), more torque (8.70 Nm at 6,000 rpm), a bigger 4.0-litre tank and a list price of 25,664,727 VND. Both use a four-speed rotary gearbox with no clutch lever - the semi-automatic format our scooter-versus-semi-automatic guide covers in detail.

## Fuel economy

This is where the RSX justifies itself. Honda publishes 1.72 L/100 km for the Wave Alpha and 1.56 L/100 km for the RSX, and the RSX carries 0.3 litres more fuel. Those are manufacturer figures under standard test conditions - real consumption always varies with load, speed and traffic. Over a month of daily Hanoi commuting, the difference is real but modest.

## Size, weight and practicalities

The RSX is slightly longer (1,922 vs 1,913 mm), a touch lower at the seat (760 vs 770 mm) and marginally heavier (98 vs 96 kg). Both ride 17-inch wheels with 70/90-17 front and 80/90-17 rear tyres. The RSX adds a U-box that holds one half helmet and a 4-in-1 key system - genuinely useful if you park on the street. The Alpha lists no storage.

## Which Wave makes sense for you?

Renting for days or weeks in Hanoi: the Wave Alpha is the price-published rental class at 150,000 VND per day in our fleet, and for city riding its simpler specification is a virtue. Contact us to confirm current availability of either model. Buying: the roughly 6.8-million-VND list-price gap buys injection, a bigger tank and storage, and the RSX is the better long-distance tool.
