This comparison is really a question about priorities: do you want the premium-badge experience of the SH Mode 125, or the stronger engine and lighter chassis of the Air Blade 160 - for around 10 million VND less? All figures come from Honda Vietnam's official product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and performance

The Air Blade 160's 156.9 cc liquid-cooled engine produces 11.2 kW at 8,000 rpm and 14.8 Nm at 6,000 rpm. The SH Mode 125's 124.8 cc liquid-cooled eSP+ engine produces 8.2 kW at 8,500 rpm and 11.7 Nm at 5,000 rpm. The Air Blade is clearly the faster machine, with more torque available lower in the rev range; the SH Mode is comfortable rather than quick - our power [and torque explainer]({{ '/articles/underbone-power-torque-explained/' | relative_url }}) covers how to read the gap.

## Fuel economy

Honda publishes 2.16 litres/100 km for the SH Mode 125 and 2.23 litres/100 km for the Air Blade 160 - nearly identical, and both efficient for their classes. See the fuel economy comparison.

## Size, weight and wheels

SH Mode: 116 kg, 765 mm seat, 16-inch front wheel, 1,304 mm wheelbase - the more stable, more accessible package. Air Blade: 113 kg, 775 mm seat, 14-inch wheels, 1,286 mm wheelbase - the lighter, more compact traffic tool. The SH Mode's 16-inch front wheel smooths out broken streets; the Air Blade is easier to thread through gaps.

## Equipment and safety

SH Mode: 4.2-inch TFT display, smart key with key-left-on warning, USB-C, 18.5 L storage, front ABS on Sport, Special and Premium versions. Air Blade 160: smart key, USB-C, LCD meter, 4.4-litre tank. Both offer premium features; the SH Mode's dash is the more modern item. ABS availability by version is covered in which scooters have ABS in Vietnam.

## Price

Honda lists the SH Mode at 66,361,091 VND and the Air Blade 160 well below it. The premium overview explains where each sits in the market.

## Renting context

The Air Blade is one of Nguyen Tu's price-published rental models: 200,000 VND per day, 800,000 VND per week, 1,400,000 VND per month, per the owner-verified price list. The SH Mode is not price-published - contact us to confirm current availability.

## Verdict

Choose the Air Blade 160 for pace, a lighter chassis and a published rental price. Choose the SH Mode for the premium look, the lower seat, the 16-inch front wheel and the modern TFT dash.
