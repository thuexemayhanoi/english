Vietnam's underbone market is a two-brand contest, and both brands publish complete specifications (retrieved 23 September 2026). This guide compares the verified pairs class by class - no forum folklore, only manufacturer data.

## The 110 class: Wave Alpha and Wave RSX vs Sirius RC

| Spec | Wave Alpha | Wave RSX | Sirius RC 110 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | 6.12 kW | 6.46 kW | 5.9 kW |
| Torque | 8.44 Nm | 8.70 Nm | 9.0 Nm |
| Claimed economy | 1.72 L/100 km | 1.56 L/100 km | 2.08 L/100 km |
| Tank | 3.7 L | 4.0 L | 4.2 L |
| Weight | 96 kg | 98 kg | 100 kg wet |
| List price | 18,841,091 VND | 25,664,727 VND | 21,993,000 VND |

Reading it honestly: Honda wins the economy numbers, and the Sirius counters with the biggest tank and the highest torque figure of the three. The Sirius also offers a disc-brake RC version. Honda publishes fuel system details for the RSX (PGM-FI); neither page states a fuel system for the Wave Alpha or Sirius - so no injection or carburettor claims are made for them here.

## The 155 class: Winner R and CBR150R vs Exciter 155 VVA

| Spec | Winner R | CBR150R | Exciter 155 VVA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | 11.5 kW | 12.6 kW | 13.2 kW |
| Torque | 13.5 Nm | 14.4 Nm | 14.4 Nm |
| Gears | manual clutch | manual clutch | 6-speed, Assist and Slipper |
| Claimed economy | 2.59 L/100 km | 2.91 L/100 km | 2.07 L/100 km |
| Weight | 124 kg | 139 kg | 123 kg wet |
| List price | 46,360,000 VND | 72,290,000 VND | 55,200,000 VND |

The Exciter takes the performance-and-economy double: the most power and the best claimed figure of the three. The CBR150R counters with the 12-litre touring tank and full fairing; the Winner R is the value entry into the class.

## The unique models

Honda's Super Cub C125 and CT125 have no direct Yamaha rival in the verified data. Yamaha has no heritage or trail underbone on its current Vietnam pages.
