The moments after a crash follow a legal script in Vietnam, and every step of it is a duty, not a suggestion. Article 80 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (No. 36/2024/QH15) lists what a person involved in a road accident must do, and the same law names fleeing the scene among the prohibited acts.

## The five duties

- Stop immediately. Do not move the bike out of the way first; the position of vehicles is evidence.
- Warn of danger. Hazard lights, a warning triangle if available, or a bystander directing traffic around the scene.
- Preserve the scene as it is.
- Help the injured. Summoning emergency help is not optional; a person who can help and deliberately does not commits a prohibited act.
- Notify the authorities — call the police (113) for the accident, and emergency services (115) if anyone is hurt.

For visitors, the practical additions: keep the rental shop's number at hand, keep documents ready, and photograph positions and damage before anything is moved.

## What the law does with failures

A violation of traffic rules that causes an accident already carries 10,000,000–14,000,000 VND for motorbike riders under Article 7(10) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP, alongside licence-point deduction and suspension. Leaving the scene to avoid responsibility is listed among the law's prohibited acts and is treated as far graver than the accident itself; it can convert an administrative fine into a criminal case.

## The insurance connection

The compulsory insurance certificate the rider carries is what the compensation process runs on after a crash — another reason it belongs in your pocket rather than a drawer. After a rental accident, the shop and the insurer need the police record, which is one more reason the police call comes before anything else.

## For riders in Hanoi

Accidents in dense traffic are usually low-speed and negotiated, but the legal duties do not scale down with the damage. Stop, warn, preserve, help, report. Everything else — repairs, deposits, negotiation — happens after those five are done.
