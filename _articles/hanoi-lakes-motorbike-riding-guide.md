Hanoi is a city built on water — the Red River and a necklace of lakes that define its neighbourhoods. West Lake gets the famous loop, but the smaller lakes are the everyday scenery of riding here, and a day spent linking them is the best low-effort tour of the city on a bike.

## The lake ladder

**Hoan Kiem** is the pivot: small, central, walking-street-fringed on weekends, and the natural start and end of any lake day. **Bay Mau** and the Thong Nhat park lakes beside it, just south, give the first green pause — a park loop where the riding is nominal and the shade is the point. **Giang Vo**, further west, is the sports lake: exhibition halls and stadium edges around a body of water that locals use as a running track. The **Thu Le zoo lake** sits in parkland near the west of Ba Dinh. And in the south, the **Linh Dam lake** loop and the lake chain around Hoang Mai — with Yen So at the far end — turn the tour suburban and calm.

## The riding

A full lake day is 25–35 km of mostly flat, mostly pleasant urban riding. The character changes with each water: Hoan Kiem is pedestrian-dense, Bay Mau is park-slow, Giang Vo is wide-avenue, Linh Dam is apartment-campus calm, and the southern lakes are the closest thing to open-sky riding inside the city. Ride them in the morning light — east to west, sun behind you — and keep the pace unhurried throughout; this is a scenery day, not a mileage day.

## Stops worth making

Each lake has one stop that earns its time: the Ngoc Son temple island at Hoan Kiem; the Tran Quoc pagoda on its West Lake peninsula — the oldest pagoda in the city; the cafe strips facing the water at Giang Vo and Thu Le; and the picnic lawns of the southern parks on weekends. Parking follows the park model everywhere: attended lots at the entrances, a few thousand dong, no drama.

## Timing and weather

The lakes are a dry-season tour best done in the morning; after rain the lakeside paths and some access lanes get slick, and the pleasure drops fast. The seasons guide covers which months reward lake days most.
