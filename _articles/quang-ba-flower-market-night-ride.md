Quang Ba flower market is Hanoi's wholesale flower trade in the raw: a covered market on the West Lake dyke where the city's florists buy by the bundle between midnight and dawn, and where a visitor with a motorbike can ride into a wall of scent and colour that most tourists never see. It is the best night ride inside the city boundary.

## When to go

The market runs in two phases. Wholesale runs from about 20:00 to 06:00 — the real flower trade, with motorbikes loaded like hay carts, trolleys, shouting and bargaining in full swing. Retail follows from about 06:00 to noon, c
almer, when locals buy single stems and small bunches. For the spectacle, arrive between 21:00 and midnight, or come at dawn to see wholesale fade into retail — the best single hour is roughly 05:30 to 06:30.

## The route

The market sits on the Nghi Tam dyke just off the northeast corner of West Lake, about 5 km from the centre. From Hoan Kiem, ride northwest to West Lake and follow the dyke from the Hong Ha street end. The route is streetlit the whole way, and the lake section is the nicest night riding in the city — but it is still night riding: run full lights, watch for unlit bikes carrying flowers that stick out further than they look, and keep the speed down.

## On arrival

Parking is market-chaos by design: the dyke around the entrance fills with delivery motorbikes and tuk-tuk carts. There are attendants; use them rather than improvising. Walk the market rather than trying to ride through it — the aisles are a working warehouse, not a promenade. Buy a small bunch and you will pay retail; buy armfuls and you will get near-wholesale prices. Cash, small notes, works best.

## Riding home with flowers

Flowers on a motorbike are a Hanoi art form: blooms forward, stems back, one arm steadying the bundle. For a visitor, a single wrapped bunch fits between your feet on the floorboard or in a backpack with the heads up. Do not strap long stems where they block your arms or lights. If you are buying seriously, bring a rear rack and bungee cords, and give the blooms the whole ride home gently.

## Combining it

The flower market pairs perfectly with the West Lake loop: ride the loop at sunset, hit the market as the wholesale hour builds, and be home by one in the morning — one of Hanoi's best short nights out on a bike.
