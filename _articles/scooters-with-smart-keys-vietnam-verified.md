Smart keys have moved from flagship exclusivity to mainstream scooters in Vietnam. Our smart-key guide explains how the systems work; this article is the companion - a verified list of which models actually offer one, based only on manufacturer product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Honda models with smart keys (verified on Honda VN pages)

- Honda SH160i/125i - smart key standard, with vehicle locate and alarm function.
- Honda Sh mode 125 - smart key with a key-left-on warning light, a first for Honda Vietnam.
- Honda Vario 125 - smart key with remote locate.
- Honda Vario 160 - smart key on the FOB remote with alarm function.
- Honda Air Blade 160 - smart key included.
- Honda Vision - smart-key versions available within the range (check the specific version).

## Yamaha models with smart keys (verified on Yamaha VN pages)

- Yamaha NVX 155 - SmartKey with steering lock, seat and fuel-cap release, plus vehicle locate.
- Yamaha Latte 125 - SmartKey with steering lock and vehicle locate.
- Yamaha Grande - smart key plus phone charging.
- Yamaha FreeGo 125 - smart key on the reviewed version.

## What this means when renting

A smart key changes your responsibilities slightly: you carry a fob rather than a metal key, the bike locks itself when you walk away, and losing the fob is more expensive than losing a key. On rentals, always confirm at pickup how the system works - how to start, how to open the seat, what the warning sounds mean - and keep the fob somewhere secure. Our guide to keeping a rental motorbike secure in Hanoi applies doubly to smart-key bikes.

## Things smart keys do not do

No manufacturer system prevents theft by itself, and none replaces a disc lock or careful parking - see our article on effective steering-lock use and the guide to overnight security. The Honda pages we reviewed explicitly describe smart keys as convenience plus alarm features, not anti-theft guarantees.

## Bottom line

Smart keys are now normal equipment from roughly 40 million VND upward, and available below that in some ranges. Availability by version varies - confirm the exact version before you buy, and contact us to confirm current availability of any rental model.
