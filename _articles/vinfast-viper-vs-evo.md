The Viper and the Evo are two answers to the same brief — a licence-free electric scooter on VinFast's two-pack swap platform — and the difference between them is a clean choice between a technology package and maximum claimed range. All figures are official launch data.

## Head to head

| | Viper | Evo |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | 3,000 W in-hub | 2,450 W in-hub |
| Top speed | up to 70 km/h | up to 70 km/h |
| Battery | 2 × 1.5 kWh removable LFP | 2 × 1.5 kWh removable LFP |
| Claimed range | up to 156 km (two packs) | up to 165 km (two packs) |
| Tech package | Smart Key, vehicle tracking, remote find, remote on/off anti-theft, LED projector headlights, dual rear shocks with auxiliary oil reservoirs | (not itemised in launch data) |
| Licence | moped class, 16+ | moped class, 16+ |

## What the numbers say

Both machines reach the same 70 km/h ceiling and share the swap ecosystem (9,000 VND per battery per swap; rental 175,000–300,000 VND monthly), so neither is a faster or more legal choice. The Viper buys its 3,000 W with 9 km less claimed range — and spends the difference on the feature list: the connected security suite (tracking, remote on/off) is the standout, genuinely useful for a machine whose packs are valuable, and the LED projector headlights are a real night-riding upgrade.

The Evo counters with the platform's best claimed range — up to 165 km — which in swap terms means fewer cabinet visits per week for the same commute.

## Who should pick which

- **Viper**: riders who park on the street, value the anti-theft tracking, ride at night, or simply want the newest connected features. The Viper review covers the package in detail.
- **Evo**: riders whose week is measured in kilometres — longer commutes, delivery-style duty, fewer swaps. The Evo review covers the efficiency case.
