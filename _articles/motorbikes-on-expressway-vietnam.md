No — and engine size does not buy an exception. Vietnamese expressways are closed to motorbikes, mopeds and similar two-wheelers of every class, under the expre
ssway rules that took effect with the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (No. 36/2024/QH15). A class A rider on a litre bike is exactly as banned as a moped.

## The rule

The expressway provisions list the participants prohibited from entering: pedestrians, animal-drawn carts, bicycles and motorised pedal bikes, mopeds, and motorbikes — together with any vehicle whose design speed is below the expressway minimum. The prohibition is structural: expressways assume closed carriageways, minimum speeds and full-width vehicles; two-wheelers fail all three assumptions.

## The penalty for entering

Riding a motorbike onto an expressway is a violation under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP in the 4,000,000–6,000,000 VND band (Article 7(7)(b)), with a six-point licence deduction attached (Article 7(13)(c)) and the bike subject to being held on the spot (Article 48). The exits are signposted, but so are the entrances — "no motorbikes" boards stand at every ramp, and riders who follow car GPS onto a ramp have usually missed them.

## When it becomes criminal

An expressway entry that causes an accident lands in the 10,000,000–14,000,000 VND band for violations causing crashes, with the suspension and point consequences scaling accordingly — and where injury results, criminal liability can follow. The traffic closing speed on an expressway is the whole reason: a motorbike hit at 100 km/h by a car at 100 km/h does not produce an administrative case.

## Route planning for riders

For motorbike travel between Hanoi and other provinces, the riding routes are the national highways and provincial roads — QL1A, QL32, the Hòa Bình direction roads and their peers. Expressway-adjacent destinations are reached by exiting the mapped car route and following the parallel old highway, which every navigation app offers as a motorbike option. When renting, plan trips on motorbike-legal roads from the start: no rental agreement makes an expressway ramp legal.
