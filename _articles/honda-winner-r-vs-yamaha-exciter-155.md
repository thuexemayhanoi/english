Vietnam's two manual-clutch sport underbones, head to head on manufacturer-published figures only (Honda Vietnam and Yamaha Vietnam pages, retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines

Exciter 155 VVA: 155 cc liquid-cooled four-valve SOHC with VVA and fuel injection - 13.2 kW (17.9 PS) at 9,500 rpm, 14.4 Nm at 8,000 rpm. Winner R: 149.2 cc liquid-cooled single - 11.5 kW at 9,000 rpm, 13.5 Nm at 7,000 rpm. The Exciter is clearly the stronger engine, and Yamaha publishes the same 14.4 Nm peak torque as Honda's much larger CBR150R.

## Gearbox and clutch

Exciter: six-speed with an Assist and Slipper clutch - lighter lever effort in traffic, and the slipper function prevents rear-wheel hop on hard downshifts. Winner R: Honda's sport underbone layout with no published slipper clutch. Both are electric start.

## Economy, weight and brakes

Exciter: 2.07 L/100 km, 5.4 L tank, 123 kg wet, two-piston front disc plus rear disc, tubeless 90/80-17 and 120/70-17 tyres, ABS on the limited ABS versions. Winner: 2.59 L/100 km (UDC method) from 4.5 L, 124 kg, wider stance on a 1,277 mm wheelbase, 90/80-17 and 120/70-17 tyres.

## Price

Winner R: 46,360,000 VND. Exciter 155 VVA: from 55,200,000 VND for the limited ABS version shown on the retrieved page (both list prices, retrieved 23 September 2026; other Exciter versions differ).

## Verdict

Exciter for performance, the six-speed box, the slipper clutch and ABS availability; Winner R for value at roughly 9 million VND less. Both sit in the A1 licence bracket - our licence guide covers what that means. Contact us to confirm current availability.
