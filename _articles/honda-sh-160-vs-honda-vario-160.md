The SH160i is Vietnam's status scooter; the Vario 160 is Honda's value sport 160 - and they share the same 156.9 cc liquid-cooled eSP+ engine family. That makes this the most interesting badge-versus-value question in the range. Figures from Honda Vietnam's official pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine

SH160i: 12.4 kW at 8,500 rpm, 14.8 Nm at 6,500 rpm. Vario 160: 11.3 kW at 8,500 rpm, 14.1 Nm at 6,500 rpm. The SH is tuned for more, but the Vario's figures are genuinely close - the gap in performance is far smaller than the gap in price.

## Braking and safety

The SH160i has ABS on both wheels plus HSTC traction control on its Sport, Special and Premium versions - top safety hardware, explained in our HSTC guide. The Vario 160 has front-wheel ABS. Both are covered in our ABS availability guide; the SH is the safety-hardware winner.

## Weight, wheels, comfort

The Vario is lighter at 118 kg versus the SH's 134 kg, with a lower 778 mm seat versus 799 mm - easier for smaller riders and tight traffic. The SH counters with 16-inch wheels front and rear (100/80-16, 120/80-16) - big-wheel stability and presence, the trait behind its image, covered in our 16-inch wheel guide.

## Storage and price

The SH's 28-litre bay beats the Vario's 17.9, though the Vario's flat floor carries what the SH cannot. The SH160i Sport lists at 104,490,000 VND; the Vario 160 The thao at 56,690,000 VND. Nearly 48 million dong buys the badge, wheels, dual ABS and HSTC.

## Verdict

Buy the SH for status, stability and safety hardware. Buy the Vario for 90 percent of the engine at half the price. Neither is a price-published Nguyen Tu rental model - contact us to confirm current availability.
