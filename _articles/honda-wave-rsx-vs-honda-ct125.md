Same badge family, different planets: the Wave RSX is Honda's economy-commuter 110, the CT125 a trail-tuned Cub with the biggest tank and highest clearance in this comparison set. Verified Honda VN specifications below (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines

Wave RSX: 109.2 cc air-cooled single with PGM-FI injection, 6.46 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.70 Nm at 6,000 rpm, four-speed rotary. CT125: 123.9 cc air-cooled single, 6.76 kW at 6,250 rpm, 10.9 Nm at 4,750 rpm, four-speed. The CT125 makes its peak torque well over a thousand rpm lower — built to lug along rough tracks rather than cruise efficiently.

## Clearance and range

CT125: 165 mm ground clearance, 800 mm seat, 5.4-litre tank, 1.61 L/100 km claimed — the biggest fuel range of the 125-class Hondas here. Wave RSX: 135 mm clearance, 760 mm seat, 4.0 litres, and the better claimed economy of the two at 1.56 L/100 km. On broken surfaces the CT125 is in its element; on tarmac the RSX spends less per kilometre.

## Price and purpose

Wave RSX: 25,664,727 VND, plus a U-box that fits a half helmet and a 4-in-1 key. CT125: 85,997,455 VND.
