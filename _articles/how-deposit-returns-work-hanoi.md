The deposit is the part of the rental people worry about most, and the return is where that worry resolves. Here is the actual mechanism.

## The standard timeline

The deposit (typically 2,000,000-5,000,000 VND) is held in cash for the rental period. At return, inside opening hours (09:00-21:00), the shop walks the bike against its handover state, and if the bike matches, the cash comes back on the spot - usually within minutes of the walk-around. Count it before you leave the counter.

## What the walk-around checks

The same items your handover photos recorded: panels and scratches, mirrors, lights, brakes, tyres. With a timestamped photo set, this is a comparison, not a debate. Fair wear is expected and never charged: dust, kilometres, a lightly scuffed lever from a stationary drop is a conversation, a bent frame is a settlement.

## What gets deducted

- Damage beyond fair wear, at an agreed repair price - not a list price from the shop's imagination. Ask for the repair quote.
- A missing or lost key (the lost-key article covers this).
- Late hours: 20,000 VND per hour, with a full additional rental day (typically 150,000-200,000 VND) possibly charged beyond six hours late.
- Fuel shortfalls against the agreed level.
- Consumables that were yours under the agreement - inner tubes and tyres can be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation.

## What does not touch the deposit

The shop's maintenance: oil, chain, tyre pressure, inspection. A mechanical failure through normal use is not a deduction - it is the owner's bike to fix.

## If there is a dispute

The record is the contract and the handover photos. This is why both exist. Shops that photograph at handover (ask - good ones do) settle returns in under a minute; renters who photograph protect themselves from the shops that do not.

## The rule that prevents it all

Take the photos. Every panel, both sides, at handover, sent to the shop's chat. The deposit article covers the holding side; this covers the coming-back side. Done right, the return is the quietest part of the rental.
