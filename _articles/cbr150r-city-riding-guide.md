The CBR150R is Vietnam's entry-level full-fairing sport bike, and a small number of riders in Hanoi use one as their only bike. It works — with trade-offs you should understand before committing. All figures are from Honda Vietnam's official page (recorded in our model database, retrieved 2026-09-23).

## The machine

A 149.2 cc liquid-cooled DOHC single, 12.6 kW at 9,000 rpm and 14.4 Nm at 7,000 rpm, six-speed manual clutch, 12-litre tank, claimed 2.91 L/100 km, 788 mm seat, 139 kg kerb, 100/80-17 front and 130/70-17 rear tyres, listed at 72,290,000 VND.

## What city traffic does to it

- **Heat**: a liquid-cooled engine with a radiator handles stop-and-go better than an air-cooled one, but at long traffic-light stops the fan runs and the fairing holds warm air around your legs. That is normal; watch the temperature light, not your shins.
- **Clutch work**: the six-speed box with a manual clutch in Hanoi-density traffic means constant left-hand work. Riders who commute daily on one develop exactly the friction-zone discipline our traffic clutch guide teaches — without it, every Old Quarter trip is a workout.
- **Fuel**: the claimed 2.91 L/100 km is roughly double a commuter underbone's figure, and real-world city economy will be higher still. Budget accordingly; the 12-litre tank mostly offsets this by making stops less frequent.
- **Space**: it is a wide-ish bike by underbone standards (1,983 mm long, 700 mm wide) and the fairing marks easily in tight parking rows. Invest in good parking etiquette and a cover.

## What it does well

Full fairing means genuine weather and wind protection at ring-road speed, and the 12.6 kW engine makes overtaking outside the city a non-event. The 1312 mm wheelbase and 151 mm clearance make it far more planted than any underbone at speed, and the six-speed gearbox is a pleasure when the road opens.

## Honest verdict

As a weekend-and-evening bike kept in Hanoi, the CBR150R is excellent. As a daily commuter in the densest districts, it asks for more clutch discipline, more parking care and more fuel budget than a semi-auto underbone, and delivers more speed, stability and presence in exchange.
