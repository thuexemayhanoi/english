Looking for a motorbike rental app in Hanoi? There is no booking app and no online payment; a WhatsApp or Zalo message is the online route. Booking is the easy part of [renting in Hanoi]({{ '/topics/rental/' | relative_url }}). There are three routes and they all end the same way: you, a bike, a deposit, and a contract.

## No app, no online payment: which route to use

Nothing about booking with Hanoi Motorbike Rental Nguyen Tu happens inside an app. It is a conversation, by message, by phone or in person at the shop, and the record of what was agreed is whatever you keep from it. If something online offers to book or take payment for the shop through an app, check its number or account details against the [published contact details]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) and with the shop itself before you pay anything.

For a one-off day rental, walking in is fine. For anything longer than a few days, message ahead and lock the model and dates in writing. Beyond that rule of thumb, your situation decides:

| Your situation | Route | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Dates fixed, still abroad | WhatsApp or Zalo message | Time to ask everything before you fly |
| In Hanoi, want a bike today | Phone call, then a message | A fast answer, then a written record |
| Can reach the shop, want to see the bike | Walk-in | You judge the actual bike before committing |
| Hotel far from the shop, or a tight schedule | Message, asking about delivery | Delivery may be available |

## Messaging first: WhatsApp or Zalo

The standard route. +84 942 467 674 reaches Nguyen Tu on both WhatsApp and Zalo, so use whichever app you already have. A booking conversation answers four things: the model you want, your dates, the price, and availability. Model stock genuinely changes week to week - a shop that has a Vision free today may not next Friday, so confirming dates in writing matters. Ask for photos of the actual bike, so you know what you are agreeing to.

Short, plain sentences are the easiest to answer and the easiest to check later. Write each date in words, such as 3 April, because 03/04 reads differently in different countries, and put one question on each line. Keep the whole booking in one thread, so the dates, price and deposit sit together and any later change is added to the same conversation.

## Phone

Calling the same number works too. A call is fastest for a same-day rental; a message is better for anything in the future because it leaves a written record of the agreed price and dates. If you settle anything by phone, send a message straight afterwards that repeats the model, dates, price and deposit, and ask for a reply that confirms or corrects it.

## Walk-in

Hanoi rentals sit all over the city - Nguyen Tu is at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De, Long Bien, open 09:00-21:00 daily ([contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }})). Walking in means seeing the actual bike before you commit; ask whether you can ride it around the block, and whether another bike is available if the seat does not fit you or the brakes feel soft. The only cost of walking in is time, and during peak tourist season the bike you wanted may be gone. A short message or call before you set out is a cheap way to avoid a wasted trip.

## What to put in your first message

Cover these points and a single reply can answer most of them.

- **Dates and times.** Pickup and return, each with a time, and whether the dates are fixed or may move. Rentals can run by the day, the week or the month, and longer terms can be discussed, so say how long you have in mind.
- **Bike type.** Scooter, semi-automatic or manual, or "not sure". Name a model only if you have a preference. If you cannot choose, [automatic, semi-automatic or manual]({{ '/articles/automatic-vs-manual-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) compares the three.
- **Pickup or delivery.** Say which you prefer. For delivery, give the hotel or area and ask what it would cost for that address. [Shop pickup versus delivery]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out the trade-offs.
- **Licence.** Name the licence you hold, the country that issued it and whether you carry an international driving permit, then ask what to bring. For who can rent and what a foreign licence needs, read [can foreigners rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/can-foreigners-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).
- **Riders and route.** The number of riders, each rider's experience, and whether you will stay in Hanoi or ride further. Support is primarily for Hanoi and local use, so say at the start if your plans go beyond the city. New to motorbikes? Read [first time renting a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/first-time-renting-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) before you commit.
- **Your questions.** Is the bike free on those dates, what is the total price and the deposit, how and when do you pay, and what should you bring?

### A message you can copy

> Hello, I would like to rent a motorbike in Hanoi. I would collect on [date, time] and return on [date, time]. I prefer a [scooter / semi-automatic / manual / not sure], and a [model] if one is available. Collection at the shop or delivery to [hotel and area]? I hold a [country, licence class] licence [with / without] an international driving permit. There will be [number] riders, [experience], riding [in Hanoi only / this route]. Could you confirm availability for these dates, the total price for the period, the deposit, how and when to pay, and what to bring?

Read the reply for what it leaves out. If it names a price but no deposit figure, or a bike but not your dates, ask about the gap in the same thread instead of assuming.

## Comparing quotes honestly

Two quotes can be compared only when they describe the same bike, the same dates and the same terms. A low daily rate attached to a large deposit, vague return terms or extras charged on top is not a lower price, only a lower first number.

### Line up the same things

| What to compare | What to ask | Why it changes the total |
|---|---|---|
| Bike | The model or category, and whether that bike is free on your dates | A different model is a different product, so price like against like |
| Period | The total for your exact dates, with extras listed separately | Rates for a day, a week and a month can differ, and only a total can be compared |
| Deposit | The amount for your bike and how it comes back | It is money tied up for the rental, so it matters as much as the rate |
| Extras | What comes with the bike and what is charged on top | Delivery, extra days and late return can each add to the cost |
| Payment | Which methods are accepted and when each payment is due | A method you cannot use, or a payment due early, changes your plans |

## What to settle in writing before you arrive

- The exact model and its [rate for your period]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) (daily, weekly, monthly).
- The [deposit amount]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and how it is held and returned.
- Whether you pick up at the shop or want [delivery to your address]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) - delivery in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement, agreed case by case.
- The return date, time and fuel convention.

Then add the points that decide how the day itself goes:

- **Whether the bike is being held for you.** A reply that says a model is available is not the same as a bike set aside for your dates, so ask whether it is held and, if so, until what time on the first day.
- **How and when you pay.** Cash, bank transfer and QR payment are accepted; ask which payments fall due on arrival and whether anything is due before you travel.
- **What to bring.** Ask for the exact list instead of guessing; [what documents you need to rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/documents-needed-rent-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out the paperwork side.
- **What comes with the bike.** A helmet is the obvious item to ask about; ask what else is included and what is not.
- **Insurance.** Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance; customers arrange their own, so sort out your cover before you ride.
- **If something goes wrong.** Ask who to contact if the bike has a problem, and who pays for consumables such as inner tubes and tyres, which may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation.

### Records to keep

Save these where you can open them without a data connection:

- Ask the shop to confirm the booking in writing, then keep a screenshot of that reply, showing the model or category, the dates and times, the rate, the deposit amount, the pickup or delivery arrangement and the return time.
- Any later change to those points, confirmed in the same thread rather than by phone alone.
- A receipt, transfer confirmation or QR payment confirmation for each payment; for cash, ask for a written or messaged acknowledgement.
- The shop's address and number, saved in your phone.

## The day you arrive

For the whole rental in order, from choosing a bike to returning it, read [how to rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/how-to-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), which also covers paying and inspecting the bike. The booking side of the day runs like this:

1. Arrive during opening hours, and message on your way so the shop knows you are coming.
2. Bring everything you were told to bring, licence and payment included.
3. Read your message thread against what is in front of you: the model, the rate and the deposit amount. Settle any difference before money changes hands.
4. Before you leave, check the return date, time and place against the thread, and ask who to message if you are running late.

## If your plans change

This site publishes no cancellation policy for the shop, so none is quoted here and none should be assumed. Ask before you pay anything, and keep the answer in the thread:

- **Changing dates before pickup.** Is it possible, and do the price or the bike's availability change?
- **Cancelling before pickup.** Is any payment or deposit kept, and how would money be returned and confirmed?
- **Arriving later than planned.** Until what time can the bike be collected, and does the booking still stand? Published hours are 09:00-21:00, so if you will arrive outside them, ask before you travel and do not assume a handover; [renting a motorbike when you arrive at Noi Bai Airport]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) covers the first day after a flight.
- **Keeping the bike longer.** Ask how to extend and how extra days are priced. A late return is charged at 20,000 VND per hour, and a return more than six hours late may be charged a full additional rental day, typically 150,000-200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle; [what happens if you return a rental motorbike late]({{ '/articles/late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) gives the detail.
- **Returning early.** There is no automatic refund entitlement for unused time, so book the period you expect to use; [can you return a rental motorbike early]({{ '/articles/early-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) explains the position.

## Quick answers before you book

### Is there an app to book a motorbike rental in Hanoi?

No. Bookings are made by WhatsApp or Zalo message, by phone or in person at the shop, and there is no booking app. Ride-hailing apps are a different service: they send a driver to take you somewhere rather than rent you a bike.

### Can I book and pay online?

The shop has no website checkout or booking app. The accepted payment methods are cash, bank transfer and QR payment, so ask which one the shop wants for each payment and when it is due.

### Do I need WhatsApp or Zalo to book?

No. The same number takes phone calls, and you can walk in during opening hours.

### Can I book before I land in Hanoi?

Yes, you can message before you travel. Send your dates and the points from the first-message list, ask in the same thread whether the bike is held for you, and settle the details in writing. If you are landing at the airport, [arriving at Noi Bai Airport]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) covers the first day.
