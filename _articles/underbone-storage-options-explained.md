Underbones are workhorses, but the manufacturers publish surprisingly little built-in storage. Here is exactly what Honda's pages promise (retrieved 23 September 2026) - and what riders actually do about the rest.

## The verified built-in storage

Wave RSX: a U-box that holds one half helmet, with a 4-in-1 key system - the only storage-carrying commuter underbone in Honda's published range. CT125: rack styling (published as styling, with no litre or kg rating stated). Everyone else - Wave Alpha, Blade, Future 125, Super Cub, Winner R, CBR150R - publishes no storage at all. Yamaha publishes none for the Sirius or Exciter either.

## The aftermarket reality

Because the factories under-equip the class, Vietnam built an ecosystem: front baskets and racks (our basket guide covers scooter-fit equivalents), top boxes on rear racks, seat hooks and cargo nets - our cargo-net and top-box guides cover safe loading, which matters more than capacity. Delivery riders build their entire businesses on this aftermarket - our delivery guide covers the working setups.

## The safety line

Underbone frames are light and the bikes narrow; heavy rear loads change handling measurably, and our luggage-carrying guide covers the low-and-central rule. Never bungee anything that can reach the rear wheel or exhaust.
