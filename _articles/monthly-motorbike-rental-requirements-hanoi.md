The baseline requirements for a monthly rental are the same as for any rental; what changes is that a long-term arrangement deserves extra confirmation on a few points. Start from the baseline in [Motorbike Rental Requirements in Hanoi: Documents, Licence, Age]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), then add the monthly layer below.

This page does not restate that list. The licence classes, ages, International Driving Permit rules and licence-free moped class stay with the requirements guide, and [what documents you need to rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/documents-needed-rent-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) keeps the identity document for the contract apart from the papers a rider carries. The monthly motorbike rental requirements below cover only what stretches over a month: the dates on your own documents, the deposit, the payments, a contact, a place for the bike to sleep and the care of the bike.

## The baseline

- A valid licence for the class of bike: a Vietnamese motorcycle licence, or your home licence with a valid IDP under the 1968 Vienna Convention (riding without one carries fines — see [Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi Without a Licence: The Real Risks]({{ '/articles/renting-a-motorbike-hanoi-without-licence/' | relative_url }}))
- Identification for the agreement — never surrender your passport to be held; shops should not keep identity documents ([Do Hanoi Motorbike Rentals Keep Your Passport? Deposit Practices Explained]({{ '/articles/do-rentals-keep-your-passport-hanoi/' | relative_url }}))
- The deposit: typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the bike ([Deposits for Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-monthly-hanoi/' | relative_url }}))
- Payment: cash, bank transfer or QR

## The monthly layer

Because the rental runs weeks, confirm these at booking rather than assuming:

- The exact monthly rate for your model and dates ([Motorbike Rental Monthly Prices in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }})) — and where no monthly rate is published, ask directly
- The maintenance arrangement during the term: what the shop includes (oil, chain, tyre pressure, inspection) and how visits are scheduled — see [Maintenance During a Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})
- Consumables: tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation — get the answer in writing
- The return standard: when the deposit comes back and against what condition checks — see [How Deposit Returns Work at Hanoi Motorbike Rentals]({{ '/articles/how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi/' | relative_url }})
- Extension terms, in case your stay grows: [Extending or Renewing a Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/extending-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})

## Licence validity across the term

A licence valid today can expire mid-term, and an expired licence is fined like an invalid one ([Fine for an Expired Motorbike Licence in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/expired-licence-fine-vietnam/' | relative_url }})). That guide gives the amounts; this page quotes none, so check any figure there against the fines decree text in force. Before starting a monthly rental, check that your licence or IDP outlasts the rental period — and if it does not, plan the renewal or exchange.

Expiry mid-rental and the long-term licence checklist are covered in [When Your Licence or Visa Expires Mid-Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/licence-expiry-during-long-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [The Long-Term Rider's Licence Checklist for Hanoi]({{ '/articles/long-term-rider-licence-checklist-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

### Where your last riding day falls

A month changes the arithmetic. For a two-day rental a document only has to be valid on those two days; for a monthly rental it has to be valid on the last day too, and the first document to run out sets that day. Under Article 57(7)(a) of Law 36/2024/QH15 (consolidated text 55/VBHN-VPQH, as amended by Law 118/2025/QH15) a licence whose term has ended has no validity, so a rental that runs on beyond that date cannot be ridden to the end on that licence.

Each kind of licence ends on its own date, and the requirements guide and the licence checklist linked above set out which: an IDP with a home licence, a Vietnamese licence exchanged for a foreigner, and a Vietnamese licence from a test. This page asks only that you find the earliest of those dates and set it beside the last day of the rental. One caution belongs here: an IDP that Vietnam itself issues under the 1968 Convention has no validity inside the country (Article 30(1) of Circular 108/2026/TT-BCA, in force from 1 July 2026), so it cannot stand in for a licence on a Hanoi rental. Where a date lands inside the term, renew or exchange the document before it, or tell the shop early and agree what happens to the rental. The published terms do not say how the shop handles that, so ask, and do not assume an answer.

### Four renters, four sets of dates

The table sets four long-stay situations against the dates to check before a monthly booking. It reflects the articles cited above, not what any shop requires.

| Renter | Check before booking | Read first |
|---|---|---|
| A tourist, home licence and 1968 IDP | Permit issue date (three years at most), licence expiry, the category on both, and the earlier date against your return day | The long-term licence checklist |
| A student on a semester | Visa or card date as well as licence dates; for an exchange, 90 days or more left on it | The mid-rental expiry guide |
| A worker with a temporary residence card | Card end date; IDP pair or exchange (Article 24(1)(a) asks for 90 days or more); the term a new licence would carry | The long-term licence checklist |
| A long-stay resident, exchanged licence | Printed term against the rental's end, and the visa or card behind it; a Vietnamese-issued IDP does not count | The mid-rental expiry guide |

Imagine a student with an exchanged licence whose residence card ends before the semester does. A rental agreed through the end of term would outlast the licence, because the licence follows the card, so the date to settle is the card's, before the rental starts and not after.

## The rest of the monthly layer, point by point

The list above is short on purpose. These are the points in it that need more than a line, plus two it leaves out: a contact the shop can reach and a place for the bike to sleep. Where the published facts run out, read the sentence as something to ask the shop, not as a description of how it works.

### Holding the deposit

The published deposit is typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions. No separate figure for a longer rental is published, so the amount for your bike and your dates is a question. What a longer rental adds is the length of the hold: ask what the deposit is held against, in what form it is taken, how its receipt is acknowledged, and how and when it is returned. The timing and form of the return are not published, so pin "the end of the term" to a date and an hour.

### When each payment falls due

The published payment methods are cash, bank transfer and QR payment. What a month leaves open is the timetable: whether the rent is due when the bike is handed over, ahead of that or later, whether it can be paid in more than one part, whether the deposit follows the same timetable, and what proof you receive each time. Whether a late payment carries any charge is not published either. [Paying for a Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/paying-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) takes each method in turn, so the requirement here is short: have the answers, and every amount in VND, in writing before the first payment.

### A contact and an address the shop can reach

The shop is at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De, Long Bien (open 09:00–21:00 daily); +84 942 467 674 reaches it by phone, WhatsApp and Zalo. The published facts name no condition about your own number or address, so treat both as things to offer and to ask about: a number that works while you are in Hanoi, where you are staying if the shop wants to know, and a message in the same thread if either changes.

Support is primarily for Hanoi and local use and no reply time is published, so a plan to ride beyond Hanoi, or a problem that arises outside opening hours, is a topic for the start of the term, not for the day it happens.

### Where the bike sleeps

For a day, parking is decided stop by stop. For a month it is a place you need every night. Nothing is published about where a rental bike stays overnight, so settle it before collecting the bike: whether it can be kept inside the building or compound where you will stay and who decides that, whether there is a charge and when the space is open, and how it will be locked and which key you carry. Then tell the shop where the bike will stay. [Starting a Monthly Motorbike Rental: The First Week]({{ '/articles/starting-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) turns this into a dry run with the real bike once you have it.

### Agreeing maintenance and the distance check

Nguyen Tu's published position is that maintenance may include an oil change, chain adjustment and tension, tyre-pressure checks and normal inspection, and that tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation. Treat that list as things to ask about for your bike, since no schedule is published. The owner's practical guidance is periodic checks at around 1,500 km or more of use. That is not a manufacturer's interval, so ask which one the shop follows for your bike.

Distance, not the calendar, sets when a check falls due. Note the odometer reading at handover, count from it, and tell the shop when the total is getting close.

## A checklist before you book a month

### Your documents

- Write down the end date of every document you rely on (licence, IDP, visa or residence card) and put the earliest beside the last day of the rental.
- Ask which identity document the contract needs and what is recorded from it; the published facts do not say.
- Where a licence exchange may follow, check how many days remain on the visa or card first.

### The money

- Deposit for this bike: what it is held against, how it is taken and when it returns.
- Total due on the first day in VND, the dates it covers and the method for each payment.
- Monthly rate for your model and dates. Contact us to confirm current availability of the model you want.

### The bike and the contact

- Which maintenance items apply, who pays for tubes and tyres, and when the shop wants to see the bike.
- A number and an address the shop can reach, and where the bike will sleep.
- Your own motorbike insurance, running for the whole term: Nguyen Tu does not provide it.
- A longer stay is negotiable, so contact us to discuss it before the term ends.

Put the answers in one message to the shop and keep the reply. [Long-Term Motorbike Rental Agreements in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-agreement-long-term-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the terms worth recording over several months.

## Questions about a monthly booking

### Does a monthly rental need different documents from a daily one?

The requirements for the rider are the same whatever the length, and the published facts describe no separate document list for a monthly rental. What grows is the time each document has to stay valid: the earliest end date among your licence, any IDP and, for an exchanged licence, the visa or card behind it, has to fall after the last day of the rental.

### What if my licence ends partway through the rental?

Article 57(7)(a) of Law 36/2024/QH15 takes away the validity of a licence once its term has run out. How the shop would treat the agreement is not published, so tell it early and ask.

### Is the deposit different for a longer rental?

The published deposit is typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions, with no separate figure for a longer term. Ask what applies to your bike, and how and when it is returned.

### Can I keep the bike for longer than a month?

Longer-term rental is negotiable: contact us to discuss it. Before agreeing, check that every licence date, and the visa or card date behind an exchanged licence, reaches the new end date, because the earliest one limits your right to ride.

## The one-line version

Same requirements as any rental, plus four confirmations a month deserves: exact rate, maintenance arrangement, consumables responsibility, return standard. Add the dates on your own documents, a contact the shop can reach and a place for the bike to sleep.
