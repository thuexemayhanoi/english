The baseline requirements for a monthly rental are the same as for any rental; what changes is that a long-term arrangement deserves extra confirmation on a few points. Start from the baseline in motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi, then add the monthly layer below.

## The baseline

- A valid licence for the class of bike: a Vietnamese motorcycle licence, or your home licence with a valid IDP under the 1968 Vienna Convention (riding without one carries fines — see renting-a-motorbike-hanoi-without-licence)
- Identification for the agreement — never surrender your passport to be held; shops should not keep identity documents (do-rentals-keep-your-passport-hanoi)
- The deposit: typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the bike (motorbike-rental-deposit-monthly-hanoi)
- Payment: cash, bank transfer or QR

## The monthly layer

Because the rental runs weeks, confirm these at booking rather than assuming:

- The exact monthly rate for your model and dates (motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi) — and where no monthly rate is published, ask directly
- The maintenance arrangement during the term: what the shop includes (oil, chain, tyre pressure, inspection) and how visits are scheduled — see maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi
- Consumables: tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation — get the answer in writing
- The return standard: when the deposit comes back and against what condition checks — see how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi
- Extension terms, in case your stay grows: extending-motorbike-rental-hanoi

## Licence validity across the term

A licence valid today can expire mid-term, and an expired licence is fined like an invalid one (expired-licence-fine-vietnam). Before starting a monthly rental, check that your licence or IDP outlasts the rental period — and if it does not, plan the renewal or exchange. Expiry mid-rental and the long-term licence checklist are covered in licence-expiry-during-long-rental-hanoi and long-term-rider-licence-checklist-hanoi.

## Daily carry, unchanged

While riding, carry your licence and the rental paperwork; the full documents-to-carry picture is in documents-to-carry-motorbike-vietnam. Monthly renters get checked the same as everyone — and being a regular does not substitute for papers.

## The one-line version

Same requirements as any rental, plus four confirmations a month deserves: exact rate, maintenance arrangement, consumables responsibility, return standard.
