The Mekong Delta is the easiest motorbike touring in Vietnam: pancake-flat, endlessly green, criss-crossed by rivers that force the roads into loops and ferry hops, and hot all year. For riders arriving from the mountain north, the delta is the opposite kind of paradise — no passes, no fog, just narrow concrete lanes between orchards, bridges that lift to let boats through, and floating markets at dawn.

## Why the delta suits motorbikes perfectly

Independent route guides describe exactly this: take the byways off the highways and lose yourself in Vietnam's deep south; old-school ferry crossings remain part of the riding. The delta's road network was built for two wheels — cars squeeze, trucks stick to the big arteries, and the delta's real life happens on the lane-width roads between houses. Touring companies run two-day loops out of Ho Chi Minh City as a standard product, but the region rewards a slower, self-guided week.

## Classic routes and loops

**From Ho Chi Minh City.** Ride out through the suburban sprawl (the least pleasant hour), cross to the Ben Tre coconut-hamlet side, and stay on the small roads all the way to Can Tho — the delta's biggest city and the natural base for the Cai Rang floating market at sunrise. Two to three days there and back is the standard sampler; our Saigon rental guide covers getting the bike.

**The deep-delta loop.** Can Tho to Soc Trang, Tra Vinh and the Khmer-temple countryside, back through Vinh Long — flat, quiet, orchard-lined. Ferries still link what bridges have not yet replaced; our guide to ferries and trains with bikes covers the practicalities of rolling aboard.

**The far-west run.** Chau Doc on the Cambodian border (see our border guide for the crossing question), up the Bassac river road toward the mountains — the delta's only hills, where the flat ends.

## Riding notes

- **Weather:** the dry half of the year (roughly December-April) is the comfortable window; in the wet season afternoon downpours arrive like clockwork and the delta's mud lanes earn their reputation. Our nationwide seasons guide has the calendar.
- **Heat:** the delta is hotter than the north even in "cool" months — start at dawn, siesta midday.
- **Ferries:** cheap, frequent, and the best photos of the trip happen from the deck. Ride on slowly, park where the crew points, chocks down.
- **Bike choice:** any automatic 110-125cc scooter is ideal — this is scooter country. Our renting-vs-buying guide covers the math for a longer stay.
- **Navigation:** Google Maps routes you onto QL highways; drag the route onto the smaller parallels for the real delta.

## Fitting the delta into a Vietnam trip

Most travellers reach the delta at the end of a north-to-south ride (see our full route guide) or as a standalone Saigon chapter. Either way the pattern is the same: rent in Saigon, keep the loop compact, and let the ferries set the pace. And when the trip is over, the story you will tell is the 5am floating market, reached by bike.
