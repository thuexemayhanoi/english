The Wave RSX is the premium version of the machine Vietnam commutes on: the same practical shape as the Alpha with more equipment and stronger numbers. Honda Vietnam's page (recorded in our model database, retrieved 2026-09-23) lists 25,664,727 VND and the figures below.

## The machine

109.2 cc air-cooled single with PGM-FI, 6.46 kW at 7,500 rpm and 8.70 Nm at 6,000 rpm — the torquiest of the Wave family — four-speed rotary gearbox with centrifugal clutch, kick and electric start, 4.0-litre tank, claimed 1.56 L/100 km (the most fuel-efficient Wave), 760 mm seat, 98 kg, 70/90-17 front and 80/90-17 rear, plus a U-box that takes a half helmet and a 4-in-1 key.

## Commuting character

- **Torque where commuters need it**: peak torque at 6,000 rpm means loaded launches and bridge ramps pull cleanly. With a passenger or a week's shopping, the RSX is the Wave you want.
- **Economy**: the 1.56 L/100 km claim with a 4.0-litre tank gives a long theoretical commute range, and real-world economy stays impressive with sensible habits.
- **Storage**: the U-box half-helmet space plus the 4-in-1 key (one key for ignition, fuel cap, U-box and steering lock) remove the small daily frictions that matter five times a day.
- **Ergonomics**: the 760 mm seat suits most riders; the slightly firmer suspension than the Alpha copes better with Hanoi's patched tarmac and speed humps.

## Where it sits

Between the Alpha (basic, cheapest) and the Future 125 (roomier, more expensive), the RSX is the sweet spot for a single daily commuter who wants fuel injection, storage and the Wave's total parts network. Our comparison with the Alpha covers the value question; the Future guide covers the bigger-bike question.

## Daily rhythm

Riding it is the classic xe so pattern: first-gear launches, early upshifts, engine braking down ramps, both brakes for the erratic.
