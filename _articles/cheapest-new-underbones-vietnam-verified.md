Vietnam's underbone market starts remarkably low: the cheapest Honda listed on Honda VN's own pages costs 18,841,091 VND. All prices below are manufacturer list prices retrieved 23 September 2026 - they change over time and exclude registration and other on-road costs.

## The verified price ladder (110/125 class)

1. Honda Wave Alpha 110 - 18,841,091 VND 2. Honda Blade - 21,943,637 VND 3. Yamaha Sirius RC 110 - 21,993,000 VND 4. Honda Wave RSX - 25,664,727 VND 5. Honda Future 125 FI - from 30,622,909 VND (three versions)

For context, the premium manual-gearbox machines sit far above: Honda Winner R at 46,360,000 VND, Yamaha Exciter 155 VVA at 55,200,000 VND (limited ABS Monster version shown), Honda CBR150R at 72,290,000 VND, Honda CT125 at 85,997,455 VND and the Super Cub C125 at 88,353,813 VND.

## What each step up buys

From Wave Alpha to Blade or Sirius (about 3 million VND): styling and, on the Blade Sport, alloy wheels and a front disc. From there to the Wave RSX (about 4 million more): fuel injection, better economy and a U-box. From RSX to Future 125 (about 5 million): a bigger engine, the best economy figure in the class and a lower seat.

## The honest advice

For pure transport, the Wave Alpha is unbeatable value and it is the machine most of Hanoi runs on. It is also one of our price-published rental classes at 150,000 VND per day - rent one before you buy one, and contact us to confirm current availability of any model.
