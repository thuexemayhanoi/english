Vietnam draws a hard line between electric mopeds and electric motorcycles, and the line is measured in kilowatts and [design speed]({{ '/articles/why-electric-mopeds-capped-50kmh-vietnam/' | relative_url }}), not in looks. Article 34(1)(g) of Law 36/2024/QH15 defines the moped class as 4 kW or less and designed for no more than 50 km/h; a machine that exceeds either limit is a motorcycle.

## Licence and age

| Class | Power | Licence | Minimum age |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electric moped | up to 4 kW and designed for no more than 50 km/h | none | 16 |
| Electric motorcycle | above 4 kW to 11 kW, or up to 4 kW but faster than 50 km/h | A1 | 18 for A1 |
| Electric motorcycle | above 11 kW | A | 18 |

## What real bikes sit where

The VinFast Evo uses a 2,450 W in-hub motor, under the 4 kW line, but its design speed of up to 70 km/h takes it out of the moped class, so it needs an A1 licence. The Evo Lite, limited to below 50 km/h, is the version VinFast says needs no licence. The VinFast Feliz II and Viper both use 3,000 W motors and also reach 70 km/h: A1 as well. Step up to the Dat Bike Weaver 200 with 6,000 W and you are above the power line too, so an A1 licence is required on both counts.

## The practical trade-offs

Mopeds are cheaper to run and simpler on paperwork, but their design speed keeps you in the slow lane on big roads; on Hanoi arterials with 50-60 km/h traffic flow you will feel the ceiling. Electric motorcycles keep up with petrol traffic and offer regenerative braking and larger batteries, but [need a licence]({{ '/articles/fast-electric-motorbike-licence-vietnam/' | relative_url }}), and battery charging is a real commitment (a full charge of a 72 V 68 Ah pack takes hours).

## Choosing between them

If you ride mostly inside districts at modest speeds and hold no licence, the moped class (4 kW and a design speed of 50 km/h or less) fits. If you already hold an A1 — many foreign residents do — the faster class opens models like the Weaver 200. For rentals, the shop's E-Bike sits in the easy class: 200,000 VND per day, availability by contact.
