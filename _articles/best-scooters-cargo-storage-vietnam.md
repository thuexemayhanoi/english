In a country where the scooter is also the shopping trolley, the delivery van and the school run, underseat storage is a serious spec. This guide ranks every capacity figure we have verified from official Honda and Yamaha Vietnam pages (retrieved 23 September 2026). No estimates - unlisted models are absent.

Models with no published capacity (Air Blade 160, Vision, Grande, Lexi) are omitted; our storage [fit guide]({{ '/articles/scooter-underseat-storage-comparison/' | relative_url }}) covers what actually fits where.

## Choosing by what you carry

- Two full helmets for a couple: Lead or Latte - nothing else publishes a two-full-helmet bay.
- Delivery and boxes: the Vario 160's flat 422 mm floor beats a big bay for boxed goods; pair it with the delivery accessory setup guide.
- One full-face helmet plus laptop: NVX, FreeGo, NMAX, SH160i.
- Minimalists: the sub-18L tier still swallows a rain jacket, lock and gloves.

## Storage versus everything else

Big bays cost something: the Lead gives up lightness (114 kg), the Latte gives up a low seat (790 mm), the SH160i gives up 66 million VND extra over the Latte. Our Lead vs Latte comparison works through that exact trade.

## When storage is not the answer

For rentals, remember that anything on the floor between your feet and anything hanging on hooks changes handling - our carrying-luggage-safely guide covers the safe setups. For a rental with good storage options, contact us to confirm current availability.
