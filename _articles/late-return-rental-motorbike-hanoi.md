Life slips; flights move; the return runs late. The terms are simple, and so is the way to minimise them.

## The terms

At Nguyen Tu, a late return is charged at 20,000 VND per hour, and a return more than six hours late may be charged a full additional rental day - typically 150,000-200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. Returns happen inside opening hours (09:00-21:00 daily); a return you plan for after closing needs to be agreed in advance, not improvised at 22:00.

## How the charge is applied

Hourly charges accrue against the deposit settlement at return - the shop adds them to the balance at the counter rather than chasing you separately. If you are six-plus hours late, the day-charge logic takes over, and the arithmetic becomes: one extra day on your model's rate (150,000-200,000 VND on the published day rates), plus the settlement of whatever the delay cost the shop's next booking.

## The move that saves the money

Message ahead the moment you know. A shop that hears "my flight moved, I will be three hours late" at 10:00 can extend your rental on the books, re-plan its handovers, and often settle the difference reasonably. A shop that discovers it at 21:30 with tomorrow's renter waiting has no such room. WhatsApp or Zalo on +84 942 467 674; the earlier, the cheaper.

## Extending instead of returning late

Most "late returns" are really unplanned extensions. If you want the bike longer, say so - a planned extra day at the day rate is a normal transaction; an unplanned late return is the same money plus friction. Availability permitting, extensions are the better path for everyone. For multi-day extensions the period article shows where the weekly rate takes over.

## What never to do

Return after closing without notice, or leave the bike unattended at the shutter with the keys on the seat. The bike is on your deposit until the shop has it back in hand. An unattended handover is a theft risk you own.

Late is a solvable problem with a phone in your hand. Early notice, agreed extension, settled at the counter.
