Who pays for a puncture on a rental motorbike is settled by the situation and by what you agreed before riding, not by one fixed rule. A flat caused by a nail or glass is a different case from a tyre that was already worn when you collected the bike, so ask who pays before you ride and keep the answer in writing. Here is the honest split.

## The rule of thumb

Inner tubes and tyres can be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation. The distinction that matters:

- A puncture from road debris - a nail, glass, a pothole strike - is the case where the tube is a consumable and the cost may fall to the renter, because it happened while the bike was in your hands.
- A tyre that was bald or damaged at handover, or a failure that comes from the bike's own condition, is the shop's: that is maintenance, and maintenance is the owner's side of the rental.

Treat these two bullets as the reasoning, not as a published schedule. What Hanoi Motorbike Rental Nguyen Tu publishes is the sentence above about inner tubes and tyres, plus the fact that its maintenance may include tyre-pressure checks and a normal inspection; [what a Hanoi motorbike rental includes]({{ '/articles/what-is-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets that list beside the items to ask about. Anything in between, such as a tyre that is worn but not bald, is a question for the shop, and the section after the table lists what to ask.

This is why the [handover checklist]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}) includes looking at the tyres: tread visible, no cracks, and photograph them. The tyre's handover state is the evidence for whose failure a later flat was.

### Situations compared

The table sorts flat-tyre situations by what is unclear and what to keep. It lists questions, not outcomes: how your own rental treats each case is for the shop to say.

| Situation | Why it is not simple | What to ask or keep |
|---|---|---|
| A nail, screw or piece of glass in the tyre | The inner tube or tyre is a consumable, which may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation | Photograph the object and the hole; keep any receipt |
| A tyre that was bald, cracked or damaged at handover | It points to maintenance rather than to anything the renter did | Your handover photos of the tread and sidewalls |
| A soft tyre and no object in sight | A slow leak at the valve, a worn tube and low pressure can look alike from the saddle | A gauge reading at the next fuel station, and the time you noticed |
| A flat after a hard pothole hit | A tube can be pinched against the rim, which is more likely when the pressure is low | Ask when the pressure was last checked, and by whom |

Wear is a separate question from a puncture. A long rental puts more road under the tyres, and [tyres, tubes and consumables on a long-term rental]({{ '/articles/consumables-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) looks at how that changes the conversation.

## Ask before you ride, and get the answers in writing

The money question is easiest to settle before the engine starts. Put these to the shop at pickup:

- Who pays for a puncture repair, and who pays if a tube or a tyre has to be replaced?
- How would a puncture from debris be told apart from a tyre that was already worn when I collected the bike?
- Should I call before any roadside repair, and what do you want from me afterwards: a receipt, a photo, both?
- If I pay a roadside tyre shop, is that settled with you at return, or would you rather arrange the repair yourselves?
- Is a tyre-pressure check part of the maintenance for my rental, and what pressure should this bike have?
- Which number do I message, and when will someone answer?

## What to do when it happens

- Stop quickly - riding on a flat shreds the tube and can wreck the tyre, turning the cheap repair into the expensive one.
- Slow down gradually and avoid hard braking, because a soft tyre changes how the bike steers and stops. Pull in at the kerb or a side street, switch off, and keep the key on you; a lost key is a separate problem, set out in [what happens if you lose the key to a rental motorbike]({{ '/articles/lost-key-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).
- Call or message the shop. Say where you are, what the tyre looks like and whether you can see the cause, and ask what the shop wants you to do. The general order for any breakdown is in [what happens if a rental motorbike breaks down in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-breakdown-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).
- Repair at a roadside tyre shop (vá xe in Vietnamese) only after you have asked. [Can you use outside repair shops on a rental motorbike?]({{ '/articles/outside-repair-shops-rental-motorbike/' | relative_url }}) explains why a call first keeps the story simple. Ask the tyre shop for the price before the work starts, watch the repair if you can, and ask for a receipt or note the shop, the price and the time on your phone.
- Keep the receipt, photograph the cause if you can (the nail in the tube is a good photo), and tell the shop what happened - especially on longer rentals where the shop tracks its bikes' condition. Whether the shop keeps a record of each bike's condition is a question for the shop.

### When the flat comes late or far from the shop

The shop's published hours are 09:00-21:00, and its support is primarily for Hanoi and local use. Get the bike off the road, send the message so it is waiting when the shop opens, and ask what the shop can do and how soon.

## What the repair bill depends on

This guide gives no repair price: the shop publishes none, and what a roadside tyre shop asks depends on the place and the work done. Ask for the price before the work starts, and ask which of three jobs is being quoted:

- Patching the tube: no new part, and the tube stays.
- A new inner tube: the tube is replaced and the tyre stays.
- A new tyre: the most parts, and the answer to damage to the tyre itself, such as a cut sidewall.

That order is by parts replaced, not by a price list. [Flat tyre repair on a motorbike in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/flat-tyre-repair-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) explains when a patch is not enough and a new tube or tyre is the answer.

Which job is quoted matters for the conversation with your rental shop too. The consumables wording names inner tubes and tyres, and a patch at a roadside stand is not the same expense as a new tyre, so it is reasonable to ask whether the answer to who pays changes between them. A cost still unsettled at return becomes a deposit question: [how deposit returns work at Hanoi motorbike rentals]({{ '/articles/how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) describes the general process, and what may be taken from a deposit is best asked before you ride.

## Tubed or tubeless: read it from the maker's page, then ask

The consumables wording says inner tubes and tyres, and which of the two a puncture touches depends on whether the bike runs tubed or tubeless tyres. Some manufacturers state it in their specification tables, and the tables differ from model to model:

| Model (maker's page) | Tyre sizes listed | Tube or tubeless |
|---|---|---|
| Yamaha Sirius RC (Yamaha Vietnam) | 70/90-17 front, 80/90-17 rear | Both listed as lốp có săm, a tyre with an inner tube |
| Honda Wave Alpha 110 (Honda Vietnam) | 70/90-17 front, 80/90-17 rear | The tyre row gives sizes only |
| Yamaha Gear 125 Hybrid (Yamaha Vietnam) | 110/70-12 | Tubeless |

The Sirius RC and the Wave Alpha 110 are underbones and the Gear 125 Hybrid is a scooter. The table shows that the answer differs by model, not what bike you will be given: which models are available is for the shop to confirm.

So ask the shop which type your bike has, and what would be repaired or replaced after a nail. [Inner tubes vs tubeless tyres on Vietnamese motorbikes]({{ '/articles/inner-tubes-vs-tubeless-motorbike/' | relative_url }}) covers how each type behaves in a puncture, so that is not repeated here.

## Prevention, in one habit

Check the tyres at handover, then once a week by feel and at any fuel-station gauge. Underinflated tyres puncture more, handle worse, and wear faster - five seconds of attention prevents most of this article.

### Feel first, then the gauge

Thumb pressure only tells you about a tyre that is clearly soft, while a gauge gives the number. The right number differs by model and by load, so ask the shop what pressure your bike should have. The maker's owner's manual is the document for a model's own figure. [Tyre care and pressure basics for motorbikes]({{ '/articles/tyre-care-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) covers the wear and damage signs to look for.

### Riding habits that save a tyre

- Where you can, keep clear of the litter and debris at the edge of the lane.
- Slow down for potholes and kerbs: a hard hit can pinch a tube.
- Recheck the pressure when you carry a passenger or luggage, because load changes what a tyre needs.
- If the bike feels vague or pulls to one side, stop and look rather than ride on.
- Another fault a rider can feel is covered in [what to do if the chain feels loose on a rental bike]({{ '/articles/loose-chain-on-rental-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).

## Questions renters ask

### Can I just use the nearest tyre shop?

Ask first. A puncture is one of the small jobs that [the guide to outside repair shops on a rental motorbike]({{ '/articles/outside-repair-shops-rental-motorbike/' | relative_url }}) treats as possible after a call to the shop, and a receipt keeps the trail clear.

### Does insurance pay for a puncture?

Hanoi Motorbike Rental Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance, and renters arrange their own. Whether a policy you arranged covers tyres is a question for that insurer, so ask before you rely on it.

### Can the cost come out of my deposit?

That is a term to ask about before you ride, and the answer to rely on is the one the shop gives you in writing. This guide cannot tell you what a particular rental allows.

### Is a patched tyre safe to keep riding on?

That is a mechanic's judgement, not something to settle from the saddle. A patch suits a simple hole, and [flat tyre repair in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/flat-tyre-repair-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) lists the signs that a new tube or tyre is needed instead. Ask the tyre shop to look over the tyre after any repair.

### What if I am on a long rental?

Over a long rental, wear becomes a cost as well as punctures. [Tyres, tubes and consumables on a long rental]({{ '/articles/consumables-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers who may pay for what, and the same advice applies: settle it before you need the answer.
