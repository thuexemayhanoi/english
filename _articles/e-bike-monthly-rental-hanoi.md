Electric bikes suit a particular Hanoi life: short commutes, quiet streets, no fuel stops. Whether they work as a monthly rental depends on what the shop publishes and what it can confirm for you.

## What is published

The E-Bike has a published daily rate of 200,000 VND. There is no published weekly or monthly rate for the E-Bike, and no monthly rate can be assumed or extrapolated from the daily price. If you want an electric bike for a month, contact Nguyen Tu to confirm the current monthly price and availability.

## Where e-bikes fit

E-bikes make sense for riders whose daily loop is short and predictable — a few kilometres between home, work and shops — and for riders who prefer not to handle a petrol bike at all. For longer cross-city commutes, the charging time and range become real constraints; the general trade-offs are covered in best-motorbike-for-commuting-hanoi.

## Legal note

Depending on classification, some electric two-wheelers sit in a lighter legal category than petrol motorbikes, but the exact rules depend on the machine's rated power. Do not assume an e-bike needs no licence without checking; the 
law and licences hub has the verified classifications.

## Set-up questions to ask

Before committing to a month on an e-bike: where will you charge it, does your building allow charging, what does the range cover on your route, and what happens if the battery or charger fails mid-term? Charging arrangements at home are the question renters most often overlook.

## The one-line version

An E-Bike month is possible, but there is no published monthly rate — contact the shop to confirm price and availability, and sort out home charging first.
