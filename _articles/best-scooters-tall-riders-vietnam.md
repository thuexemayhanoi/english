## Seat heights, verified

Higher seats give taller riders more legroom. The verified ranking at the top of the market: Honda SH160i at 799 mm, Yamaha NVX 155 and Yamaha Latte 125 at 790 mm, Yamaha FreeGo 125 at 780 mm, Honda Vario 160 at 778 mm, Honda SH Mode 125 and Yamaha Janus 125 at 770 mm and 765 mm-class lows for smaller bikes. Full table in our seat-height comparison.

## The tall-rider problem is not only seat height

Seat height is the visible number, but legroom is about seat-to-floorboard distance, and almost no manufacturer publishes it. Two checks no spec sheet replaces: sit on the bike and see where your knees sit relative to the handlebars, and test a 20-minute ride - tall riders report knee-on-panel discomfort on some low, compact scooters that never shows up in a data table. Handlebar risers and peg-position changes can help; our ergonomics articles cover both.

## Big wheels, big frames

The SH160i is the strongest tall-rider candidate in the scooter class for reasons beyond its 799 mm seat: 16-inch wheels, a 7-litre tank and a roomy chassis add leg and shoulder space. The NMAX 155 (770 mm) also has a broader, roomier seat than its height suggests; the NVX's 790 mm seat and longer proportions suit taller riders who want sportier ergonomics.
