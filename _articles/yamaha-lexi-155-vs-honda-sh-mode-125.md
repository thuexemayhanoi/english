The Lexi 155 and the SH Mode 125 are the "sensible premium" options from Yamaha and Honda: one step below the SH160i and NMAX flagships, one step above the commuter fleet. Comparing them is really a question about value: what does the extra engine capacity and lower price of the Lexi trade against the SH Mode's badge and 16-inch front wheel? Figures from official Yamaha and Honda Vietnam pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine

Lexi: 155.1 cc Blue Core VVA, 11.3 kW at 8,000 rpm, 14.2 Nm at 6,500 rpm, 2.19 L/100 km from a 4.2-litre tank. SH Mode: 124.8 cc eSP+ four-valve liquid-cooled EURO 4, 8.2 kW at 8,500 rpm, 11.7 Nm at 5,000 rpm, 2.16 L/100 km from a 5.6-litre tank. The Lexi is clearly the stronger engine; the SH Mode counters with a bigger tank and near-identical published economy.

## Wheels and ride

SH Mode: 80/90-16 front and 100/90-14 rear - the SH-family big front wheel, the best pothole behaviour in the class (our 16-inch [wheel guide]({{ '/articles/16-inch-wheel-scooters-vietnam/' | relative_url }})). Lexi: 90/90-14 and 100/90-14 - big for a Yamaha, smaller than the SH Mode's front.

## Weight and seat

Lexi: 118 kg, seat 768 mm. SH Mode: 116 kg, seat 765 mm - the two closest premium-lite matches in our verified tables, with the lowest seats in the premium bracket ([seat-height comparison]({{ '/articles/scooter-seat-heights-comparison-vietnam/' | relative_url }})).

## Equipment

Lexi: front ABS, VVA, 3-year/30,000 km warranty. SH Mode: 18.5-litre underseat bay, ABS on the Sport/Special/Premium versions (our ABS guide). Storage: the SH Mode's published bay is larger than what the Lexi's page publishes.

## Price - the story of this comparison

Yamaha listed the Lexi from 48,500,000 VND. Honda listed the SH Mode from 66,361,091 VND. Nearly 18 million dong buys the Honda badge, the 16-inch front wheel and the SH family's resale strength; the Lexi buys more engine, ABS and modern equipment for materially less money. ## Verdict

SH Mode for badge, resale and the big front wheel; Lexi for value, capacity and comfort hardware. Neither is price-published at Nguyen Tu - contact us to confirm current availability.
