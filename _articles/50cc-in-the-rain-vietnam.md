Rain in Hanoi arrives hard, floods fast, and dries unevenly. On a 50cc it demands more respect than on a bigger bike, for one mechanical reason: the contact patches doing all the work are smaller.

## Why small wheels lose grip first

A narrow tyre has less rubber meeting the road, and its contact patch reaches its grip limit sooner on a slick surface. Wet painted lines, metal manhole covers, smooth polished asphalt and the algae-slick cobbles of the Old Quarter are the classic traps — our wet-roads article details the surfaces. On a 50cc, treat each as ice.

## The first ten minutes are the worst

Light rain on a dry street lifts months of oil and dust into a slick film long before the road is washed clean. If you can shelter for ten minutes when rain starts, do it. That is when most single-bike falls happen.

## Technique

- **Throttle as a volume knob, not a switch.** Open it in fractions, especially turning out of junctions. A 50cc has little power to spare — use even less.
- **Brake earlier, both brakes, never in a lean.** Straighten, then brake. Many 50s run a front drum: drums recover grip more slowly after a soaking, so the first squeeze is a test squeeze. Our drum-brake guide covers rain behaviour.
- **Railway lines and kerb cuts crossed square-on**, never at an angle mid-turn.
- **Standing water you cannot see the bottom of is not a puddle — it is a decision.** The flooded-streets article covers depth judgement and hidden potholes, which swallow small wheels whole.

## Gear and visibility

Rain dulls you and hides you: lights on, speed down, and proper wet gear matters more than courage — our rain gear article covers what actually keeps you functional. Gloves are not optional in rain; cold wet hands brake badly.

## After the ride

Wipe the bike down, park it under cover, and check the next day for water in places it should not be — airbox, electrics, brake action. A ten-minute wipe-down is the cheapest maintenance a 50cc gets. Tyre condition checks belong in the weekly routine; our small-wheels tyre article explains what to look for.

## The honest alternative

A 50cc in rain works. A 50cc in a monsoon downpour with flooded lanes, at night, does not — wait it out. Coffee in Hanoi is cheap and everywhere.
