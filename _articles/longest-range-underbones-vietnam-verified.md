Range is tank size multiplied by economy - and both numbers are published by the manufacturers (retrieved 23 September 2026). Manufacturer figures apply to standard test conditions, so real range varies with speed, load, terrain and weather; treat these as comparable estimates, not promises.

## The verified range table

Approximate tank range from published figures:

- Honda CBR150R - 12 L at 2.91 L/100 km, roughly 410 km
- Honda CT125 - 5.4 L at 1.61 L/100 km, roughly 335 km
- Honda Future 125 FI - 4.6 L at 1.47 L/100 km, roughly 310 km
- Yamaha Exciter 155 VVA - 5.4 L at 2.07 L/100 km, roughly 260 km
- Honda Wave RSX - 4.0 L at 1.56 L/100 km, roughly 255 km
- Honda Winner R - 4.5 L at 2.59 L/100 km, roughly 175 km
- Honda Wave Alpha - 3.7 L at 1.72 L/100 km, roughly 215 km
- Yamaha Sirius RC - 4.2 L at 2.08 L/100 km, roughly 200 km
- Honda Blade - 3.7 L at 1.85 L/100 km, roughly 200 km
- Honda Super Cub C125 - 3.7 L at 1.55 L/100 km, roughly 240 km

## What the table teaches

The CBR150R wins despite the worst economy, purely on its 12-litre tank - the only underbone here in the touring class for range. Among semi-automatics, the Future 125 and CT125 are the distance tools: the Future on economy, the CT on tank size plus clearance for the roads where fuel stations thin out.
