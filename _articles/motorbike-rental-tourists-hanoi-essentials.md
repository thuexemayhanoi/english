If you are visiting Hanoi and considering a motorbike, this is the whole picture in one place: what is legal, what it costs, what you get, and what to do on day one.

## The legality question, answered first

Tourists can rent motorbikes in Hanoi — the question is licensing. Riding legally requires either a Vietnamese motorcycle licence or your home licence accompanied by a valid IDP under the 1968 Vienna Convention. Riding without a valid licence is an administrative violation with fines, and it can void the practical side of your trip if police stop you. The details are in [Can Tourists Legally Rent and Ride a Motorbike in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/can-tourists-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [Can Tourists Legally Ride a Motorbike in Vietnam?]({{ '/articles/can-tourists-ride-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}). If you hold neither, your legal options are 50cc-class machines (licence-free for riders 16 and over) or rides as a passenger. The documents a shop and the police will ask for are listed in [Motorbike Rental Requirements in Hanoi: Documents, Licence, Age]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## What tourists typically book

Most visitors rent for one to three days: enough for the Old Quarter, a day trip to Bat Trang or the Perfume Pagoda area, and evening rides along West Lake. The full booking process — when to reserve, what to confirm, pickup versus delivery — is in [How to Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi: A Step-by-Step Guide]({{ '/articles/how-to-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [How to Book a Motorbike Rental in Hanoi (WhatsApp, Zalo, Walk-In)]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}). For sightseeing inside the city, an automatic scooter is the usual choice; see [Best Bike for Riding in the Hanoi Old Quarter]({{ '/articles/best-bike-for-hanoi-old-quarter/' | relative_url }}) for the reasoning. Landing at Noi Bai Airport? [Renting a Motorbike When You Arrive at Noi Bai Airport]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) explains how handover works for arrivals.

## What it costs

Rental prices in Hanoi are modest: published daily rates at Nguyen Tu run 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the bike (see [How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }})), plus a deposit, typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND, returned when the bike comes back ([How Deposit Returns Work at Hanoi Motorbike Rentals]({{ '/articles/how-deposit-returns-work-hanoi/' | relative_url }})). Payment is cash, bank transfer, or QR. Add fuel — inexpensive — and you have the full budget picture.

## What a rental includes

Helmets are normally included at no extra charge. What else is included varies by shop and agreement: tools, fuel level, maintenance responsibility — see [What Is Included in a Hanoi Motorbike Rental?]({{ '/articles/what-is-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}). One thing to confirm explicitly is insurance: rental shops in Hanoi, including Nguyen Tu, do not provide rider insurance, so any medical or liability cover is your own arrangement. The full picture is in [Is Insurance Included with Motorbike Rentals in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/is-insurance-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [Do You Need Your Own Insurance to Ride in Vietnam?]({{ '/articles/do-you-need-insurance-to-ride-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).

## Day one, done right

Inspect the bike at handover with the checklist in [Rental Motorbike Inspection Checklist: What to Check Before You Ride]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}): photograph existing scratches, test both brakes, lights and horn, check tyre condition, and confirm where the fuel cap is. Ride the first hour deliberately: tourists crash most often in hour one, in Old Quarter traffic, going too fast for conditions. Hanoi traffic moves to its own rhythm — read [Best Motorbike for Hanoi Traffic: What Actually Works]({{ '/articles/best-motorbike-for-hanoi-traffic/' | relative_url }}) for what to expect.

## The essentials in one line

Legal licence, booked bike, inspected handover, deposit receipt, helmet on, calm first hour — after that, a motorbike is the best way to see Hanoi.
