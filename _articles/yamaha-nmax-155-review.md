The NMAX is Yamaha's MAX-series premium scooter for Vietnam: a 155 cc machine with the sort of hardware list that used to belong only to big bikes - dual-wheel ABS, traction control and phone connectivity. Every figure below comes from the official Yamaha Motor Vietnam NMAX product page (retrieved 23 September 2026); anything Yamaha does not publish is left out rather than estimated.

## Engine and performance

The NMAX runs Yamaha's G3Y1E engine: a 155.1 cc single-cylinder four-stroke, liquid-cooled unit with SOHC, four valves and VVA - Yamaha's variable valve timing that favours low-rev flexibility and high-rev punch at the same time. Official maximum power is 11.3 kW at 8,000 rpm and maximum torque 14.2 Nm at 6,500 rpm, fed by electronic fuel injection from a 7.1-litre tank. Published consumption is 2.26 litres/100 km; real-world figures vary with speed, load and conditions. Our VVA explainer covers what the variable valve system actually does.

## Brakes and safety hardware

This is where the NMAX separates itself from almost every scooter in its price class: a 230 mm front disc AND a rear disc, both with ABS (dual-channel), plus a traction control system that reduces wheel spin when you accelerate hard on slippery surfaces. ABS and traction control do different jobs - our TCS vs ABS guide explains the difference - and the combination makes the NMAX one of the best-equipped mainstream scooters sold in Vietnam.

## Size, weight and comfort

Yamaha lists 131 kg weight, 770 mm seat height, 1935 x 740 x 1200 mm dimensions, 1,340 mm wheelbase and 127 mm ground clearance. The two-level seat gives rider and pillion their own positions, and the underseat bay holds 24 litres - enough for a full-face helmet plus extras. Maximum payload is listed at 167 kg; what that figure means for rider, passenger and luggage is covered in our payload [guide]({{ '/articles/scooter-payload-load-limits-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).

## Technology

A smart key lets you unlock, open the seat and start without a metal key. The Y-Connect app link brings call and message alerts, fuel-consumption tracking and maintenance reminders to the cockpit, and there is a USB Type-C charging port. A Stop and Start System II switches the engine off at traffic lights and restarts it when you twist the throttle - our idling-stop explainer covers how these systems behave in Hanoi traffic.

## Who it suits

Riders who want maximum safety equipment and are willing to pay for it: Yamaha's list price at retrieval time was 69,000,000 VND for the standard NMAX, with a TECH MAX version also offered.
