The all-in cost of an A1 licence is built from three layers: the training-centre course, the state exam fees, and the licence issuance fee. The state layers are fixed and published; the course fee is market-priced.

## The state fees

From 1 January 2026, Circular 154/2025/TT-BTC of the Ministry of Finance sets the exam fees per attempt: 60,000 VND for the theory sitting and 70,000 VND for the practical. Each re-sit is charged again. The same circular sets the licence issuance fee for the card itself, and reduced it by 20,000 VND against the previous schedule — an odd but real detail of the 2026 fee reform. Missing a scheduled sitting forfeits the fee paid.

## The training course

The course is the variable layer. Centres licensed for A1 training in Hanoi typically package tuition, dossier preparation, the health certificate and the exam registration together; market pricing in 2025 ran roughly from under a million to around two million VND all-in depending on the centre and the schedule speed. The fixed-hour requirement of Circular 14/2025 (effective 1 September 2025) sets the floor on what a course contains, so "weekend express" pricing compresses the calendar, not the hours.

## What does not cost extra

The licence itself has no expiry for A1, so there is no renewal line-item ever. Points restoration, if you ever need it, is a separate process with its own schedule.

## A note for foreigners

Foreigners holding a valid national motorbike licence usually pay far less overall, because the exchange route skips the training course — dossier, translation and issuance only. Before paying for a course, check whether your licence can be exchanged: the savings are the whole course fee.
