This is the widest capability gap inside the underbone world: Honda's entry rotary-gearbox commuter against Yamaha's flagship six-speed manual sport bike. All figures are official Honda VN and Yamaha VN specifications (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and transmission

Wave Alpha: 109.2 cc air-cooled single, 6.12 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.44 Nm at 5,500 rpm, four-speed rotary. Exciter 155 VVA: 155 cc liquid-cooled four-valve single with variable valve actuation and EFI, 13.2 kW at 9,500 rpm, 14.4 Nm at 8,000 rpm, six-speed gearbox with an Assist and Slipper clutch. The Exciter more than doubles the Wave Alpha's output — but you need clutch skills to access it. Our xe so versus xe con tay explainer covers the practical difference between these gearbox types.

## Weight, tanks and economy

Wave Alpha: 96 kg, 3.7-litre tank, 1.72 L/100 km claimed. Exciter: 123 kg wet, 5.4 litres, 2.07 L/100 km claimed. Both manufacturer figures; real-world numbers move with speed and load. The Wave Alpha still wins the running-cost contest, as it should at a third of the price — 18,841,091 VND versus 55,200,000 VND list for the limited ABS Monster version shown.

## Who each suits

The Wave Alpha is deliberately undramatic: cheap, light, economical, and one of the two price-published rental classes at Nguyen Tu (150,000 VND/day).
