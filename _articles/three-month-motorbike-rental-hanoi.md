One to three months is the classic "long stay, not a life" duration: interns, semester-abroad students, seasonal workers, contract staff, slow nomads. Motorbike rental fits it well — here is how the arrangement works at quarter scale.

## The structure

Multi-month rentals are built on the monthly product (monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi): a monthly rate where published (motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi), a deposit held for the term, maintenance included. For a fixed multi-month term, the arrangement for the whole period is agreed directly with the shop — contact us to discuss the arrangement for your dates. Never extrapolate a three-month price yourself; multi-month terms are set in the conversation.

## What to negotiate at booking

- The rate structure for the full term, and what happens if you leave early (no automatic refund entitlement — see shortening-long-motorbike-rental-hanoi)
- The maintenance schedule across the term: a quarter of daily riding will need at least one oil/chain visit — maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi
- Consumables responsibility for the term, in writing
- The return date and time, plus the renewal path if the contract extends — extending-motorbike-rental-hanoi

## The quarter's rhythm

- Month 1: the settling-in routine from starting-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi
- Month 2: the maintenance visit, the check routine (monthly-rental-inspection-schedule-hanoi), and your first taste of the mileage question if you ride far — high-mileage-monthly-rental-hanoi
- Month 3: wind-down — decide renewal or return a couple of weeks out, book the return, and budget the exit time (late-return-monthly-rental-hanoi)

## Budgeting the quarter

Monthly rate × 3, plus fuel and parking, plus the deposit held throughout — the full budget picture is in motorbike-rental-budget-monthly-hanoi. Seasonal note: if your three months fall across the rainy season, add rain gear; across winter, add layers (renting-motorbike-hanoi-rainy-season, renting-motorbike-hanoi-in-winter).

## The one-line version

A monthly product with a pre-agreed multi-month term: negotiate the whole period up front, keep the maintenance visits, and decide the exit two weeks early.
