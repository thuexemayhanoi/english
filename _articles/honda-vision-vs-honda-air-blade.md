Honda's two mainstream scooters sit one rung apart: the Vision 110 is the entry point, the Air Blade family (125 or 160) the step up. Verified Honda Vietnam figures (retrieved 23 September 2026), with the Air Blade 160 as the stronger sibling:

## The numbers side by side

| | Vision 110 | Air Blade 160 |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 109.5 cc, air-cooled, eSP | 156.9 cc, liquid-cooled |
| Max power | 6.59 kW @ 7,500 rpm | 11.2 kW @ 8,000 rpm |
| Max torque | 9.29 Nm @ 6,000 rpm | 14.8 Nm @ 6,000 rpm |
| Kerb weight | 97 kg | 113 kg |
| Seat height | 785 mm | 775 mm |
| Claimed economy | 1.82 L/100 km | 2.23 L/100 km |
| Fuel tank | 4.8 L | 4.4 L |
| Notable equipment | LCD meter, smart key versions | USB-C, smart key, LCD meter |
| Listed retail price | from 31,506,545 VND | family from 42,404,727 VND |

## What the numbers mean

- **Performance:** the Air Blade's extra 47 cc delivers roughly two-thirds more power - the difference between relaxed commuting and confident two-up riding.
- **Weight:** 16 kg apart. The Vision is easier to handle and park for smaller riders; the Air Blade feels steadier at speed.
- **Economy:** the Vision's 1.82 L/100 km is among the best published figures in the class; the Air Blade pays for its performance.
- **Cooling:** air-cooled simplicity (Vision) vs liquid-cooled thermal headroom (Air Blade) - our cooling explainer covers the trade-offs.

## Which should you choose?

Vision for economy, lightness and price. Air Blade for pace, a passenger and longer weekend routes.
