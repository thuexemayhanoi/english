The FreeGo and the Air Blade 125 are the sporty-looking mid 125s that most buyers actually choose between, and they split along a clean line: the FreeGo is the practical sporty scooter, the Air Blade is the sharper Honda. Figures from official Yamaha Vietnam and Honda Vietnam pages (retrieved 23 September 2026); unpublished fields are omitted.

## Engine

The FreeGo's air-cooled Blue Core 124.9 cc makes 7.0 kW at 8,000 rpm and 9.0 Nm at 5,500 rpm, at 2.03 litres/100 km from a 3.9-litre tank. Honda's Air Blade 125 page did not publish output or economy figures in our retrieval - what is verified is the 125 cc liquid-cooled engine with a coolant temperature warning. The FreeGo quotes its numbers; the Air Blade keeps its mystique.

## Practicality

The FreeGo's flat floor and 25-litre underseat bay make it the cargo tool of the pair. The Air Blade counters with a 23.2-litre bay that fits two half helmets plus a USB-C charge port - Honda's best-documented storage figure at this level.

## Weight and equipment

FreeGo: 100 kg wet, 780 mm seat, smart key on current versions, LED headlight, disc/drum. Air Blade 125: 110 kg kerb, liquid cooling, USB-C. Ten kilograms apart: the FreeGo is the easier bike to wrangle, per our kerb weight table.

## Price and renting

FreeGo lists from about 30,340,000 VND. The Air Blade sits above it in Honda's range; check Honda's current pricing when buying. Rental note: Nguyen Tu publishes rates for the Honda Air Blade category (200,000 VND day; 800,000 VND week; 1,400,000 VND month); the FreeGo has no published rate - contact us to confirm current availability of either.

## Verdict

Carry more, pay less, quote your own figures: FreeGo. Want the Honda badge, liquid cooling and a slightly sportier feel: Air Blade 125. Then read our Vario 125 comparisons to see Honda's more modern alternative.
