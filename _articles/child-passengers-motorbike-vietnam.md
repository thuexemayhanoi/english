Children ride pillion in Vietnam every day — the school run is a pillar of Hanoi traffic — and the law carves the child cases out of the general passenger limits explicitly. Article 33 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (No. 36/2024/QH15) allows a motorbike to carry up to two people in defined situations, and a child travelling with an adult is one of them.

## The passenger rule for children

The default is one passenger. Carrying two — three people on the bike — is permitted when the second is a child under 14 with an adult, alongside the other exceptions (medical transport, frail elderly or disabled persons, escorting a violator). A parent taking a child to school may legally carry the child plus no other adult; the exception does not open the door to a family of four.

## The helmet rule for children

Every child on a motorbike needs a helmet meeting the national standard, fastened correctly. There is no general age exemption from wearing one — small children need properly sized helmets, which rental shops and markets stock. The narrow exemption that exists is about the helmetless-passenger fine: carrying someone without a helmet is not fined when that person is a child under 6 (or a patient being taken for medical care, or an escorted violator). The exemption is about the fine for the rider, not a statement that helmets are optional for small children.

## Why this matters more for visitors

Foreign riders are less accustomed to carrying children in dense traffic and tend to underestimate two things: the child's helmet (a full-size helmet does not protect a small head) and the stability cost of a shifting passenger. If a rental includes carrying a child, ask for a child-size helmet and accept the second-passenger rule's shape: one adult, one child, one bike.

## The fines

Carrying a passenger outside the permitted cases is 400,000–600,000 VND under Article 7(2) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP; carrying three or more riders is a heavier violation with licence-point deduction, and any overload that contributes to a crash moves the case into the accident-causing band. The child exception keeps the school run legal — it does not keep three adults on one bike legal.
