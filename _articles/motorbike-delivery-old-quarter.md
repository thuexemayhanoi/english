A motorbike rental with hotel delivery may be available for an Old Quarter address, depending on location and arrangement. For visitors staying in the Old Quarter, hotel delivery is the pattern that makes a [motorbike rental]({{ '/topics/rental/' | relative_url }}) practical: the district is the worst place in Hanoi to navigate to on your first day, and one of the best to ride out of once you have the bike.

## What delivery to the Old Quarter means

Nguyen Tu arranges delivery and pickup depending on your location and what is agreed — case by case, by arrangement, inside opening hours (09:00–21:00 daily). There is no published fixed delivery fee, no guaranteed arrival window, and no delivery outside opening hours; the conditions for your rental are agreed when you book. The general delivery framework is in [Shop Pickup vs Delivery When Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

The shop has not published how a delivery is carried out or who carries it out, so this page sticks to what is in your hands: what to ask, what to prepare, and what the Old Quarter changes about the handover, the first ride and the parking. The district itself, from the choice of bike to the riding rules, is covered in [Renting a Motorbike in the Hanoi Old Quarter: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-old-quarter/' | relative_url }}).

## Arranging it, step by step

1. Message or call with: hotel address in the Old Quarter, the dates, and the bike category — see [How to Book a Motorbike Rental in Hanoi (WhatsApp, Zalo, Walk-In)]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})
2. Agree the delivery arrangement and any conditions with the shop in that conversation
3. Confirm current availability of the model you want — bike stock varies, so ask
4. On the day: do the full handover at your hotel — allow the time it needs, because it is not a formality

In the Old Quarter a street name and number may not be enough to find the right door, so the first two steps repay some care.

### What to tell the shop about an Old Quarter address

Put the dates, the bike and how you will pay in your first message. For a hotel in the Old Quarter, add these:

- The hotel's name and the street its entrance faces, plus a map pin shared in the chat. If the entrance is down a lane, say so.
- What the hotel has told you about reaching that entrance, including which way the street runs.
- A second number that reaches you on the day, such as the hotel's own.
- The window you want inside 09:00–21:00, given as a window rather than an exact minute, since no arrival time is guaranteed. Pick one away from your hotel's busiest moments at the door.
- Whether that window falls on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday evening, for the reason given in the weekend section below.
- How and when you want the bike back, including whether you want it collected from the hotel.

The shop's number for WhatsApp, Zalo and calls is +84 942 467 674.

### What to ask your hotel

Hotels differ, so treat each of these as a question rather than an assumption, and ask them before you give the shop a handover time.

| Ask the hotel | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Can a motorbike reach the entrance, and which way does the street run? | A one-way street or narrow lane changes where the handover can happen. |
| Is there a spot off the carriageway where a bike may stand during an inspection? | It lets you check the bike without blocking the lane. |
| Can the front desk call your room when the bike arrives? | Be at the handover yourself; ask the shop whether anyone else may receive the bike. |
| Where do guests leave a motorbike overnight, is it watched, and is there a charge? | The bike needs somewhere to sleep on the day it arrives. |
| Is the street covered by the Friday-to-Sunday evening notice below? | A hotel on a named street may be affected on those evenings. |

### What to have ready

Keep your documents on you rather than locked away in the room, and ask the shop which ones it wants to see. If the hotel is holding any of them, collect them before the bike arrives. [Can Foreigners Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/can-foreigners-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers eligibility and documents for visitors from abroad.

The deposit is typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the vehicle and conditions, and payment is by cash, bank transfer or QR. Ask when and how the deposit is paid for a delivered bike, then count the cash or test your banking app before the day. Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance, so arrange your own before the bike arrives.

## The handover at the hotel door

Hold the handover at your hotel to the same standard as one at the shop: the inspection checklist in [Rental Motorbike Inspection Checklist: What to Check Before You Ride]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}), the terms and deposit receipt from [Understanding a Motorbike Rental Agreement in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-agreement-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), and photographs of existing scratches. A lobby or a curb is the same place as a shop forecourt for this purpose.

What changes in the Old Quarter is the room around you, not the checks. Two points belong to the district:

- **Choose where you stand.** Inspect at the agreed spot, off the carriageway and clear of the entrance, not wherever the bike stops.
- **Keep the moving check short.** A crowded lane is no place for a test ride: roll a few metres at walking pace where the way is clear and test both brakes.

[The Long Bien delivery walk-through]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) covers the rest of a doorstep handover, from confirming the model to photographing every panel and signing last. The signature records the condition of the bike at handover, so the person who signs and rides should be the one who inspects it.

## The first ride: from the hotel door to the first corner

Work out your first two turns before you start the engine. If the street outside the hotel is one-way, you cannot simply turn round in it, and the way to a main road may be a loop, so ask the hotel which way to leave and check it against your map. [Riding the Old Quarter's One-Way Street Maze]({{ '/articles/old-quarter-one-way-streets-riding/' | relative_url }}) explains how the grid works and what to do when you are on the wrong side of it. If a turn goes wrong, stop at the first safe gap, dismount and walk the bike rather than push on against the traffic.

Old Quarter streets are narrow and busy: plan a first practice ride outside the core (the French Quarter grid to the south is the calm option — see [Renting a Motorbike in Hoan Kiem: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-hoan-kiem/' | relative_url }})) rather than out the hotel door. Reaching calmer streets still means riding out through the core first, so start when you are not hurrying. If that prospect worries you, the comparison under "When pickup beats delivery" below sets out a first-time rider's options side by side.

## Where the bike sleeps: parking after the handover

A delivered bike needs somewhere to stay from the day it arrives, so settle the parking question at the handover, not after it. The answers to your hotel questions decide what happens next: a place at the hotel, a place the hotel points you to, or an attended lot you find yourself. [Where to Park a Motorbike in Hanoi: Rules and Etiquette]({{ '/articles/where-to-park-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers paid lots, no-parking zones and overnight parking.

Before you walk away for the first time, lock the steering, take the key and your helmet, and leave nothing under the seat. Ask the shop what the terms say if the bike is stolen or damaged while it is parked, and settle the answer before you sign.

## Friday to Sunday evenings: check the notice first

If your hotel is in the Old Quarter, a restriction reported in early July 2026 may reach its street.

VietnamPlus (1 July 2026) reports a first phase from 1 July to 31 December 2026: motorcycles may not enter Zone 1 between 19:00 and 24:00 on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, except vehicles operated by the Ministries of National Defence and Public Security. The Viet Nam Government Portal (2 July 2026) gives the same hours and defines Zone 1 by a list of named streets, which may be narrower than the whole Old Quarter. VietnamPlus describes Zone 1 as including the Old Quarter, so do not assume a street missing from the list is outside it. For the detail, see [renting in Hoan Kiem]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-hoan-kiem/' | relative_url }}).

Neither report covers dates after the first phase, so check the current official notice for the streets, hours and dates before you plan a Friday, Saturday or Sunday evening around the bike. For a hotel in the Old Quarter that leaves three practical questions, which the reports do not answer:

- Is the hotel's street one of the named streets? Ask the hotel, and check the notice.
- Does a handover or return fall between 19:00 and 24:00 on one of those days? The shop's 09:00–21:00 window overlaps that period from 19:00 to 21:00, so ask the shop before you fix a time in that slot. A time earlier in the day falls outside the reported hours.
- If you come back to the hotel with the bike on one of those evenings, where will it wait? [Best Bike for Riding in the Hanoi Old Quarter]({{ '/articles/best-bike-for-hanoi-old-quarter/' | relative_url }}) suggests leaving the bike at the edge of the area and walking in.

## Returning the bike

Return follows the same arrangement: shop return across the river, or collection from the hotel, if the shop can arrange it. Decide which when you book, and write the day, the hour and the place in the chat. Leave buffer time before any departure, because a one-way grid can turn a short distance into a loop, and mind the late-return clock in [What Happens If You Return a Rental Motorbike Late in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}). If the agreed hour falls on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday evening, apply the same check as for the handover, and remember that the shop closes at 21:00.

## When pickup beats delivery

Staying flexible on the model, or wanting to see several bikes, argues for the shop visit: Nguyen Tu is across the river at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street in Long Bien — see [Motorbike Delivery to Long Bien: How It Works]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) and [Renting a Motorbike in Long Bien: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) for that side of the arrangement.

For an Old Quarter guest the choice comes down to a few situations:

| Your situation | Hotel handover | Shop pickup |
|---|---|---|
| First time on a motorbike in Vietnam | Your first ride starts inside the district | The handover is away from the district's busiest streets; the ride to your hotel still follows |
| Want to see more than one bike | Only the bike that arrives | A chance to see several, if several are available |
| Short on time | No trip to Long Bien first, if the shop can arrange your address and dates | A journey across the river before the rental starts |
| Prefer to end the rental at the hotel | Ask whether the shop can collect the bike from there | Return it at the shop across the river |

## Common questions

### Can I get a motorbike rental with hotel delivery in the Old Quarter?

Delivery or pickup in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement, so ask the shop when you book. Give the hotel's name, street and entrance, and confirm that the model you want is available for your dates. No arrival time is guaranteed, and delivery is not available outside 09:00–21:00.

### Is there a fee for delivery to an Old Quarter hotel?

No fixed delivery fee is published, and delivery is not promised as a free service. Ask whether the arrangement carries any charge, and have the amount in the chat before the day rather than on arrival.

### My hotel is down a lane. Does that rule out delivery?

Only the shop can say whether your address works for your dates. Tell it the street and that the entrance is down a lane, ask the hotel where a bike can stand, and agree the place in the chat before the day.

### Can I ride into the Old Quarter on a Friday or Saturday evening?

Two reports of early July 2026 describe a first-phase restriction that prohibits motorcycles from entering Zone 1 between 19:00 and 24:00 every Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and VietnamPlus reports it as running from 1 July to 31 December 2026. The Viet Nam Government Portal defines Zone 1 as a list of named streets that may be narrower than the whole Old Quarter, but VietnamPlus describes Zone 1 as including the Old Quarter, so do not assume a street missing from the list is outside it. Neither report covers later dates, so check the current official notice and ask your hotel whether its street is covered.

### What does a late return cost if the bike was delivered?

Late return is charged at 20,000 VND per hour. More than 6 hours late, a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. Write the agreed return hour in the chat so both sides work from the same time.
