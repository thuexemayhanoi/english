For visitors staying in the Old Quarter, hotel delivery is the pattern that makes a [motorbike rental]({{ '/topics/rental/' | relative_url }}) practical: the district is the worst place in Hanoi to navigate to on your first day, and one of the best to ride out of once you have the bike.

## What delivery to the Old Quarter means

Nguyen Tu arranges delivery and pickup depending on your location and what is agreed — case by case, by arrangement, inside opening hours (09:00–21:00 daily). There is no published fixed delivery fee, no guaranteed arrival window, and no delivery outside opening hours; the conditions for your rental are agreed when you book. The general delivery framework is in [Shop Pickup vs Delivery When Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Arranging it, step by step

1. Message or call with: hotel address in the Old Quarter, the dates, and the bike category — see [How to Book a Motorbike Rental in Hanoi (WhatsApp, Zalo, Walk-In)]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})
2. Agree the delivery arrangement and any conditions with the shop in that conversation
3. Confirm current availability of the model you want — bike stock varies, so ask
4. On the day: do the full handover at your hotel — ten minutes, not a formality

The handover at delivery is identical to the shop version: the inspection checklist in [Rental Motorbike Inspection Checklist: What to Check Before You Ride]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}), the terms and deposit receipt from [Understanding a Motorbike Rental Agreement in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-agreement-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), photographs of existing scratches. A lobby or a curb is the same place as a shop forecourt for this purpose.

## The Old Quarter-specific realities

- Old Quarter streets are narrow and busy: plan a first practice ride outside the core (the French Quarter grid to the south is the calm option — see [Renting a Motorbike in Hoan Kiem: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-hoan-kiem/' | relative_url }})) rather than out the hotel door
- Parking near your hotel matters more than anywhere in the city: ask your hotel where renters park, and read [Where to Park a Motorbike in Hanoi: Rules and Etiquette]({{ '/articles/where-to-park-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }})
- Return follows the same arrangement: shop return across the river, or agreed collection — buffer time before departure, and mind the late-return clock in [What Happens If You Return a Rental Motorbike Late in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }})

## When pickup beats delivery

Staying flexible on the model, or wanting to see several bikes, argues for the shop visit: Nguyen Tu is about fifteen minutes' ride away on Nguyen Van Cu in Long Bien — see [Motorbike Delivery to Long Bien: How It Works]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) and [Renting a Motorbike in Long Bien: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) for that side of the arrangement.
