For visitors staying in the Old Quarter, delivery is the pattern that makes a motorbike rental practical: the district is the worst place in Hanoi to navigate to on your first day, and one of the best to ride out of once you have the bike.

## What delivery to the Old Quarter means

Nguyen Tu arranges delivery and pickup depending on your location and what is agreed — case by case, by arrangement, inside opening hours (09:00–21:00 daily). There is no published fixed delivery fee, no guaranteed arrival window, and no delivery outside opening hours; the conditions for your rental are agreed when you book. The general delivery framework is in motorbike-rental-pickup-or-delivery-hanoi.

## Arranging it, step by step

1. Message or call with: hotel address in the Old Quarter, the dates, and the bike category — see how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi
2. Agree the delivery arrangement and any conditions with the shop in that conversation
3. Confirm current availability of the model you want — bike stock varies, so ask
4. On the day: do the full handover at your hotel — ten minutes, not a formality

The handover at delivery is identical to the shop version: the inspection checklist in rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist, the terms and deposit receipt from motorbike-rental-agreement-hanoi, photographs of existing scratches. A lobby or a curb is the same place as a shop forecourt for this purpose.

## The Old Quarter-specific realities

- Old Quarter streets are narrow and busy: plan a first practice ride outside the core (the French Quarter grid to the south is the calm option — see renting-motorbike-hoan-kiem) rather than out the hotel door
- Parking near your hotel matters more than anywhere in the city: ask your hotel where renters park, and read where-to-park-motorbike-hanoi
- Return follows the same arrangement: shop return across the river, or agreed collection — buffer time before departure, and mind the late-return clock in late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi

## When pickup beats delivery

Staying flexible on the model, or wanting to see several bikes, argues for the shop visit: Nguyen Tu is about fifteen minutes' ride away on Nguyen Van Cu in Long Bien — see motorbike-delivery-long-bien and renting-motorbike-long-bien for that side of the arrangement.
