The short answer for anyone looking at year-long motorbike rental in Hanoi: Nguyen Tu's published rental periods are daily, weekly and monthly, and a twelve-month term is negotiable, so the price for the year is agreed with the shop and written down instead of read from a table. A year is the duration where rental stops being a convenience purchase and becomes a transport policy. Here is how year-scale rental works in Hanoi and what to weigh before committing.

## How the arrangement works

Longer-term arrangements beyond the published monthly rates are negotiable with the customer — contact us to discuss longer-term rental for your dates and expected usage. A year-scale agreement is built on the monthly product ([Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi: How It Works]({{ '/articles/monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})) but typically takes one of two shapes: a fixed-term arrangement agreed once for the whole year, or a rolling month-to-month renewal held open all year. The rolling version is the flexible one; the fixed-term version is the one to negotiate carefully — early ending carries no automatic refund entitlement ([Shortening a Long Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/shortening-long-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})).

Over twelve months the two shapes differ in where the negotiation happens. A rolling arrangement has a renewal point each month, the moment to ask for the rate and the bike's availability to be confirmed again; a fixed term is one negotiation and one exit clause.

Neither shape comes off a price list, because the published rates stop at the month. [Motorbike Rental Monthly Prices in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the models that have a published monthly rate; for a model or term not listed there, contact us to confirm the current price and availability. Do not work out a year-long price by multiplying a monthly figure: longer terms are agreed with the shop, so ask for the year's figure directly and keep the reply in your message thread.

## Which length fits your stay

Twelve months is one point on a scale, and the neighbouring lengths have guides of their own.

- End date still uncertain: stay on the rolling pattern, and treat each renewal point as the moment to decide whether to continue.

- Maintenance becomes a schedule, not an event: plan for the checks the shop's maintenance may include — the pattern is in [Maintenance During a Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

### Questions that frame the comparison

A year is also close to the point where buying gets a fair hearing, so test the choice against your own situation.

- How long will you really stay? A firm end date favours renting; an open-ended stay is where owning gets a fair hearing.
- What would a bike cost you? Take a current quote or listing rather than a rule of thumb, and set it against the year-long price once the shop has given you one in writing.
- What could you sell it for, and how fast? Leaving on a fixed date means selling on a fixed date.
- Who carries the risk of repairs, tyres and paperwork? Under a rental the risk is shared under the terms you agreed, and consumables may be yours; owning puts all of it on you.

Decide a few months before the term ends, so that renewing, returning or buying is a plan rather than a default.

## Terms to agree for twelve months

A vague term matters more in a year-long agreement, because it runs twelve times as long as a monthly one. Three terms deserve a written answer from the shop before you sign, and the reply is worth keeping with your handover photos. The wider checklist is in the [long-term rental agreement guide]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-agreement-long-term-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

| Term | What to ask the shop to confirm |
|---|---|
| Price and payment | The price for the full year, the dates it covers and how it is split across payments. The shop accepts cash, bank transfer and QR payment, and the split of a year into payments is not published, so agree the schedule in writing |
| Deposit | The amount, typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on the vehicle and conditions, what it is held against and how and when it is returned |
| Renewal and return | The end date and time, and how renewal works |

The published late-return charge is 20,000 VND per hour; ask whether it applies at the end of the term. Arrange a renewal before the end date instead of after it.

## Licence and insurance for a resident

### Licence

Riding for a year is a different position from riding for a week. [How Foreigners Exchange a Licence for a Vietnamese One]({{ '/articles/foreign-licence-exchange-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) draws the line between a visitor's International Driving Permit and a resident's exchange of a national licence.

Under Article 24 of Circular 12/2025/TT-BCA, a foreigner who lives, works or studies in Vietnam and holds a national driving licence that is still valid can be considered for an exchange into the corresponding Vietnamese licence class. The foreigner must also hold one of the residence papers named in Article 24, such as a temporary or permanent residence card, with a duration of three months or more. The exchange guide adds that an International Driving Permit cannot be exchanged, that a licence covering only cars does not convert into motorbike categories, and that applications go to the Traffic Police Department or a commune-level police point that accepts them.

The practical points are separate from the law. Start gathering the dossier early, including the application form, the certified translation, a copy of the residence card and a health certificate where one is required. Put every expiry date in one list, and work back from the date your current permission to ride ends.

### Insurance

Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance, so a twelve-month renter arranges their own. [Arranging Your Own Insurance as a Long-Term Renter in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/insurance-for-long-term-renter-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the questions to put to an insurer. Over a year, check that the policy's term matches the rental's and that its exclusions do not rule out motorbike riding at the licence class you hold.

## A maintenance rhythm across twelve months

The shop's maintenance may include an oil change, chain adjustment and tension, tyre-pressure checks and normal inspection. Over a year the renter's task is to make those visits happen on a rhythm instead of waiting for a problem. As general guidance, not a manufacturer's service schedule: before the wet months and after a soaking, check tyres, brakes, lights and that the seat and fuel cap close properly.

## Hanoi weather and Tet across the year

### Wet months and cold months

A twelve-month term means riding through every season the city has, whichever month you start, so the wet months and the cold months belong in the plan from the start. What a year-long renter adds is permanence. Buy the seasonal kit once, a rain suit for the wet months and gloves and a windproof layer for the cold ones, and keep it with the bike instead of rebuilding it every season. Report a hard start on a cold morning or a flickering light after a storm the same day, rather than waiting for the next scheduled check.

### Tet and the holiday weeks

Tet comes once a year and its date moves with the lunar calendar, so a year-long term will normally include one holiday period. Nguyen Tu's normal hours are 09:00–21:00 daily, but the arrangements for the holiday itself should be confirmed directly with the shop. Check three dates against the holiday: a renewal date, a maintenance visit and the end of the term. [Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi During Tet and Holidays]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-hanoi-tet-holidays/' | relative_url }}) explains how to confirm opening and return arrangements.

## A planning calendar for the year

A year has no fixed calendar of its own, so build one from the dates you already hold and work backwards from each. Keep the list with the agreement and the messages that confirm it.

**End of the term.** The renew, return or buy decision, using the comparison above, with any renewal terms confirmed in writing before the date arrives.

**Licence expiry.** Whether your permission to ride covers the whole term. If it does not, [what to do when a licence or visa expires mid-rental]({{ '/articles/licence-expiry-during-long-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out the order of work.

**Residence card expiry.** The duration of the residence papers you hold, set against the exchange route above, before an application starts.

**Insurance renewal.** A renewal date that falls inside the term, with the policy's term matching the rental's.

**Tet.** The coming holiday dates, held against every other date here: a renewal, an expiry or a payment that falls in the holiday weeks is better handled before them than during them.

**Odometer.** The reading against the ~1,500 km guidance for a periodic check, which is practical owner guidance and not a manufacturer interval. A daily commuter reaches it sooner than a weekend rider, so count in kilometres rather than months.

## Questions about renting for a year

### How much does a year-long motorbike rental in Hanoi cost?

No year-long price is published. Ask the shop for one in writing, giving your dates and expected usage, and treat the [published monthly rates by model]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) as a reference point only.

### Can I leave before the twelve months are up?

There is no automatic refund entitlement for unused time, so ask what applies to your term before you sign. On a rolling arrangement, ask whether ending means simply not renewing, and how much notice the shop wants.

### Can I change bikes during the year?

Whether a swap is possible, and when, is a conversation with the shop, and the model you want needs its availability confirmed: contact us to confirm current availability. A renewal date is the natural moment to ask, and the terms for a different bike are worth settling in the same message.

### Can I ride the bike outside Hanoi?

Support is primarily for Hanoi and local use, so a trip beyond the city is something to agree with the shop first. Ask at the same time what happens if the bike needs attention while you are away.

## The one-line version

Negotiate the whole term up front, schedule the maintenance, manage mileage and licence validity, and run the buy comparison honestly — a year of rental done right is cheaper than a year of surprises.
