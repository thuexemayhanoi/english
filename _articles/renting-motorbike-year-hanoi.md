A year is the duration where rental stops being a convenience purchase and becomes a transport policy. Here is how year-scale rental works in Hanoi and what to weigh before committing.

## How the arrangement works

Longer-term arrangements beyond the published monthly rates are negotiable with the customer — contact us to discuss longer-term rental for your dates and expected usage. A year-scale agreement is built on the monthly product (monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi) but typically takes one of two shapes: a fixed-term arrangement agreed once for the whole year, or a rolling month-to-month renewal held open all year. The rolling version is the flexible one; the fixed-term version is the one to negotiate carefully — early ending carries no automatic refund entitlement (shortening-long-motorbike-rental-hanoi).

## What a year of riding changes

- Maintenance becomes a schedule, not an event: expect several oil/chain/inspection visits across the year — the pattern is in maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi
- Wear items become real: tyres will likely need attention during the year, and consumables may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation (consumables-monthly-rental-hanoi)
- Mileage management matters if you ride far: the practical owner guidance is periodic checks around every ~1,500 km of use — see high-mileage-monthly-rental-hanoi
- The licence must outlast the bike: check expiry dates up front — licence-expiry-during-long-rental-hanoi

## The resident's decision

At a year, run the honest rent-versus-buy comparison: long-term-renting-vs-buying-residents-hanoi. Year-scale rental still wins for most expats on fixed contracts — known cost, maintenance on the shop's side, clean exit — but the comparison should be made with numbers, not folklore. The six-month article (renting-motorbike-six-months-hanoi) covers the adjacent case.

## Protecting a year-long relationship

Document the handover state, keep the message thread, book maintenance visits instead of deferring them, and renew or return on time — a year of small good habits is what makes the final deposit return boring. The agreement terms that matter most at this scale are in motorbike-rental-agreement-long-term-hanoi.

## The one-line version

Negotiate the whole term up front, schedule the maintenance, manage mileage and licence validity, and run the buy comparison honestly — a year of rental done right is cheaper than a year of surprises.
