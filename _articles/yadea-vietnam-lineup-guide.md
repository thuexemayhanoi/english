## Reading the range

The entry cluster (13.9-16 million VND) is the student and errand tier — light, licence-free city machines. The middle (18-34 million) adds sportier styling and more capable motors; the Voltguard and Velax models form the performance end, with the Velax U as the flagship. New models arrive regularly — Yadea listed the Omee and Osta as its newest additions at retrieval time.

## What to verify before buying

Yadea publishes prices but full technical specification tables per model were not readable from the automated page view at retrieval — check rated motor power (the 4 kW licence boundary) and real-world range with the dealer before committing, and take a proper test ride. Our first-bike decision guide covers the class questions that matter more than the badge.

## Where Yadea fits

Against VinFast, Yadea sells owned-battery bikes at fixed prices rather than a swap-plus-rental ecosystem; against Dat Bike, it plays in the licence-free city classes rather than A1 performance. For many buyers that makes it the budget-rational middle path.
