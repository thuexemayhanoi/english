Scooter prices in Vietnam span a five-to-one range, and manufacturer list prices are the only numbers worth planning around. This table records only prices published by Honda Vietnam and Yamaha Vietnam on official product pages, retrieved 23 September 2026. Retail prices change with promotions - always confirm the current figure with a dealer.

Yamaha also lists the NMAX from 69,000,000 VND at the top of its scooter range. Honda Vision, Air Blade and Lead prices were not among the figures we retrieved on 2026-09-23, so they are omitted rather than estimated - check Honda Vietnam for current figures.

## What list prices do not include

Registration, plate, insurance (compulsory third-party insurance is mandatory - see our insurance guide), accessories and dealer fees come on top. Versions matter too: most models span several versions, and the cheapest version is rarely the one displayed.

## List price versus rental price

Buying at 30-40 million VND plus running costs only beats renting when you ride constantly for a long period. Nguyen Tu's owner-approved rental rates for comparison: Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius 150,000 VND/day; Honda Vision 200,000 VND/day, 800,000-1,000,000 VND/week, 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND/month; Honda Air Blade 200,000 VND/day, 800,000 VND/week, 1,400,000 VND/month; Honda Click and Yamaha Mio 150,000 VND/day, 600,000-700,000 VND/week, 1,000,000-1,200,000 VND/month. Our renting versus buying analysis works through the break-even math.

## Bottom line

Decide your class first (see the Honda and Yamaha lineup guides), then compare versions within it - and remember that for foreign visitors and new expats, a rental with the owner-verified daily rates above is usually the lower-risk start.
