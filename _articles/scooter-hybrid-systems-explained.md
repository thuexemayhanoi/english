Hybrid scooters have arrived in Vietnam's entry segment, and the name promises more than the hardware delivers - so here is exactly what the manufacturers publish, no more.

## What a scooter hybrid actually is

The systems sold on Vietnamese scooters are not electric vehicles and cannot ride on electricity alone. They are mild hybrids: a small electric motor - usually integrated with the generator - gives the petrol engine a brief boost during launch. Yamaha's official description of the Gear 125 Hybrid system: within a maximum of about three seconds from launch, the electric assist is activated and adds pulling power, which Yamaha highlights as useful uphill or riding two-up; when the bike needs help, the smart generator draws energy from the battery to support smooth acceleration.

## What the system does not do

- It cannot propel the scooter without the petrol engine.
- It does not meaningfully recharge under braking the way a full hybrid car does - the assist energy comes from the battery, replenished by the engine's generator.
- It does not turn a 125 into a 160: the Gear 125 Hybrid publishes 6.2 kW, less than the non-hybrid Janus's 7.0 kW. The assist fills in low-end pull, not top-end speed.

## Verified hybrid models

- Yamaha Gear 125 Hybrid - the system is the model's headline: 96 kg, 1.7 L/100 km published (the best figure in our verified economy table), 17.4L bay, SmartKey.
- Yamaha Grande - Yamaha's page describes the Blue Core Hybrid engine family for the current Grande; the page does not publish engine output figures, so we state none.

## Is the extra battery a reliability risk?

The honest answer is that the system adds components (battery, control unit, assist motor) that a simple scooter does not have, while the manufacturers publish 3-year/30,000 km warranties on these models (Yamaha's listed terms at our retrieval date). Practical ownership guidance: batteries hate being parked for months - our battery care guide covers storage - and assist systems should be diagnosed at brand service points rather than general shops, per our warranty and dealer service guide.

## Should a renter care?

Renters rarely feel the difference in normal use; buyers doing daily launches two-up or on slopes will.
