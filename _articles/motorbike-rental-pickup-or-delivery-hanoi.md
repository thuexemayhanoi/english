Both work for a [Hanoi motorbike rental]({{ '/topics/rental/' | relative_url }}). The right choice depends on how far you are from the shop and how much you care about test-riding the exact bike.

Hanoi motorbike rental delivery, to a hotel or an address, may be available depending on location and arrangement, so the real question is which route suits your first day. Pick up at the shop when you want the bike, the contract and your questions in one place; ask about delivery when distance, luggage or timing make the trip itself the hard part. The tables below sort the usual situations, and the sections after them cover what to ask, what to agree in writing and how the return works. For the whole sequence from first message to return, start with [how to rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/how-to-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Pickup or delivery by situation

Each row names the route that fits better and what decides it. Where two rows apply at once, give way to the constraint you cannot change, such as a landing time or a suitcase, before the preference you can change, such as seeing the bike first.

### Arrival, luggage and riding experience

| Your situation | Better fit | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival day | A next-day handover, by either route | After a flight you may be tired, and both routes sit inside opening hours, so a late landing waits for the morning. |
| Luggage | Delivery, or pickup once your bags are in your room | A scooter is not a way to carry a suitcase, and delivery saves a separate trip to the shop. |
| First-time rider | Pickup | The shop is in sight for your first minutes, and the handover is the moment to ask how the controls work. |

### Length of stay and where you sleep

| Your situation | Better fit | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Long stay | Delivery, if you are based far from the shop | You hand over once at each end, and you can ask how routine checks would work at that distance. |
| Hotel | Delivery, once the hotel agrees | A lane address is hard to find from a number alone, and the bike needs somewhere to sleep. |
| Apartment | Delivery, once parking is settled | A building adds a gate, a lot and its own staff, so ask what it allows before the day. |

Imagine a visitor who lands at midday with two suitcases and has never ridden a scooter in Vietnam. Luggage and a first ride pull in different directions, so a calm order is bags to the hotel, a rest, then a pickup the next morning with the shop in sight. Now imagine a resident moving into an apartment for a few months: the handover happens once, parking is the real question, and delivery to the building fits better than a trip across the city.

Two of these rows have their own guides. For the arrival day, [the Noi Bai arrival plan]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) lays out the first 24 hours after landing, and for a first rider, [First Time Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi: A Beginner Walkthrough]({{ '/articles/first-time-renting-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) goes through the first hour and first day.

## Shop pickup: the inspect-first option

Collecting the bike yourself at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Long Bien, means you stand next to the bike you are actually renting. You can inspect it properly - the [inspection checklist]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}) lists the walk-around - ask to test-ride it around the block, and ask for a swap on the spot if something feels wrong. Deposit, contract and handover photos all happen face to face. If you are new to riding in Vietnam, pickup is worth the trip: the first ten minutes on a Vietnamese rental are easier when the shop is still in sight.

### What to have and ask when you collect

- **Documents.** Ask which documents the shop wants to see. [Motorbike Rental Requirements in Hanoi: Documents, Licence, Age]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out what the shop and the law each ask for, and [Can Foreigners Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/can-foreigners-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) answers who may rent.
- **Payment.** The accepted forms are cash, bank transfer and QR payment, so do not plan the deposit around a card.
- **Insurance.** Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance; renters arrange their own, so sort that out before the day.
- **Getting there.** You need a way to reach the shop before you have a bike. Work out the trip in advance, leave the luggage behind, and confirm that your model is available for your dates before you travel.

## Delivery: the convenience option

Delivery to your hotel or address in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement - it is agreed case by case, within opening hours (09:00-21:00 daily). There is no published fixed delivery fee; agree the cost with the shop [when you book]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), not when the bike arrives. Delivery suits renters already settled somewhere far from the shop, or anyone on a tight schedule. Arriving at Noi Bai Airport? [Renting a Motorbike When You Arrive at Noi Bai Airport]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) explains how handover works after landing.

### What to ask before you accept delivery

Ask whether your address and dates can be served at all: "may be available" is the published position, so send the full address, a map pin and your dates. Agree the handover spot too, a level, well-lit place off the carriageway, and give a phone number you will answer when the time comes.

## How delivery changes the handover

The inspection still happens - just at your door. Check lights, brakes, tyres and fuel before signing, and photograph existing scratches with the deliverer present. The deposit and contract travel with the bike. If the bike is not the model you agreed, refuse it then; after signing, the handover photos are the record.

### What stays the same and what changes

The handover is one event on both routes, and the same [rental motorbike inspection checklist]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}) applies to both. Only a few parts of it move.

| Part of the handover | On both routes | With delivery |
|---|---|---|
| The checks | The same walk-around, in the same order. | You choose the spot, so pick level ground in good light. |
| The photos | Every panel and every existing mark, before you sign. | Send them to the booking chat at once, so both phones hold a timestamp. |
| The papers | Ask to see the registration certificate with the bike. | Check it before you sign, while the person handing over the bike is still with you. |
| The deposit | Typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions; ask for the figure for your bike. | Test your banking app, or count your cash, before the day. |
| Who is there | The person who signs and rides. | A receptionist is not a substitute for you at the inspection. |

The amount and the return of the deposit are explained in [Motorbike Rental Deposits in Hanoi: Typical Amounts and Return]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Hotel or apartment: ask the building first

A handover can go perfectly and still leave you with a bike and nowhere to put it. Before the day, ask the building four things:

- Where guests park a motorbike overnight, and whether a rental bike is welcome there.
- Whether the bike may come to the entrance or the lot for the handover, or the inspection has to happen on the street.
- Who is on duty, and who is responsible if the bike is damaged while it is parked in the building.
- Whether reception or security needs to know that someone is bringing a motorbike for you.

Hotel delivery in the centre has its own guide in [Motorbike Delivery to the Hanoi Old Quarter: How It Works]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-old-quarter/' | relative_url }}), and [Renting a Motorbike in Tay Ho: What to Know]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-tay-ho/' | relative_url }}) deals with lakeside apartments and villas. If you are looking for a motorbike rental near your Hanoi hotel, remember that the shop is in Long Bien: where your hotel is far from it, ask about delivery to the hotel instead of treating the shop as close. The detail for the shop's own district is in [Motorbike Delivery to Long Bien: How It Works]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-long-bien/' | relative_url }}).

## What to agree in writing

A message in the booking chat gives you a timestamped record on your own phone. [How to Book a Motorbike Rental in Hanoi (WhatsApp, Zalo, Walk-In)]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) covers the channels; before the day, the chat should show:

- The model and the rental period, with the exact day and hour of the return.
- How the bike reaches you and how it goes back: pickup or delivery at the start, shop return or collection at the end, each with a place and a time window.
- The daily rate for your model, published as 150,000 VND for the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Honda Click and Yamaha Mio and 200,000 VND for the Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade, and the deposit for your bike.
- Any cost for delivery or collection, written as an amount before the day.
- How you will pay, and a receipt for the deposit.
- The fuel level at handover and the level agreed for the return.

## Timing: a time given is a window

Treat any time you are given as a window, not an appointment: no delivery time is published, and none is guaranteed. Traffic and narrow lanes can move a handover by more than anyone allows for, so plan around the window instead of hoping it holds.

- Keep anything that cannot move, such as a flight, a train, a booked tour or a hotel checkout, well clear of the handover.
- Ask for a range, for example late morning to early afternoon, and message the shop that morning to confirm the window still holds.
- Choose daylight if you can. The inspection is easier to do properly, and the first ride is easier to read.
- Ask whether the rental period starts at the agreed time or when you have the keys, and have the answer written down. If the handover runs late, that answer decides what you are paying for.

## Returns work both ways too

Return at the shop for the quickest deposit settlement, or arrange collection from your address the same way delivery was arranged. Same rule: agree it in advance.

### Choosing the return

- **Back at the shop.** You control the timing, so plan the whole trip back, not just the last stretch, and arrive inside opening hours with room to spare. [How Returning a Rental Motorbike in Hanoi Works]({{ '/articles/returning-a-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the steps in order.
- **Collection from your address.** This may be available depending on location and arrangement, like delivery. Agree the place and the window, then ask which moment ends the rental, the agreed collection time or the moment the bike reaches the shop, and how and when the deposit is settled.

### The late-return rules

The published terms are 20,000 VND per hour late. More than six hours late, a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. Late is measured against the return you agreed, so write that time and place down, ask how part-hours are counted, and agree any change before the deadline rather than explaining it afterwards. [What Happens If You Return a Rental Motorbike Late in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) explains the terms and why a message ahead helps.

## Mistakes that cost renters time or money

- **Collecting in the evening.** Inspecting a bike and riding it for the first time are both harder in the dark, and a pickup near closing time leaves no room to raise a problem.
- **Signing because someone is waiting.** A hotel desk, a pile of luggage or the clock should not shorten the checks: your signature records the condition of the bike.
- **Deleting the evidence.** Keep the booking chat, the handover photos and the deposit receipt until the deposit is settled, since they are the record if anything is disputed.
- **Paying for something that was never written down.** Any cost for delivery or collection belongs in the chat as an amount before the day, and any money that changes hands at the door deserves a receipt.

## Common questions

### Can a rental motorbike be delivered to my hotel or apartment in Hanoi?

Delivery, and collection at the end, may be available depending on location and arrangement. Send the address, a map pin and your dates, ask the shop to confirm availability, and check with your building where the bike will be kept.

### Does delivery cost extra?

No fixed delivery fee is published, so do not assume the bike arrives at no charge. Ask for any cost as an amount in the booking chat before the day, and keep the answer.

### Can I get a bike if I land after the shop closes?

Delivery and pickup both stay inside opening hours, so a late landing means the handover waits until the next day. The [airport arrival guide]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) has the arrival plan.

### Can my hotel or a friend receive the bike for me?

There is no published rule, so ask the shop before the day instead of assuming. The person who signs and rides should be at the inspection, because the signature records the condition of the bike.

## The honest rule of thumb

Inside a few kilometres, pickup. Far away, short of time, or [renting long-term]({{ '/topics/monthly-rental/' | relative_url }}), delivery. Either way, the checklist and the photos are what protect your deposit - the location only changes where you do them.
