Both work for a [Hanoi motorbike rental]({{ '/topics/rental/' | relative_url }}). The right choice depends on how far you are from the shop and how closely you want to check the exact bike first.

Hanoi motorbike rental delivery, to a hotel or an address, may be available depending on location and arrangement, so the real question is which route suits your first day. Pick up at the shop when you want the bike, the contract and your questions in one place; ask about delivery when distance, luggage or timing make the trip itself the hard part. The tables below sort the usual situations, and the sections after them cover what to ask, what to agree in writing and how the return works. For the whole sequence from first message to return, start with [how to rent a motorbike in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/how-to-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Pickup or delivery by situation

Each row names the route that fits better and what decides it. Where two rows apply at once, give way to the constraint you cannot change, such as a landing time or a suitcase, before the preference you can change, such as seeing the bike first.

### Arrival, luggage and riding experience

| Your situation | Better fit | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival day | Next day, by either route, is the safe default; ask about same day only if you land early and feel rested | After a flight you may be tired, and a handover needs your full attention. |
| Luggage | Delivery, or pickup once your bags are in your room | A scooter is not a way to carry a suitcase, and delivery saves a separate trip to the shop. |
| First-time rider | Pickup | The handover is the moment to ask how the controls work, face to face with the shop. |

### Length of stay and where you sleep

| Your situation | Better fit | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Long stay | Delivery, if you are based far from the shop | You hand over once at each end, and you can ask how routine checks would work at that distance. |
| Hotel | Delivery, once the hotel agrees | A lane address is hard to find from a number alone, and the bike needs somewhere to sleep. |
| Apartment | Delivery, once parking is settled | A building adds a gate, a lot and its own staff, so ask what it allows before the day. |

Imagine a visitor who lands at midday with two suitcases and has never ridden a scooter in Vietnam. Luggage and a first ride pull in different directions, so a calm order is bags to the hotel, a rest, then a pickup the next morning. Now imagine a resident moving into an apartment for a few months: the handover happens once, parking is the real question, and delivery to the building fits better than a trip across the city.

## Shop pickup: the inspect-first option

Collecting the bike yourself at 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Long Bien, means you stand next to the bike you are actually renting. You can inspect it properly - the [inspection checklist]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}) lists the walk-around - ask whether a short test ride is possible, and what the shop can offer if the bike is not right. Deposit, contract and handover photos all happen face to face. If you are new to riding in Vietnam, pickup is worth the trip: your first minutes on a Vietnamese rental are easier when the shop is still close, and [First Time Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi: A Beginner Walkthrough]({{ '/articles/first-time-renting-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) goes through the first hour and first day.

### What to have and ask when you collect

- **Documents.** Ask which documents the shop wants to see. [Motorbike Rental Requirements in Hanoi: Documents, Licence, Age]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out what the shop and the law each ask for.
- **Payment.** The accepted forms are cash, bank transfer and QR payment, so do not plan the deposit around a card.
- **Insurance.** Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance; renters arrange their own, so sort that out before the day.
- **Getting there.** You need a way to reach the shop before you have a bike. Work out the trip in advance, leave the luggage behind, and confirm that your model is available for your dates before you travel.

## Delivery: the convenience option

Delivery to your hotel or address in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement - it is agreed case by case, within opening hours (09:00-21:00 daily). Delivery suits renters already settled somewhere far from the shop. Arriving at Noi Bai Airport? [Renting a Motorbike When You Arrive at Noi Bai Airport]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-noi-bai-arrival/' | relative_url }}) explains how handover works after landing.

### What to ask before you accept delivery

Ask whether your address and dates can be served at all: send the full address, a map pin and your dates. Agree the handover spot too, and give a phone number you will answer when the time comes.

No delivery time is published and none is guaranteed, so think of the handover as a stretch of the day rather than a fixed moment. Keep anything that cannot move, such as a flight or a hotel checkout, well clear of it, ask for a daylight window if you can, and message the shop that morning to confirm.

## How delivery changes the handover

The inspection still happens - just at your door. Check lights, brakes, tyres and fuel, and photograph existing scratches before you sign, with whoever hands over the bike present; ask how the deposit and contract are handled when the bike is delivered. A waiting hotel desk, a pile of luggage or the clock is no reason to shorten the checks. If the bike is not the model you agreed, refuse it then; after signing, the handover photos are the record.

### What stays the same and what changes

The handover is one event on both routes, and the same checklist applies to both. Only a few parts of it move.

| Part of the handover | On both routes | With delivery |
|---|---|---|
| The checks | The same walk-around, in the same order. | Ask for a level spot in good light, agreed in advance. |
| The photos | Every panel and every existing mark, before you sign. | Send them to the booking chat at once, so both phones hold a timestamp. |
| The papers | Ask to see the registration certificate with the bike. | Check it before you sign, while whoever hands over the bike is still with you. |
| The deposit | Typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions; ask for the figure for your bike. | Ask where and when the deposit is paid. |
| Who is there | The person who signs and rides. | A receptionist is not a substitute for you at the inspection. |

The amount and the return of the deposit are explained in [Motorbike Rental Deposits in Hanoi: Typical Amounts and Return]({{ '/articles/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Hotel or apartment: ask the building first

A handover can go perfectly and still leave you with a bike and nowhere to put it, so the building's answer belongs in the choice between pickup and delivery. Ask it one question before anything is booked: may a rental motorbike stay here overnight, and where? What you hear points in one of three directions.

| What the building says | What it points to |
|---|---|
| A clear yes, ideally in a message you can show the shop | Ask the shop whether delivery can reach the address for your dates, and send the building's reply along with the address. |
| Maybe, or nobody can say | Hold off on fixing a delivery day: until the bike has somewhere to stay, a handover only moves the problem to your door. |
| No | Neither route solves it. Decide where the bike will stay before you rent, then plan the handover around that place. |

If you are looking for a motorbike rental near your Hanoi hotel, remember that the shop is in Long Bien: where your hotel is far from it, ask about delivery to the hotel instead of treating the shop as close.

## What to agree in writing

A message in the booking chat gives you a timestamped record on your own phone. Before the day, the chat should show:

- The model, the dates, the exact return hour, and whether the rental starts at the agreed time or when you have the keys.
- The route at each end, pickup or delivery and shop return or collection, each with a place and a time window.
- The daily rate for your model, published as 150,000 VND for the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Honda Click and Yamaha Mio and 200,000 VND for the Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade.
- Any cost for delivery or collection: there is no published fixed delivery fee; agree the cost with the shop [when you book]({{ '/articles/how-to-book-a-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }}), not when the bike arrives.
- How you will pay, and that you will ask for a receipt for the deposit.
- The fuel level at handover and the level expected at return.

## Returns work both ways too

Return the bike at the shop, or arrange collection from your address the same way delivery was arranged. Same rule: agree it in advance, and ask how and when the deposit is settled on each route.

### Choosing the return

- **Back at the shop.** You control the timing, so plan the whole trip back, not just the last stretch, and arrive inside opening hours with room to spare. [How Returning a Rental Motorbike in Hanoi Works]({{ '/articles/returning-a-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) lists the steps in order.
- **Collection from your address.** Ask whether collection is possible, as with delivery. Agree the place and the window, then ask which moment ends the rental: the agreed collection time or the moment the bike reaches the shop.

### The late-return rules

The published late-return terms are 20,000 VND per hour, and more than six hours late a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle. Whichever route you choose, ask whether late is counted from the agreed return time and how part-hours are counted, and agree any change before the deadline rather than explaining it afterwards. [What Happens If You Return a Rental Motorbike Late in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/late-return-rental-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) explains the terms and why a message ahead helps.

## Common questions

### Can a rental motorbike be delivered to my hotel or apartment in Hanoi?

It may be, depending on location and arrangement, and the shop confirms it for your address and dates. Check first with your building that a rental bike has somewhere to stay.

### Can I get a bike if I land after the shop closes?

Delivery and pickup both stay inside opening hours, so a late landing means the handover waits until the next day. Rest first and arrange it for the morning.

### Can my hotel or a friend receive the bike for me?

There is no published rule, so ask the shop before the day instead of assuming. [Motorbike Delivery to Long Bien: How It Works]({{ '/articles/motorbike-delivery-long-bien/' | relative_url }}) explains why the person who signs and rides should be at the handover.

## The honest rule of thumb

If you are in or near Long Bien, pickup. Far away, short of time, or [renting long-term]({{ '/topics/monthly-rental/' | relative_url }}), delivery, with a window rather than a fixed time. Either way, the checklist and the photos are what protect your deposit - the location only changes where you do them.
