This is the sharpest sporty-scooter value question in Vietnam right now: Yamaha's premium NMAX 155 or Honda's aggressively priced Vario 160. Figures below are from the official Yamaha Vietnam and Honda Vietnam pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Power

The NMAX's Blue Core 155.1 cc VVA engine makes 11.3 kW at 8,000 rpm and 14.2 Nm at 6,500 rpm. The Vario 160's 156.9 cc eSP+ makes 11.3 kW at 8,500 rpm and 14.1 Nm at 6,500 rpm. On paper they are inseparable - this is not a decision about speed. What VVA does at different rpm is explained in our VVA guide.

## Braking hardware - the real difference

The NMAX has ABS on both wheels plus a traction control system. The Vario 160 has front-wheel ABS only. If you ride year-round through Hanoi's wet season, that rear ABS plus TCS is genuine added safety, not marketing - see our ABS guide and our TCS vs ABS explainer. The Vario's front ABS still beats drum-only rivals.

## Weight and practicality

The Vario is lighter at 118 kg versus the NMAX's 131 kg, with a lower seat (778 mm vs 770 mm - a near tie) and a 422 mm flat floor for boxes. The NMAX counters with 24 litres of underseat storage versus 17.9, plus a 7.1-litre tank versus 5.5.

## Price

Yamaha lists the NMAX at 69,000,000 VND; Honda lists the Vario 160 The thao version at 56,690,000 VND. That gap buys the second ABS channel and traction control - you are paying premium money for premium safety hardware.

## Verdict

Maximum safety hardware and brand prestige: NMAX 155. Best value 160-class sport scooter with a flat floor: Vario 160.
