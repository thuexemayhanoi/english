Ba Vi is the big forested massif you can see from western Hanoi on clear days, and riding into it is one of the most satisfying day trips from the city: about 60–70 kilometres out, then a long, cool climb through pine and cloud forest inside the national park. Count on roughly an hour and a half to reach the park area, plus the climb itself.

## Getting there

The straightforward route runs west out of the city and follows Highway 32 through Son Tay — direct, well-surfaced and busy through the built-up stretches. Near the end of the approach, turn at the Tan Linh crossroad and continue a few kilometres to the park entrance. An alternative is to ride out the Thang Long corridor first and cut across to the same road; either way, the final approach is signed and the park gates are the landmark.

Fuel is easy on Highway 32, but top up before turning into the park — there is nothing to buy once you are inside.

## The climb

From the entrance the road climbs continuously through the park, gaining altitude on sweeping bends until it reaches the upper area at around 1,100 metres, where there is a small rest area, a shop and the start of the walking trails to the old summit church ruins and the upper temples. The climb is several kilometres of steady gradient — sealed but damp, shaded and often misty. This is the best mountain-practice road near Hanoi: no traffic police worries, predictable bends, and views opening over the Day River plain.

At the top, the forest walks are short and well worth the dismount: the ruins of the old Catholic church are the classic photo, and the summit trails look north to peaks that in winter can be genuinely cold. Bring a layer — the temperature gap between Hanoi and the top can be startling, especially from November to February.

## Practical notes

- Start early: the climb plus walking plus the return ride makes a full day.
- Weekends are busy with Hanoi families; the upper road can have a short entry queue at midday.
- Descend in a low gear — the return leg is long and brake-fade on a loaded scooter is real.
- Rain in the park turns the bends slick within minutes; check the forecast and do not gamble with afternoon storms.
