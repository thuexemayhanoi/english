## How to choose within the class

Maximum safety hardware: SH160i (dual ABS plus HSTC) and NMAX 155 (dual ABS plus traction control). Best value: Lexi 155 and NVX 155, which share the same published 11.3 kW as the NMAX for 14-20 million less. Most practical: Vario 160, with its 422 mm flat floor. Lightest: Air Blade 160 at 113 kg. Badge and big wheels: SH160i with 16-inch wheels front and rear.

## Comfort and storage

Seat heights: SH160i 799 mm, NVX 790 mm, Vario 160 778 mm, NMAX 770 mm, Lexi 768 mm. Storage: SH160i 28 L, NVX 25 L, NMAX 24 L, Vario 160 17.9 L; the Lexi's page did not publish a storage figure. Tanks: NMAX 7.1 L, SH160i 7 L, NVX and Vario 160 5.5 L, Lexi 4.2 L.
