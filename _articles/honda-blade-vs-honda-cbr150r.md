On Honda Vietnam's list, nothing is further apart than the Blade and the CBR150R — the cheapest underbone and the most expensive fully-faired sport bike. Verified Honda VN specifications below (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines and transmissions

Blade: 109.1 cc air-cooled single, 6.18 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.65 Nm at 5,500 rpm, four-speed rotary. CBR150R: 149.2 cc liquid-cooled DOHC single, 12.6 kW at 9,000 rpm, 14.4 Nm at 7,000 rpm, manual clutch. Roughly double the power again — and a completely different rider skill requirement, since the CBR's clutch and gearbox demand practice the Blade never will.

## Weight, tank and economy

Blade: 98-99 kg, 3.7-litre tank, 1.85 L/100 km claimed. CBR150R: 139 kg, 12-litre tank — the largest in Honda's underbone and sport-150 set — and 2.91 L/100 km claimed. With its fairing, long 1,312 mm wheelbase and 151 mm clearance, the CBR is built for distance and cornering, not alleys; our kerb-weight explainer shows what 40 kg means at parking speed.

## Money and reality

Blade: 21,943,637 VND. CBR150R: 72,290,000 VND. The Blade is transport; the CBR is commitment — see how it fares against the Exciter 155 in our 150cc manual guide. Neither model is a price-published rental class at Nguyen Tu; contact us to confirm current availability.
