Monthly rental is a different product from a daily rental, not just a longer version of it. The terms, the relationship with the shop, and the things that can go wrong all change at month scale. Here is how the monthly product actually works in Hanoi.

## What a monthly rental is

You rent one bike for a fixed period — usually a month at a time — at a monthly rate, with a deposit held for the term. Nguyen Tu's rental periods run daily, weekly and monthly, with longer-term arrangements negotiable with the customer: contact us to discuss longer-term rental. The published monthly rates that exist are listed in motorbike-rental-monthly-price-hanoi; for models without a published monthly rate, contact us to confirm the current monthly price and availability.

## What changes compared with daily rental

- Price structure: a monthly rate replaces day-rate multiplication, where a monthly rate is published for the model
- The relationship: you will deal with the same shop across weeks, so handover quality and communication matter more
- Maintenance: a month of real riding makes the shop's included maintenance (oil, chain, tyre pressure, inspection) part of the deal, not a footnote — see maintenance-during-monthly-rental-hanoi
- Wear: consumables such as tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation — agree this up front
- The deposit sits held for weeks, so its terms deserve attention — see motorbike-rental-deposit-monthly-hanoi

## How to set one up

The process is the Cluster 1 rental process with a monthly agreement on top: confirm your licence situation (motorbike-rental-requirements-hanoi), agree the model and the monthly rate, arrange pickup or delivery (delivery-pickup-monthly-rental-hanoi), inspect the bike at handover (rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist), and put the agreed terms in writing — the long-term-specific terms are covered in motorbike-rental-agreement-long-term-hanoi.

## What a month of riding really involves

A monthly renter in Hanoi typically commutes daily, parks in the same lots, rides through rain and rush hours, and puts more kilometres on the bike in a month than a tourist manages in a week. The articles in this cluster cover each of those realities: the check routine (monthly-rental-inspection-schedule-hanoi), high mileage (high-mileage-monthly-rental-hanoi), security (keeping-rental-motorbike-secure-hanoi) and the exit (early-return-monthly-rental-hanoi, late-return-monthly-rental-hanoi).

## The short version

Monthly rental = a published or agreed monthly rate, a deposit held for the term, maintenance included on the shop's side, consumables agreed in advance, and a relationship with the shop measured in weeks. Set it up deliberately and it is the most practical transport arrangement in Hanoi.
