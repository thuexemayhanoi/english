Two new-generation 125 cc city scooters, two very different philosophies: Honda's Vario 125 is the modern equipment-first machine, while Yamaha's Latte trades hardware for lightness and cargo space. All figures come from the official Honda Vietnam and Yamaha Vietnam product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and cooling

The Vario 125 uses a liquid-cooled 125 cc eSP engine with PGM-FI, producing 8.48 kW at 8,500 rpm and 11.9 Nm at 5,000 rpm, plus an idling stop system. The Latte uses an air-cooled (forced-air) 124.9 cc Blue Core engine with two valves, producing 6.05 kW at 6,500 rpm and 9.77 Nm at 5,000 rpm. The Honda is clearly the stronger engine; the Yamaha counters with exceptional economy - see our air-cooled vs liquid-cooled explainer for what the cooling difference means in Hanoi heat.

## Fuel economy

Yamaha publishes 1.80 litres/100 km for the Latte - the best figure in the current 125 class. Honda publishes 2.11 litres/100 km for the Vario 125 and claims around 260 km per 5.5-litre tank under normal conditions. Both vary with traffic and riding style; the Latte is the cheaper bike to run.

## Weight and handling

Latte: 100 kg, 790 mm seat, small 12/10-inch wheels. Vario 125: 111 kg, 769 mm seat, 14-inch wheels. The Latte is the lighter, more flickable machine at walking pace; the Vario's 14-inch wheels and extra 11 kg give it more stability on broken arterial roads - a classic small-wheel versus big-wheel trade-off.

## Brakes

The Vario 125 uses a front disc with CBS; the Latte uses a front disc and rear drum without CBS or ABS. If combined braking matters in your daily riding, the Honda has it - see which scooters have ABS in Vietnam for the wider safety picture.

## Storage and features

Latte: 37 litres of underseat storage with a flat floor (A4 folders, 13-inch laptop), smart key, LCD, external fuel cap. Vario 125: 18 litres (a half helmet plus daily items), smart key, LCD, USB-C, full LED lighting, idling stop. The Latte is the cargo and style choice; the Vario is the equipment choice.

## Price

Honda lists the Vario 125 at 41,913,818 VND; Yamaha lists the Latte at 39,077,000 VND for the 2025 limited colour version. Nearly 3 million VND apart.

## Verdict

Choose the Latte for lightness, economy, storage and style at the lower price. Choose the Vario 125 for the stronger liquid-cooled engine, CBS, USB-C and bigger wheels. Neither is a Nguyen Tu price-published rental model - contact us to confirm current availability.
