Both of these Hondas are 125 cc semi-automatic underbones sold by the same manufacturer in the same market - yet one costs nearly three times the other. All figures below come from the official Honda Vietnam product pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## The engines are almost interchangeable

Super Cub C125: 123.9 cc, 6.87 kW at 7,500 rpm, 10.15 Nm at 5,000 rpm, 1.55 L/100 km, 3.7 L tank. Future 125 FI: 124.9 cc, 6.83 kW at 7,500 rpm, 10.2 Nm at 5,500 rpm, 1.47 L/100 km, 4.6 L tank. Power is effectively identical; the Future posts slightly better economy and carries 0.9 litres more fuel.

## Chassis

Super Cub: 109 kg, 780 mm seat, 136 mm clearance, 1,910 x 718 x 1,002 mm - the low, long classic silhouette. Future: 104 kg, 756 mm seat, 133 mm clearance, 1,931 x 711 x 1,083 mm. The Future is lighter and easier to muscle around; the Cub's proportions are the point of the bike.

## The price gap

Super Cub C125: 88,353,813 VND. Future 125 FI: from 30,622,909 VND (both list, retrieved 23 September 2026). Nearly 58 million VND separates them. What the money buys is design heritage, detailing and badge - not speed, not economy, not practicality, where the Future matches or beats it.

## Verdict

Buy the Future for transport; buy the Super Cub because you love what it is. There is no wrong answer - they are different products that happen to share an engine class.
