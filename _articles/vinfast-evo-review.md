The VinFast Evo is the entry point to Vietnam's battery-swap ecosystem — a licence-free city scooter built around removable power. This review uses only VinFast's official published data.

## Official specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 2,450 W in-hub |
| Top speed | up to 70 km/h |
| Batteries | two 1.5 kWh LFP packs under the seat |
| Range (two batteries, standard conditions) | up to 165 km |
| Licence | none required (16+) |
| Swap fee | 9,000 VND per battery per swap |
| Battery rental | 175,000 VND/month (one), 300,000 VND/month (two) |
| Launch price | 19,990,000 VND excluding battery (Jan 2026) |

## What stands out

The swap architecture is the point. Two removable 1.5 kWh LFP packs mean fuelling takes under a minute at a V-Green cabinet — 4,500 stations were installed by January 2026, with 45,000 planned across 34 provinces and cities by Q1 2026. Home charging and public chargers remain options.

The range figure is strong for the class: 165 km under st
andard conditions beats most licence-free rivals, though real city pace trims it — see our real-world range guide for how much.

## Trade-offs

At 2,450 W the Evo is a steady commuter, not a quick one; heavier riders on bridges will notice. The sub-seat battery bay trades some underseat storage for the packs. And the price excludes batteries: the true cost picture needs the rental-or-buy decision made alongside.

## Who it suits

Riders who cannot charge where they park, commuters who want licence-free simplicity with a nationwide fuel network, and anyone who wants electric without battery-ageing risk (rent the packs and let VinFast carry the fade).
