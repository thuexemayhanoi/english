The Viper and Feliz II are mechanical twins — same 3,000 W BLDC in-hub motor, same 70 km/h ceiling, same two-battery swap platform, same 156 km standard-conditions range. VinFast's official data separates them almost entirely by equipment.

## What the Viper adds (official)

- Smart Key system with vehicle tracking
- Remote vehicle-finding
- Remote on/off control for anti-theft support
- LED projector headlights
- Dual rear shock absorbers with auxiliary oil reservoirs
- An entirely new design language

## What the Feliz II offers instead

The inherited, familiar scooter body of Vietnam's best-known electric family — proven ergonomics, easy parts familiarity and a shape passengers already trust — at what is typically a lower rung of the range, with the identical swap economics (9,000 VND per battery per swap; rental 175,000/300,000 VND per month).

## How to decide

**Choose the Viper if** security and lighting matter to your life: tracking plus remote immobilisation materially changes theft risk for street parkers, and projector LED lighting is a real night-riding upgrade. The upgraded dual rear shocks also suit rougher routes and heavier loads.

**Choose the Feliz II if** you value the traditional format and would rather spend the difference on batteries you own or extra swap credit — the riding performance is identical by specification.

Both are licence-free and network-identical, so this comparison is a straight equipment-versus-preference decision rather than a performance one. The full reviews carry every verified figure.
