Short answer: motorbike speeding fines under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP step by how far over the limit you were: 400,000–600,000 VND for 5 to under 10 km/h over; 800,000–1,000,000 VND for 10 to 20 over; 6,000,000–8,000,000 VND with four licence points for more than 20 over; and 8,000,000–10,000,000 VND with a 10–12 month licence suspension when two or more riders speed together as a group.

## The bands, exactly

Article 7 of the decree prices the motorbike bands as follows. Under 5 km/h over the limit is not fined. Five to under 10 over: 400,000–600,000 VND (Article 7(2)(b)). Ten to 20 over: 800,000–1,000,000 VND (Article 7(4)(a)). Above 20 over: the 6–8 million VND band with a four-point deduction (Article 7(8)(a) with (13)(b)). Group speeding — riding as a pack of two or more above the limit — is singled out at 8–10 million VND and a 10–12 month suspension of the right to use the licence (Article 7(9)(b) with (12)(b)), because a group racing each other through traffic is treated as categorically more dangerous than one rider going fast alone.

The escalation continues beyond the schedule: speeding that causes a crash moves into the 10,000,000–14,000,000 VND accident band with ten points, and into criminal territory where people are harmed. And every band interacts with the limits themselves — urban Hanoi is typically 50 or 60 km/h for motorbikes depending on the road, with mopeds capped at 40 km/h, so the gap between legal and fined is smaller than most visitors assume.

## How the gap is measured

Speed enforcement is measured, not estimated: fixed cameras, average-speed stretches on the ring roads, and radar at checkpoints. The fine follows the plate by post when the camera does the measuring — the owner of the bike receives the notice first, and a rental bike routes it to the shop, which routes it to the deposit. Contesting a camera measurement is possible through the stated objection procedure, but the honest planning assumption is that the camera read correctly.

## Points, and what they mean

The licence starts each period with twelve points. The four points for a serious single speeding ticket, stacked with the ordinary deductions of urban riding, can empty a licence inside a month of careless commuting — and a licence at zero points is treated as no valid licence, with all that follows. The bands above 20 over carry no suspension for a lone rider, but the group-speeding suspension shows where the system's ceiling is: it removes the right to use the licence for up to a year.

## The habit that keeps the licence whole

The practical arithmetic: on a 50 km/h street, 20 over is 70 — impossible without deliberately trying. On the 70 km/h ring-road stretches, 20 over is 90, which a 125 cc scooter reaches at three-quarters throttle. The riders who lose licences are not the ones who misjudged by five; they are the ones who rode as if the limits were advisory. The bands are graduated; the licence is not.
