Moving from a semi-automatic to a manual-clutch bike (xe con tay) is the biggest step in Vietnamese riding. Only three current manual bikes have fully verified manufacturer data (retrieved 23 September 2026) - here is how they rank for a first-time clutch rider.

## The three verified manual bikes

| Spec | Winner R | Exciter 155 VVA | CBR150R |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | 11.5 kW @ 9,000 rpm | 13.2 kW @ 9,500 rpm | 12.6 kW @ 9,000 rpm |
| Torque | 13.5 Nm @ 7,000 rpm | 14.4 Nm @ 8,000 rpm | 14.4 Nm @ 7,000 rpm |
| Kerb weight | 124 kg | 123 kg wet | 139 kg |
| Seat height | 795 mm | not published | 788 mm |
| Clutch | manual | Assist and Slipper | manual |
| List price | 46,360,000 VND | 55,200,000 VND (limited ABS) | 72,290,000 VND |

## Why the Exciter is the easiest clutch bike

The Exciter's Assist and Slipper clutch is the beginner's unfair advantage: assist makes the lever lighter to pull, and the slipper function smooths clumsy downshifts - exactly the two mistakes new clutch riders make. It is also the lightest of the three (123 kg wet) and the cheapest to run at a claimed 2.07 L/100 km.

## Why the Winner R is the value choice

The cheapest entry into the manual class, with the friendliest power delivery of the three and a 795 mm seat. Its claimed 2.59 L/100 km (UDC method) reflects the sporty state of tune.

## Why the CBR150R is the hardest first bike

15 kg heavier, full fairing to repair, and gearing aimed at highway speeds. Superb second manual bike - demanding first one.
