Both scooters sell on practicality rather than pace, and both put storage first - but with different characters. The Lead is Honda's cargo tool; the Grande is Yamaha's style-first take on the same brief. Verified figures from Honda Vietnam and Yamaha Vietnam (retrieved 23 September 2026). Note: Yamaha does not publish engine output figures for the current Grande, so engine rows show only what each manufacturer states.

## The numbers side by side

| | Honda Lead 125 | Yamaha Grande |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 124.8 cc, liquid-cooled | Blue Core Hybrid (output not published) |
| Claimed economy | 2.1 L/100 km | (not published for current Grande) |
| Fuel tank | 6.0 L | (not published) |

| Equipment | analogue+digital meter | SmartKey, USB, Y-Connect app |
| Listed retail price | from 39,753,818 VND | from 46,244,000 VND |

## What the numbers mean

- **Storage:** the Lead's 37-litre bay is the largest of the two; the Grande's 27-litre bay adds lighting.
- **Weight:** the Grande is 14 kg lighter - easier to wrangle in tight parking.
- **Clearance:** the Lead's 140 mm sits higher over Hanoi's broken edges than the Grande's 127 mm.
- **Features:** the Grande leans tech-forward (smart key, app connectivity); the Lead offers an ABS version.
- **Tank:** the Lead's 6.0-litre capacity is the largest here - fewer fuel stops.
