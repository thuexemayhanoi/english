Big tank plus small thirst wins: the NMAX and Gear 125 lead on implied range; the Lexi and FreeGo carry the smallest tanks in their classes.

## What eats real-world range

Manufacturer economy figures come from controlled conditions - real range varies with load, speed, temperature, terrain and riding style, so treat the table as a ceiling, not a promise. Two-up riding, high-speed ring-road running and stop-start traffic all cut it. Our fuel economy comparison and economy-improvement guide cover the levers you control.

## Practical advice

Plan rural trips around fuel stops, not the tank's ceiling, and do not run a unfamiliar scooter to fumes - fuel quality and gauge accuracy vary (our fuel gauge article explains why).
