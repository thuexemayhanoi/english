The FreeGo is Yamaha's answer to buyers who find the Janus too small and the Grande too style-first: a mid-priced automatic scooter with sporty lines and genuinely useful storage. All figures below are from Yamaha Vietnam's official FreeGo product page (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engine and performance

A Blue Core 124.9 cc single-cylinder, two-valve, SOHC engine with forced-air cooling and CVT. Official figures: maximum power 7.0 kW at 8,000 rpm, maximum torque 9.0 Nm at 5,500 rpm, published fuel consumption 2.03 litres/100 km from a modest 3.9-litre tank - the tank to plan fuel stops around on longer rides. Engine oil capacity is listed at 0.84 litres at oil change.

## Size and handling

Wet weight 100 kg, seat height 780 mm, dimensions 1,915 x 685 x 1,110 mm, wheelbase 1,280 mm, ground clearance 135 mm. The underseat bay holds 25 litres, and the tyre sizes are 100/90-12 front and 110/90-12 rear, both tubeless - mid-size wheels that balance agility and stability (see our tyre sizes explainer).

## Equipment

Front disc and rear drum brakes, LED headlight, smart key on current versions, a phone charging port, and a 3-year or 30,000 km warranty. An ABS-equipped FreeGo S version is also offered - see which scooters have ABS in Vietnam.

## Who it suits

Riders who want one affordable scooter that does the daily commute and the occasional weekend run with a full-face helmet stowed.
