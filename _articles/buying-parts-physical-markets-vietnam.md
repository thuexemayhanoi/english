Vietnam’s physical parts markets — whole streets of stalls selling everything from gaskets to whole engines — are magnificent and dangerous: magnificent in price and availability, dangerous in a way the online-parts article ([Buying Motorbike Parts Online in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/buying-motorbike-parts-online-vietnam/' | relative_url }})) does not prepare you for, because in a market you cannot read a listing; you read a stall.

## How the markets work

Concentrated part streets cluster by brand and by bike family; one street of Honda parts, another of electricals, another of bodywork. Prices are negotiable, often well below shop rates, and many stalls are run by people who can identify a part from your phone photo in seconds — genuine expertise, and part of what you are buying.

## The fake problem, in person

The counterfeit reality in [Genuine Versus Fake Motorbike Parts in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/genuine-vs-fake-parts-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) applies with full force: genuine and counterfeit versions of the same part sit side by side at different prices, sometimes from the same pile. For safety-critical parts — brakes, hard structure, anything between you and the road — the market’s bargain is not worth the risk. For consumables, trim, and non-critical items, market prices are unbeatable. Bring the old part when you can; a physical match beats any description.

## Practical tactics

Compare two or three stalls before buying — prices vary within the same street by surprising margins. Ask about warranty even at a stall; many offer an exchange window, worthless for fakes but useful for wrong-fit parts. Know your model and year exactly, because "same thing" from a seller often means "similar". And when the price gap between the market and a proper shop is small, buy at the shop — the shop’s accountability is what you are paying for, and the pricing anatomy is in [Getting Repair Quotes in Vietnam: Parts, Labour and Fair Deals]({{ '/articles/repair-quotes-parts-pricing-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).

## Where parts are scarce

For rare models, markets are also the social network of parts availability — a stall owner who "knows a man" is often the only way to source what the dealers no longer stock; the availability landscape is mapped in [Spare Parts Availability in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/spare-parts-availability-vietnam/' | relative_url }}). Bring patience and a phone with photos.
