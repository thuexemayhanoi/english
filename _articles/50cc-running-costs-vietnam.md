A 50cc is one of the cheapest motorised vehicles on earth to run — provided it is maintained. Neglected, it becomes a small stream of workshop bills. Here is the honest cost map. (We deliberately publish no fixed price figures for work: workshop rates vary across Hanoi; ask your mechanic for quotes.)

## Fuel

The engine may be small, but the tank is smaller. Consumption depends on condition, load and the right hand — our fuel-habits article covers the riding style that keeps small engines efficient. Expect to visit petrol stations often in city use; small tanks turn good consumption into frequent stops. Our petrol-stations guide covers grades and payment.

## The consumables that actually cost

- **Tyres and tubes.** Small wheels wear faster and puncture more — city glass, kerbs and unswept shoulders see to that. Budget tyres plus tubes are the recurring cost of 50cc life; our tyres article covers lifespan factors.
- **Brake shoes.** Most 50s run drums all round; shoes wear and the front one does the real work. Our brakes article covers the checks.
- **Engine oil.** Small engines, small volumes — cheap per change, expensive to skip. The level check takes two minutes.
- **Chain and sprockets** (geared underbones): adjustment is free, replacement is not — the chain-care article is the cheapest money you will never spend.
- **Battery and bulbs.** Ageing electrics are the classic 50 failure; a battery that dies at night is a taxi fare home.

## Service costs

The work itself — oil, chain, brakes, cables, carburettor or injection cleaning — is routine for any neighbourhood mechanic, and our what-a-service-includes article separates the basic items from the periodic bigger ones. Follow the bike's own manual for intervals; no rule-of-thumb interval from the internet beats the manufacturer's book for your specific model.

## Insurance

Compulsory civil-liability insurance attaches to the vehicle and is renewed annually — a modest but non-negotiable line, as our insurance [article]({{ '/articles/do-you-need-insurance-to-ride-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) explains.

## The economics that surprise people

Two items dominate everything else: **purchase condition** (a bike bought cheap with a worn bore costs more than a dearer sound one) and **resale** (a maintained 50cc holds value in a way a neglected one never does — our resale article covers it). Running a 50cc cheaply is less about the bike and more about the habits around it.
