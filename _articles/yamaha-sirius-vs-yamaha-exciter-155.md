Yamaha Vietnam's two headline underbones mark the two steps of a riding life: the Sirius RC 110 that carries the country to work, and the Exciter 155 VVA that carries it fast on weekends. Both specifications are from Yamaha's own pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines and gearboxes

Sirius RC: 110.3 cc, SOHC, air-cooled, 5.9 kW at 8,000 rpm, 9.0 Nm at 5,000 rpm, four-speed rotary with a wet centrifugal clutch - no clutch lever. Exciter 155 VVA: 155 cc, SOHC four-valve, liquid-cooled with VVA variable valve timing and EFI, 13.2 kW at 9,500 rpm, 14.4 Nm at 8,000 rpm, six-speed with Assist and Slipper clutch. The jump is enormous: more than double the power and a completely different skill requirement.

## Weight and brakes

Sirius: 100 kg wet, front hydraulic disc (RC version) and rear drum, tubed tyres. Exciter: 123 kg wet, discs front and rear, ABS on limited versions, tubeless tyres. The Exciter's two discs are meaningful at Vietnamese roundabout speeds; our disc-versus-drum guide covers why.

## Economy

Sirius: 2.08 L/100 km, 4.2-litre tank. Exciter: 2.07 L/100 km, 5.4 litres - remarkably similar thirst despite double the performance. Manufacturer figures; real use varies.
