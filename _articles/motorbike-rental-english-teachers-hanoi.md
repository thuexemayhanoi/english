Teaching English in Hanoi usually means several workplaces: a language centre here, a public school there, private students across the city. A motorbike is the tool that makes that schedule possible, and renting one lets you start without buying. The licence, not the rental, is the part to plan, because a rental agreement does not make you a licensed rider. This page follows a teacher from the first weeks to a settled term.

## From arrival to a settled term

A teacher's needs change in four stages, and so do the questions to put to the shop.

| Stage | Licence position | Rental question |
|---|---|---|
| First weeks, no residence card | National licence plus a 1968-convention IDP, or the licence-free moped class | Which documents does the shop want to see, and can a short first period be extended? |
| Residence card in hand | Exchange for a Vietnamese licence becomes possible | Can the term be set to match your plans? |
| Settled term | A Vietnamese licence, or both foreign documents kept in date | What do notice, maintenance and consumables look like? |
| Contract ending | Nothing new to arrange | What is the return date, and what applies if you are late? |

## Licence reality

Teachers on tourist visas or visa-exemption entries face the same licensing rule as everyone: ride legally with a Vietnamese licence or a home licence plus a valid 1968 IDP. That sentence names two routes, not all of them: [Can Foreigners Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi?]({{ '/articles/can-foreigners-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out every kind of licence that Article 57 of Law 36/2024/QH15 recognises, including the treaty routes.

### The first weeks, before residence papers

Until you hold a residence card the exchange route below is not open, so the options are the ones any visitor has.

- **A national motorcycle licence plus a 1968 Vienna Convention IDP.** Carry both documents, in date and matching the class of bike. Article 57 limits the permit to no more than three years from its issue date and requires it to be consistent with the remaining validity of the national licence, so compare both dates with the end of your rental.

- **The licence-free moped class.** Vietnamese law treats a bike of 50 cc or less, designed for no more than 50 km/h, as a moped that needs no driving licence for a rider aged 16 or over, as [the 50cc licence guide]({{ '/articles/50cc-licence-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) explains from Articles 34(1)(g) and 59(1)(a) of Law 36/2024/QH15. Nguyen Tu's 50 cc availability and price are by contact only.

- **Neither of these.** An IDP without the national licence behind it, a 1949-convention IDP and a car-only licence do not cover a scooter; the table in [the foreigners guide]({{ '/articles/can-foreigners-rent-a-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) sets out how each case stands. Riding a rental scooter without a valid licence is riding unlicensed.

### Once residence papers exist

If you are settling into a work permit and residence, plan the licence exchange — [Vietnam Motorbike Licence for Long-Term Expats]({{ '/articles/expat-motorbike-licence-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) lays out the path.

Once you hold a residence card of three months or more, Article 24(1) of Circular 12/2025/TT-BCA lets a foreigner with a valid national licence be considered for exchange. An IDP is not exchanged, so keep both documents in date until the Vietnamese licence is issued.

## The commute pattern

A teaching week can spread across several locations, with rush-hour travel between them. What matters for the bike is not speed but reliability and comfort in stop-start traffic: automatic scooters suit that use for this reason. The bike-choice reasoning, including the automatic-versus-semi-automatic trade-off for teachers who ride further, is in [Best Bike for English Teachers Living in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/best-bike-for-english-teachers-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [Best Motorbike for Commuting in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/best-motorbike-for-commuting-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

### Plan the week on paper first

List every site with its days and start times, then note what lies between them: a river crossing, a ring road, a market street. Build slack into legs that cross the river or use a ring road.

Then ride each leg once at the hour you will really ride it, before your first teaching day if you can. A map shows distance; only the ride shows where you will park, which entrance you will use and how long the leg takes. For each site, settle three things:

- The time you must be inside the building, not at the gate.
- Where staff bikes go. Parking at a school or centre is that site's own arrangement, so ask the staff whether a fee or pass applies and where a bike can stay during a late class.
- What you do if the bike will not start: another way to reach the site, and a message ready for the centre.

### An illustrative week

Imagine a teacher with a morning class at a language centre, an afternoon class at a school across the river, and two evenings a week with private students in a third district. Three points decide whether that week works: the gap before the afternoon class, the river crossing, and the ride home after dark.

## Carrying teaching materials

A teaching bag is heavier and more fragile than a commuter's: it might hold a laptop, printed worksheets that spoil in rain, markers and a speaker. Storage is the one specification worth checking before you accept a bike, and the verified figures are few.

| Model | Underseat storage on the manufacturer's page | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Honda Air Blade 125 | 23.2 L, described as room for two half-helmets | The page also lists a USB-C charge port |
| Honda Wave RSX | A U-box described as holding one half-helmet | Small: your bag may need to travel on you |
| Honda Wave Alpha 110 | No storage figure published | Plan where the bag travels |
| Honda Vision, Honda Click, Yamaha Mio | No storage figure verified | Try your bag in the compartment |

The Air Blade 160 is a different model with no storage figure verified, and the shop's Wave and Air Blade could be any of these versions, so ask which version you would get. Litres also do not describe shape: a rigid laptop bag fits flat or it does not, so bring the bag you actually carry to handover and try it in the compartment with the seat closed.

Where the bag travels matters as much as whether it fits. Heavy items ride best low and close to the bike's centre, and a bag hung from the handlebars makes steering heavy and vague, as [Carrying Luggage and Loads on a Motorbike]({{ '/articles/carrying-luggage-motorbike-safely-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) explains. Paper and electronics are what rain ruins first, so keep them in the compartment or on your back inside a waterproof liner.

## Split shifts and evening classes

The shop is open 09:00–21:00 daily. If a split timetable or a late finish leaves you free only outside those hours, say so when you enquire and ask what can be arranged; do not assume a handover, return or repair outside them.

Evening classes can mean riding home after dark. From 18:00 to 06:00, and whenever fog, smoke, dust, rain or other bad weather cuts visibility, a motorbike's lights must be on, as [the motorbike light rules guide]({{ '/articles/motorbike-night-lights-vietnam/' | relative_url }}) explains. Check the headlight at handover and keep the visor clean.

Hanoi's rain tends to arrive in short heavy bursts between May and October, and [the rain-riding guide]({{ '/articles/riding-rental-motorbike-hanoi-rain/' | relative_url }}) covers the slippery first minutes of a shower, flooded sections and braking. If rain stops you on the way to a class, a late arrival costs less than a slip: message the centre and wait under cover.

- **Deposit.** Typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND depending on vehicle and conditions. Ask what it is held against and write the answer down.

- **Insurance.** Nguyen Tu does not provide motorbike insurance, so arrange your own. Check whether your health or travel policy excludes motorbike riding or requires a licence of the class you hold, and ask your employer whether any cover it arranges includes riding. - **Consumables and running costs.** Inner tubes and tyres may be the renter's responsibility depending on the situation. Fuel grows with the number of legs you ride and parking fees differ between lots, so take both figures from your own routine.

- **The end of the term.** Early return carries no automatic refund entitlement. A late return costs 20,000 VND per hour, and more than 6 hours late a full additional rental day may be charged, typically 150,000–200,000 VND depending on the motorcycle.

## Reliability matters more for teachers

Missing a class because of a mechanical problem is a professional problem, not just an inconvenience. Two protections: rent from a shop that covers routine maintenance (Nguyen Tu's maintenance may include an oil change, chain adjustment, tyre-pressure checks and a normal inspection — see [What Is Included in a Hanoi Motorbike Rental?]({{ '/articles/what-is-included-motorbike-rental-hanoi/' | relative_url }})), and ask at handover what happens on a breakdown and who to call ([What Happens If a Rental Motorbike Breaks Down in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-breakdown-hanoi/' | relative_url }})).

No roadside rescue or response time is published, so a plan for a dead bike on a teaching morning is yours to make.

Report anything that feels wrong early — [What to Do If Your Rental Motorbike Does Not Feel Normal]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-feels-wrong/' | relative_url }}) covers which symptoms justify a visit back.

### A handover check for a teaching bike

- Both lights, the indicators and the horn work; you will use them after dark.
- The compartment opens, closes and takes your bag.
- Brakes and tyres look sound, especially before the rainy season starts.
- You can reach the rain layer without unpacking your bag.
- The helmet fits. Whether a helmet comes with the rental is something to ask, not assume.

## Common questions

### Can the bike be delivered to my school or my apartment?

Delivery or pickup in Hanoi may be available depending on location and arrangement. Ask when you first enquire, and treat nothing as arranged until the shop confirms it.

### Can I ride outside Hanoi for a weekend job or a trip?

Support is primarily for Hanoi and local use. If a private student or a weekend job lies beyond the city, tell the shop where you plan to ride before you rent, and raise any trip before you leave rather than after.

### Is a 50 cc moped enough for a teacher's commute?

It is legal without a licence for a rider aged 16 or over, but a capped design speed is a practical limit on routes that include main roads. Availability and price of 50 cc bikes are by contact only.
