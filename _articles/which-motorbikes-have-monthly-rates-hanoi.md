If you are planning a month-long rental, the first practical question is which bikes you can price without a phone call. Here is the model-by-model answer for Nguyen Tu's approved price list.

## Published monthly rates

| Model | Monthly rate |
|---|---|
| Honda Vision | 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND |
| Honda Air Blade | 1,400,000 VND |
| Honda Click | 1,000,000–1,200,000 VND |
| Yamaha Mio | 1,000,000–1,200,000 VND |

These four are the automatic scooters on the list — and that is not a coincidence: the automatic commuter class is where monthly demand lives.

## Contact-only for monthly

- Honda Wave: 150,000 VND/day published; no published monthly rate
- Yamaha Sirius: 150,000 VND/day published; no published monthly rate
- E-Bike: 200,000 VND/day published; no published monthly rate (see e-bike-monthly-rental-hanoi)
- 50cc motorcycles: no published price at all; direct contact only

For all of these, contact us to confirm the current monthly price and availability. A monthly figure for these models is a conversation, never an inference.

## What the split means for choosing

The published-monthly group happens to cover the main commuter use cases well: the Vision for comfort, the Click and Mio as the light economical options, the Air Blade for a firmer, more powerful ride. The contact-only group adds the underbones (Wave, Sirius) — relevant if you prefer semi-automatic shifting — and electrics. The choice logic between those classes at month scale is in automatic-or-semi-automatic-monthly-hanoi and choosing-motorbike-monthly-rental-hanoi.

## Availability, always

Whatever the price list says, the current physical availability of any model varies: use "contact us to confirm current availability" as your booking step for every model, published rate or not.

## The one-line version

Four automatics carry published monthly rates; the underbones, the E-Bike and 50cc bikes are monthly-priced by direct contact — and availability of any specific model is always confirmed, never assumed.
