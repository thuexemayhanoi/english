The A1 licence is the workhorse motorcycle licence in Vietnam, and the class almost every renter needs to think about. Its scope changed on 1 January 2025.

## What a new A1 licence covers

- Petrol two-wheelers from 50cc up to (but not including) 125cc
- Electric two-wheelers from 4 kW up to (but not including) 11 kW
- Minimum age: 18

That covers nearly every bike a typical rental shop in Hanoi offers: 110–125cc scooters and underbones such as the Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade, Honda Wave, Honda Click and Yamaha Mio all fall inside 
A1.

## The pre-2025 transitional rule

A1 licences issued before 1 January 2025 keep their old scope: petrol bikes from 50cc up to (but not including) 175cc, or electric machines from 4 kW up to (but not including) 14 kW. No retest is required. This is why some Vietnamese riders with old A1 licences legally ride 150cc bikes.

## What A1 does not cover

- 125cc and above on a new licence (that needs class A, or A2 from 175cc)
- Electric machines of 11 kW and above on a new licence
- Three-wheel motorbikes (class B1)

## For foreign visitors

Most visitors do not hold a Vietnamese A1. Instead, recognition depends on your home licence plus an International Driving Permit that includes a motorcycle category — see our IDP guide. Riding a 110cc scooter with no valid licence of any kind is still an offence and can complicate insurance and liability after an accident.
