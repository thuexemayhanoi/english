The automatic scooter is the default rental in Hanoi, and three models dominate. Here is the honest comparison.

## Honda Vision - the all-rounder

The most popular scooter in Vietnam, and the reason is balance: light, low seat, tidy under-seat storage, and a shape that shrinks traffic gaps. Published rental rates: 200,000 VND per day, 800,000-1,000,000 VND per week, 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND per month. The Vision suits most riders, and it is the safe pick if you are choosing blind.

## Honda Click - the sportier sibling

Sharper bodywork, similar running gear to the Vision, slightly firmer feel. Published rates undercut the Vision: 150,000 VND per day, 600,000-700,000 VND per week, 1,000,000-1,200,000 VND per month. A Click is the value pick when the budget matters and the riding is city-only.

## Yamaha Mio - the light alternative

Yamaha's answer to the Click class: light, easy to park, easy to flick through traffic. Published rates match the Click: 150,000 VND per day, 600,000-700,000 VND per week, 1,000,000-1,200,000 VND per month.

## How to choose between them

- Seat height and fit matter more than spec sheets - sit on all three; the one that lets both feet nearly flat is the one.
- All three are around the 110-125 cc class: enough for Hanoi, enough for day trips, not tourers.
- All three take regular unleaded and get serviced by the rental shop as part of the deal.
- Rate differences are small but real on long rentals: a month on a Click/Mio versus a Vision differs by several hundred thousand dong.
