If you want a Honda semi-automatic with more presence than a Wave Alpha, the choice narrows to the Blade and the Future 125 FI. One is a styled 110, the other a practical 125. Honda Vietnam's published specifications (retrieved 23 September 2026) make the comparison straightforward.

## Engines

The Future's 124.9 cc engine produces 6.83 kW at 7,500 rpm and 10.2 Nm at 5,500 rpm - against the Blade's 6.18 kW and 8.65 Nm from 109.1 cc. In a city of stop-light drags and loaded ramps, the Future's torque advantage is the difference you feel every day, especially with a pillion.

## Economy

Future: 1.47 L/100 km, 4.6-litre tank. Blade: 1.85 L/100 km, 3.7-litre tank. The Future goes further on less - manufacturer figures, with real-world results varying with conditions.

## Dimensions and versions

Future 125: 1,931 mm long, 1,258 mm wheelbase, 756 mm seat, 104 kg. Blade: 1,920 mm, 1,217 mm wheelbase, 769 mm seat, 98-99 kg depending on version. The Blade Sport version adds alloy wheels and a front disc; Honda does not itemise Future versions on the retrieved page.

## Prices

Blade: 21,943,637 VND. Future: from 30,622,909 VND across three versions. Neither is a price-published rental class here - contact us to confirm current availability. Tall riders and two-up commuters should take the Future; style-focused riders who mostly stay inside the city can happily stay with the Blade.
