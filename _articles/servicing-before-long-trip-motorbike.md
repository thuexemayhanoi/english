The difference between a motorbike trip in Vietnam and a motorbike adventure is usually one part that was 500 kilometres past its service life when the trip started. A long trip concentrates use: a month of city mileage in a day or two, at sustained speeds a city bike never sees. Servicing before the trip is how the machine is ready for that concentration.

## The service-within-a-service rule

Do not start a long trip near a service boundary. If the oil, chain, brake pads or tyres will come due mid-trip, do the service early — a slightly early oil change costs almost nothing; a service due mid-trip in a small provincial town, done by an unknown shop, in the rain, costs peace of mind. The scheduling logic of what is due when is in [When to Service a Motorbike in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/motorbike-service-when-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).

## The pre-trip inspection list

**Wheels and brakes**

- Tyres: tread above the limit, no sidewall cracks, no embedded debris, and pressures set for the load you will carry (two-up with luggage changes pressures — per the manual's loaded figures). Tyre basics are in [Tyre Care and Pressure Basics for Motorbikes]({{ '/articles/tyre-care-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).
- Brakes: pad thickness with margin, lever feel firm, discs within limits — [Inspecting Brake Discs: Scoring, Warping and When to Worry]({{ '/articles/inspecting-brake-discs-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).
- Wheels: spokes checked, rim dents, bearing play — [Wheel Bearings: How to Check for Play Before They Fail]({{ '/articles/wheel-bearings-check-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).


**Drivetrain**

- Chain: within adjustment range, lubricated, sprocket teeth not hooked — the full routine is [Chain Maintenance on a Motorbike in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/chain-maintenance-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}); a chain at the end of its adjustment range at trip start will be loose by trip end.
- Cables: clutch and throttle free play correct, no fraying — [Throttle Cable Care and Free Play on a Motorbike]({{ '/articles/throttle-cable-free-play-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).

**Engine and fluids**

- Oil fresh and at the correct level; no weeping gaskets (a slow oil weep in the city becomes a fast one at sustained revs).
- Coolant, on liquid-cooled bikes, per [Coolant and Radiator Care for Liquid-Cooled Motorbikes]({{ '/articles/coolant-radiator-care-liquid-cooled-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).
- Fuel lines and filter healthy — [Fuel Filters and Fuel Lines: Small Parts That Matter]({{ '/articles/fuel-filters-fuel-lines-motorbike/' | relative_url }}).

**Electrics and lights**

- Battery healthy and charging (a battery on its last legs survives short city trips and fails exactly on a long one).
- All lights, horn, aim adjusted — [Aiming Your Motorbike Headlight]({{ '/articles/headlight-aim-adjustment-motorbike/' | relative_url }}) — because much of Vietnamese rural riding happens around dusk.

## The carry list

The under-seat spare kit covers the rest: tubes or a repair kit, levers, plugs, a pump, and for remote routes a belt or chain-link, zip ties and cable. The full list is in [What Spare Parts Belong in an Under-Seat Kit]({{ '/articles/spare-parts-kit-underseat-motorbike/' | relative_url }}). Add: the phone number of a mechanic who knows your bike, and roadside thinking that keeps minor failures minor — [Flat Tyre Repair on a Motorbike in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/flat-tyre-repair-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }}).

## The last honest step

A shakedown ride: one longer day ride in the week before departure, at highway speed, with luggage loaded. Faults that hide in city riding surface at sustained speed — vibration, fuel starvation, heat-stalling — and a week out is enough time to fix them calmly. The trip itself should hold no mechanical surprises; that is what the service was for.
