The difference between a motorbike trip in Vietnam and a motorbike adventure is usually one part that was 500 kilometres past its service life when the trip started. A long trip concentrates use: a month of city mileage in a day or two, at sustained speeds a city bike never sees. Servicing before the trip is how the machine is ready for that concentration.

## The service-within-a-service rule

Do not start a long trip near a service boundary. If the oil, chain, brake pads or tyres will come due mid-trip, do the service early — a slightly early oil change costs almost nothing; a service due mid-trip in a small provincial town, done by an unknown shop, in the rain, costs peace of mind. The scheduling logic of what is due when is in motorbike-service-when-vietnam.

## The pre-trip inspection list

**Wheels and brakes**

- Tyres: tread above the limit, no sidewall cracks, no embedded debris, and pressures set for the load you will carry (two-up with luggage changes pressures — per the manual's loaded figures). Tyre basics are in tyre-care-motorbike-vietnam.
- Brakes: pad thickness with margin, lever feel firm, discs within limits — inspecting-brake-discs-motorbike.
- Wheels: spokes checked, rim dents, bearing play — wheel-bearings-check-motorbike.

**Drivetrain**

- Chain: within adjustment range, lubricated, sprocket teeth not hooked — the full routine is chain-maintenance-motorbike-vietnam; a chain at the end of its adjustment range at trip start will be loose by trip end.
- Cables: clutch and throttle free play correct, no fraying — throttle-cable-free-play-motorbike.

**Engine and fluids**

- Oil fresh and at the correct level; no weeping gaskets (a slow oil weep in the city becomes a fast one at sustained revs).
- Coolant, on liquid-cooled bikes, per coolant-radiator-care-liquid-cooled-motorbike.
- Fuel lines and filter healthy — fuel-filters-fuel-lines-motorbike.

**Electrics and lights**

- Battery healthy and charging (a battery on its last legs survives short city trips and fails exactly on a long one).
- All lights, horn, aim adjusted — headlight-aim-adjustment-motorbike — because much of Vietnamese rural riding happens around dusk.

## The carry list

The under-seat spare kit covers the rest: tubes or a repair kit, levers, plugs, a pump, and for remote routes a belt or chain-link, zip ties and cable. The full list is in spare-parts-kit-underseat-motorbike. Add: the phone number of a mechanic who knows your bike, and roadside thinking that keeps minor failures minor — flat-tyre-repair-motorbike-vietnam.

## The last honest step

A shakedown ride: one longer day ride in the week before departure, at highway speed, with luggage loaded. Faults that hide in city riding surface at sustained speed — vibration, fuel starvation, heat-stalling — and a week out is enough time to fix them calmly. The trip itself should hold no mechanical surprises; that is what the service was for.
