A battery can look new and hold 60% of what it held when it was. On a used electric motorbike, the pack is most of the value, so the health check is the inspection. Here is the routine, in the order that catches the most expensive problems first.

## 1. Ask the numbers

How old is the pack, how was it charged, has it ever been replaced, and does a warranty transfer? The Dat Bike Weaver 200 publishes a 150,000 km / 15-year battery-life claim with a 3-year battery warranty; VinFast structures pack economics around rental or swap rather than a single life figure. Whatever the brand, a pack that has lived on fast chargers in summer heat ages differently from one that lived on evening charges.

## 2. Observe the range honestly

The single most telling test costs nothing: ride a known loop. A display that falls through the familiar part of a route faster than the machine's claimed range says it should is telling you the pack's real capacity. Compare against the manufacturer's standard-condition claim (the Evo's up-to-165 km on two packs, the Weaver 200's 200 km at 35 km/h), not against the seller's memory of "basically new".

## 3. Watch the charge

Charge from the display's low state and time it against the manufacturer's figures where published (the Weaver 200: 1 hour for the first 100 km of range). A pack that charges suspiciously fast to "full" is often a pack that no longer accepts the capacity it was born with. Also feel the pack after charging: warm is normal, hot is a question.

## 4. Inspect the physical pack

For removable packs (the VinFast class): check the casing for cracks, bulging or impact marks, and the connector faces for scorching or green corrosion. Connector damage is a bargain-killer — a beaten connector eventually arcs. For integrated packs: look at the mounting area and the charge port for the same signs.

## 5. Check the errors

Turn the machine on before riding and look for fault lights or display codes; ask for the app connection where the bike has one and read what it reports. A seller who "lost the charger" for a machine you cannot test-charge is a seller to walk away from.

## What you are deciding

A healthy pack makes a used electric a bargain; a tired one makes it a rolling battery-replacement invoice (our replacement article covers what that costs). Take the time — the ten minutes of this check is the cheapest insurance in the whole purchase.
