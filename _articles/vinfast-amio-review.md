The Amio is VinFast's simplest electric — a compact, pedal-assisted scooter aimed squarely at students, and the most licence-free machine in the line-up. All figures are VinFast's official published data.

## Official specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 800 W in-hub |
| Top speed | 30 km/h |
| Battery | 1.024 kWh LFP |
| Range (standard conditions) | up to 65 km per charge |
| Format | pedal-assisted, compact and lightweight |
| Licence | none required |

## What stands out

At 30 km/h the Amio sits below every threshold that matters: no licence, no power-class questions, no arterial temptation. The 1.024 kWh LFP pack is small — which is the feature: light weight, easy charging, and chemistry chosen for safety and durability. VinFast positions it explicitly as designed for students who need an easy, user-friendly machine for urban environments.

The 65 km standard-conditions range covers a school week on one or two charges for most users, and pedal assistance flattens the anxiety of a low gauge — you can always pedal a light bike home.

## Trade-offs

This is a short-hop machine by design. District lanes, school runs and market trips: perfect. Ring roads and bridges at flow speed: not its habitat. The pedal format also means a different posture and less weather protection than scooter-style electrics.

## Who it suits

First-time riders, students below or without licence options, and parents choosing the gentlest entry into powered two-wheelers.
