Hanoi's rainy season runs roughly May to October, with the heaviest, most predictable bursts from June to August. Renting through it is completely normal — the whole city rides through it daily — but it changes how you plan.

## What the season actually looks like

Rain arrives mostly in afternoon and early-evening storms: sudden, heavy, short. Mornings are usually dry, which is why the local rhythm is to do the riding early and accept being somewhere sheltered by late afternoon. Multi-day drizzle happens but is the exception, not the rule.

## Gear decisions at rental time

- A rain suit is the single most valuable purchase of a rainy-season stay; costs little, sold everywhere
- Keep it under the seat — what to carry and where is covered in [What to Carry While Riding a Motorbike in Vietnam]({{ '/articles/what-to-carry-riding-motorbike-vietnam/' | relative_url }})
- A clear-visored helmet beats sunglasses; see [Do Hanoi Rentals Include Helmets?]({{ '/articles/do-hanoi-rentals-include-helmets/' | relative_url }}) for what rentals provide
- Covered shoes dry faster than they look; a spare pair at home saves the day

## Bike choice in the season

Automatics with flat floors and under-seat storage protect gear best; drum brakes behave worse wet, so factor extra stopping distance rather than different brakes. The choice trade-offs are in [Scooter vs Semi-Automatic Motorcycle for Hanoi Riding]({{ '/articles/scooter-vs-semi-automatic-hanoi/' | relative_url }}) and [Best Motorbike for Commuting in Hanoi]({{ '/articles/best-motorbike-for-commuting-hanoi/' | relative_url }}).

## Rental planning differences

- Fuel caps and seat covers matter: check they close properly at handover ([Rental Motorbike Inspection Checklist: What to Check Before You Ride]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-inspection-checklist/' | relative_url }}))
- Electrical gremlins appear in the season; anything that feels wrong is worth reporting immediately ([What to Do If Your Rental Motorbike Does Not Feel Normal]({{ '/articles/rental-motorbike-feels-wrong/' | relative_url }}))
- Standing water on parked bikes is normal street life, but overnight parking under cover is worth choosing — see [Where to Park a Motorbike in Hanoi: Rules and Etiquette]({{ '/articles/where-to-park-motorbike-hanoi/' | relative_url }})

## The honest trade-off

The rainy season is also when Hanoi is greenest and its day trips are at their best — the Perfume Pagoda road and Ba Vi in particular. Renting in this season is not a worse experience, just a scheduled one: mornings out, afternoons flexible, rain gear always under the seat. The seasonal counterpart, riding through the northern winter, is covered in [Renting a Motorbike in Hanoi in Winter]({{ '/articles/renting-motorbike-hanoi-winter/' | relative_url }}).
