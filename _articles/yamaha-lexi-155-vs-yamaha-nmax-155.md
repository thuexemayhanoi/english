The Lexi and the NMAX share an engine and a price bracket but not a personality: the Lexi is the comfort commuter with 14-inch wheels, the NMAX is the MAX-series flagship with dual ABS. Both publish identical headline figures - 11.3 kW, 14.2 Nm from the 155.1 cc Blue Core VVA - so this comparison is about everything else. All figures from Yamaha Vietnam's official pages (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Brakes - the decisive gap

NMAX: ABS on both wheels plus traction control. Lexi: front ABS with a rear drum. It is the single biggest difference between the two, and the reason the NMAX costs what it costs. Our TCS vs ABS explainer covers what you give up.

## Wheels and stability

Lexi: 90/90-14 front and 100/90-14 rear - the biggest wheels of Yamaha's 155 trio, giving it a calm, planted ride on imperfect roads. NMAX: 110/70-13 and 130/70-13 - sportier, more agile. For commuters crossing bridges and ring roads daily, the Lexi's geometry (1,353 mm wheelbase, the longest here) is genuinely reassuring; our wheelbase [explainer]({{ '/articles/scooter-wheelbase-stability-explained/' | relative_url }}) has the numbers.

## Weight and comfort

Lexi: 118 kg, 768 mm seat - the lowest of the 155 class. NMAX: 131 kg, 770 mm. The Lexi is 13 kg lighter with an easier seat, which matters at every stoplight.

## Range and economy

Lexi: 2.19 L/100 km but only a 4.2-litre tank - the smallest in the class, so plan fuel stops on longer rides. NMAX: 2.26 L/100 km with a 7.1-litre tank and 24 litres of storage. Around town the Lexi's economy wins; touring, the NMAX's range wins.

## Price

Yamaha listed the Lexi 155 VVA-ABS from 48,500,000 VND (premium version 48,900,000 VND) against the NMAX from 69,000,000 VND. The NMAX's premium buys the second ABS channel, traction control and the flagship body.

## Verdict

Lexi for daily commuting comfort at a mid-premium price; NMAX for the full safety-hardware package. Neither is a price-published rental model - contact us to confirm current availability - and whichever you ride, start with our rental inspection checklist.
