Both Hondas trade on looks — the Blade on angular street styling at a budget price, the Super Cub C125 on decades of classic design at a premium one. Verified Honda VN specifications below (retrieved 23 September 2026).

## Engines

Blade: 109.1 cc air-cooled single, Euro 3, 6.18 kW at 7,500 rpm, 8.65 Nm at 5,500 rpm, four-speed rotary. Super Cub C125: 123.9 cc air-cooled single, 6.87 kW at 7,500 rpm, 10.15 Nm at 5,000 rpm, four-speed manual-shift. The Cub is bigger, torquier lower down and uses a nostalgic foot-shift with no clutch lever; both are simple to ride, and our rotary-versus-manual explainer covers the shifting difference.

## Weight, seats and economy

Blade: 98 kg (99 kg for the disc/alloy Sport version), 769 mm seat, 3.7-litre tank, 1.85 L/100 km claimed. Super Cub: 109 kg, 780 mm seat, 3.7 litres, 1.55 L/100 km claimed. The Cub is the more efficient machine of the two; the Blade is the lighter, cheaper city tool.

## The price question

Blade: 21,943,637 VND. Super Cub C125: 88,353,813 VND — four times the price for 15 cc and heritage styling. That is a lifestyle purchase, and our premium-underbones guide discusses exactly when it makes sense. Nobody buys a Cub to save money; nobody buys a Blade to make a statement.
