<p>Riding in Vietnamese traffic is a skill, and most of it is predictable once you understand the flow: ride defensively, keep your speed down, assume nobody has seen you, and give buses and trucks their blind spots. These guides cover lane discipline, intersections, rush hour in Hanoi, crossing the bridges, night riding, heavy rain and heat, emergency braking, potholes, mountain passes on trips out of the city, and the gear that keeps a slow fall from becoming an injury. Practical riding advice is kept separate from the legal rules, which live in <a href="{{ '/topics/law-licences/' | relative_url }}">laws and licences</a>.</p>
