<p>Electric two-wheelers are spreading fast in Hanoi, from pedal-assist e-bikes and speed-limited electric mopeds to full electric motorcycles that need a driving licence. The legal class depends on motor power and top speed, and so does the licence you need. These guides explain those classes, realistic range and charging times, battery lifespan and care, VinFast's battery rental model, and how electric compares with petrol for city riding. Nguyen Tu publishes a day rate for its E-Bike; weekly and monthly electric rates are quoted on request.</p>
