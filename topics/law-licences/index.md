<p>To ride a motorbike above 50cc legally in Vietnam you need a licence that is valid here: a Vietnamese licence, or a home-country licence together with a 1968 Vienna Convention international driving permit, or a licence exchanged for a Vietnamese one. Bikes of 50cc or less need no licence. These guides explain the licence classes, which IDPs are recognised, helmet and lane rules, speed limits, the documents to carry, compulsory insurance and the current fines. Each legal article states the rule and the practical reality separately, cites primary sources such as the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety and Decree 168/2024, and records when it was last reviewed.</p>
