Motorbike rental in Hanoi at a glance

Hanoi Motorbike Rental Nguyen Tu rents scooters and motorbikes by the day, week or month from 112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De, Long Bien, open 09:00–21:00 daily. Published day rates are 150,000 VND for the Honda Wave, Honda Click and Yamaha Mio, and 200,000 VND for the Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade; for any other model, contact us. Contact us to confirm current availability.

Deposit
Typically 2,000,000–5,000,000 VND, depending on the vehicle and conditions.
Booking
WhatsApp, Zalo or phone on +84 942 467 674, or walk in. There is no booking app and no online payment.
Payment
Cash, bank transfer or QR payment.
Delivery
Delivery to your hotel or address may be available depending on location and arrangement.
Insurance
Not provided by the shop: arrange your own cover before you ride.
Licence
A motorbike over 50 cc needs a licence that is valid in Vietnam. A moped (50 cc or less, or an electric motor of 4 kW or less, designed for no more than 50 km/h) needs no licence, from age 16; can foreigners rent a motorbike in Hanoi explains what counts.
